   - mypy.ini (6 lines) duplicates pyproject.toml [tool.mypy]
   - pytest_improvements.ini (37 lines) duplicates pyproject.toml [tool.pytest.ini_options]

   Status: Configuration fragmentation
   Files: mypy.ini, pytest_improvements.ini
