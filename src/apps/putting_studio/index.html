1. Coordinate Frames & 3D Basis Vectors

The putter swings about pivot hub O = (0,0,0) on an inclined stroke plane tilted at lie angle ψ = 70° from horizontal (β = 20° from vertical):

  • &hat;e_s(θ, φ): Radial unit vector along the putter shaft from pivot to clubhead.
  • &hat;e_θ(θ): In-plane tangential unit vector (positive toward target/downswing).
  • &hat;e_⊥(θ, φ) = &hat;e_s × &hat;e_θ: Off-plane normal vector perpendicular to the 70° stroke plane.

2. 4-Point Contact Forces & 2 Midpoint Torque Equations

Each hand applies an opposing force couple along shaft coordinate s, eliminating lateral hub shear (ΣF_L,θ = 0, ΣF_R,θ = 0) while generating counterbalanced midpoint torques:

  • Left Pinky (P_L,pky @ s = 0.07 m): F_L,pky = +F_L,∥(t) &hat;e_θ - F_L,⊥(t) &hat;e_⊥ (pushes forward in-plane, pulls down-plane).
  • Left Forefinger (P_L,idx @ s = 0.17 m): F_L,idx = -F_L,∥(t) &hat;e_θ + F_L,⊥(t) &hat;e_⊥ (pushes back in-plane, pushes up-plane).
  • Left Midpoint Torque (M_L @ s = 0.12 m, d_L = 0.10 m): τ_L = -d_L F_L,∥ &hat;e_⊥ - d_L F_L,⊥ &hat;e_θ (Negative backswing torque τ_L,∥ ≤ -0.85 N·m + off-plane anti-droop torque).
  • Right Pinky (P_R,pky @ s = 0.15 m): F_R,pky = -F_R,∥(t) &hat;e_θ - F_R,⊥(t) &hat;e_⊥ (pulls back in-plane, pulls down-plane).
  • Base of Right Forefinger (P_R,idx @ s = 0.25 m): F_R,idx = +F_R,∥(t) &hat;e_θ + F_R,⊥(t) &hat;e_⊥ (pushes forward in-plane, pushes up-plane).
  • Right Midpoint Torque (M_R @ s = 0.20 m, d_R = 0.10 m): τ_R = +d_R F_R,∥ &hat;e_⊥ - d_R F_R,⊥ &hat;e_θ (Positive downswing torque τ_R,∥ ≥ +0.85 N·m + off-plane anti-droop torque).

3. 4-Channel Variability Decomposition & Putter Balance Type Interaction

Total impact face-angle variance decomposes into four biomechanical channels: σ_total² = σ_hub-shear² + σ_backlash² + σ_off-plane² + σ_shaft-twist²(e_face, e_back). A putter head with CG offsets (e_face, e_back) perpendicular to the shaft axis generates parasitic shaft-axis torque τ_head,s(t) = -M_h L θ̈(t) e_face + M_h g sin(β)(e_back cos(θ) - e_face sin(θ)):

  • Toe-Hang Blade (45°, e_face=15mm, e_back=12mm, Peak |τ_s|=77 mN·m): Strong inertial opening torque during downswing acceleration. Switching from Unbalanced to 4-Pt Counterbalanced cuts Face SD from 1.303° to 0.365° (15-ft make rate jumps from 27.0% to 92.1%).
  • Face-Balanced Mallet (0°, e_face=0mm, e_back=26mm, Peak |τ_s|=31 mN·m): Zero inertial toe-lag torque, but deep rearward CG creates gravitational closing torque on the 70° lie plane if the shaft droops. 4-Pt Counterbalanced off-plane lock cuts Face SD from 0.957° to 0.312° (15-ft make rate 96.6% vs. 37.2%).
  • Zero-Torque / Lie-Angle Balanced (e_face=0mm, e_back=0mm, Peak |τ_s|=0 mN·m): Shaft axis passes directly through 3D head CG, eliminating both inertial and gravitational shaft twist (Face SD 0.305° in 4-Pt Counterbalanced vs. 0.804° in Unbalanced).
  • Heavy Counterbalanced Mallet (+75g Butt Weight, MOI=5,800 g·cm²): Raises system rotational inertia to filter high-frequency signal-dependent motor noise, achieving the lowest Face SD of 0.268° (98.2% 15-ft make rate).

4. AffineDrift & UpstreamDrift Integration Architecture (TDD, DbC, LoD, DRY)

The Python engine (putting_dynamics_v3.py) and 16-test verification suite (test_putting_dynamics_v3.py) are structured for zero-friction incorporation into both D-Sorganization/UpstreamDrift and AffineDrift:

  • Control-Affine Tangent Bundle Decomposition (AffineDriftDecomposition): Formulates the state derivative on x = [θ, φ, θ̇, φ̇]T as ẋ = fdrift(x) + Gcontrol(x) u, where fdrift(x) encapsulates gravitational pendulum torque, off-plane gravitational lie-droop τg,⊥(θ) = −M g Lcom sinβ cosθ, and centrifugal Euler whip, and Gcontrol(x) maps the 4 dual-axis midpoint torque inputs u = [τL,∥, τR,∥, τL,⊥, τR,⊥]T.
  • Design by Contract (DbC): Enforced via runtime require() preconditions, ensure() postconditions, and check_invariant() invariants.
  • Law of Demeter (LoD) & DRY: GreenSurface exposes direct facade properties (green.effective_friction, green.stimp_rating, green.evaluate_cup_interaction()) and shared mathematical kernels (minimum_jerk_polynomial(), smooth_hyperbolic_split(), compute_exact_impact_time()).

5. Off-Plane Lie-Angle Constraint & Extension Roadmap for Next Agent

To keep off-plane deviation φ(t) = 0 on the 70° plane, the off-plane force couples satisfy d_L F_L,⊥ + d_R F_R,⊥ = M g L_com sin(β) cos(θ) - I_Cross θ̇² cos(θ). Future agents can extend this suite by:

  • Adding 6-DOF Shaft Axial Twist (γ) via palmar friction torque couples around &hat;e_s to model off-center heel/toe impact gear-effect twist.
  • Connecting real IMU / force-sensing resistor (FSR) grip sensor streams into simulate_3d_counterbalanced_system() in the Colab Notebook.

6. Apples-to-Apples Nominal Equivalence & Grip Pressure Optimization (v3.3)

To avoid comparing a poorly executed swing pattern to a good one, v3.3 decomposes the 4-channel hand actuation into orthogonal subspaces u(t) = Acouple+ τtask(t) + Nnull τpreload:

  • Unperturbed Baseline Equivalence (test_17): Both the Moment-of-Force player (τpreload = 0) and the Counter-Torqued Stabilized player (τpreload > 0) supply the exact same inverse-dynamics task moment τtask(t). With zero perturbation and zero motor noise, both execute the exact same trajectory and ball roll (Δθ(t) = 0, Δφ(t) = 0 to 10−12).
  • Perturbation Impulse & Eccentric Recoil Rejection (test_18, test_19): Under a 0.25 N·m mid-downswing disturbance (t = 0.72 s) or a +10 mm toe mishit, locking the 4 grip contacts at τpreload = 0.60 N·m raises axial stiffness Kface from 1.80 to 10.50 N·m/rad and effective MOI Ieff from 11,521 to 25,194 g·cm2, cutting impact face twist by 83.2% (+0.235° vs. +1.401°).
  • Convex U-Shaped Grip Pressure Tradeoff (test_20): Sweeping τpreload ∈ [0, 2.0] N·m reveals an optimal sweet spot at τpreload* ∈ [0.50, 0.75] N·m (6.25–9.4 N/finger, 90.3% make rate, σφ,tot = 0.357°) before supralinear Harris-Wolpert Signal-Dependent Motor Noise (∝ τpreload1.42) degrades high-tension ("strangle-grip") putting accuracy.
