02 / Capture to Analysis

Choose the Path Your Evidence Supports

Record or import, inspect observations, then follow the single-view or calibrated multi-view path. Each stage has a defined input and output.

Download Workflow Diagram ↗

One camera: 2-D events, tempo and normalized motion. Multiple cameras: calibrate lenses, check synchronization and geometric observability, then reconstruct. Fit completion and low residuals do not establish physical validity.

gap

BunkerShot3D designer workbench (W2 sole parameters, W3 sand condition, F0 dynamic-RFT shot, W7 metrics, playability window, bounce utilisation, animated sole load field, 3-D shot animation through the ADR-0027 viewport, linked scalar traces with a validity band, F1 sand-field cross-sections, A/B comparison, validity verdict)

Moved out of engines.load_and_simulate by #8618: the tile is a club-design workbench, not an engine load/simulate surface, and the two launcher tiles (bunkershot3d -> a notebook script, bunker_shot -> the GUI) were reconciled into this one entry point. The computation lives in src/tools/bunker_shot_gui/model.py, which imports no GUI toolkit and is the layer a React port would consume; no API route or web page exists yet. The visualization suite (epic #8699) keeps that split: shot3d.py and traces.py compute, render3d.py and render_traces.py draw headlessly through matplotlib, and viewport_widgets.py only wires them to a canvas. A React port would consume shot3d.viewport_payload, which is the backend-neutral ADR-0027 ViewportOverlayPayload and carries the envelope status and fidelity tier in its metadata so any renderer can stamp its own frames. The F1 sand cut (#8710, #8711) keeps the same split and adds one constraint a port must honour: the field carries its fidelity tier and validity status as data covered by a SHA-256 over the provenance and the arrays together, so any client that renders a field must read the standing out of the file rather than out of its filename, and must refuse a field whose digest does not match. Tracking issue #8607.
