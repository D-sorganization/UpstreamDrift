02 / Capture to Analysis

Choose the Path Your Evidence Supports

Record or import, inspect observations, then follow the single-view or calibrated multi-view path. Each stage has a defined input and output.

Download Workflow Diagram ↗

One camera: 2-D events, tempo and normalized motion. Multiple cameras: calibrate lenses, check synchronization and geometric observability, then reconstruct. Fit completion and low residuals do not establish physical validity.

The F1 sand cut (#8710, #8711) keeps the same split and adds one constraint a port must honour: the field carries its fidelity tier and validity status as data covered by a SHA-256 over the provenance and the arrays together, so any client that renders a field must read the standing out of the file rather than out of its filename, and must refuse a field whose digest does not match.
