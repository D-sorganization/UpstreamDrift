## 2025-02-23 - Fast Small Vector Norms
**Learning:** Calling `np.linalg.norm()` on small vectors (e.g., 3-element coordinates or 4-element quaternions) inside tight loops carries significant overhead due to NumPy's function dispatching and safety checks.
**Action:** When computing magnitudes for individual 2D, 3D, or 4D vectors (not batched arrays) in a hot loop, use `math.hypot(*v)` instead of `np.linalg.norm(v)`. `math.hypot` bypasses NumPy's internal overhead and provides a measurable ~2-6x performance speedup. Ensure `math` is imported and the unpacking operator `*` is correctly applied to the vector.
