## Executive Summary

1. **The grip epic has three curl-to-contact solvers, and the one built as the
   canonical builder has no callers.** `grip_build` (GB-2/GB-3) is reached only
   by its own tests and its goldens script. The hand that users see is posed by
   `hand_backends/posing.py::curl_to_contact`, and the MyoHand backend by
   `hand_backends/contact_solver.py`. The shipped solver is the better one: it
   takes the *minimum* clearance and documents its contract. The orphaned
   builder has the max-not-min defect the Critic found, and it has diverged in
   other ways too (see DRY below). Settle this before the preview (GB-4) and the
   clamp controllers (GB-5) are built on one of them.
2. **Delivery shape is uneven.** The bundle-adjustment series (#15296) is a good
   tracer bullet: core, then convergence, then lane adapter, then an opt-in
   pipeline stage, then settings. Each PR is 250 to 450 lines. #15288 squashed
   about 15,000 lines and 13 change fragments into one commit, with
   `skinned_human.py` at 1,185 lines and its test at 1,197 lines. Both are within
   a few lines of the 1,200-line cap on their first day.
3. **Design by Contract is strong in the new appearance and QA code.**
   `trail_grip_qa.py` and `posing.py` state their preconditions and
   postconditions in plain words. Other repositories should copy this.

Overall health of the grip work: **Needs Attention**. The architecture is sound
and the contracts are good. The duplication is what makes the defects possible.

## Principle Assessment

### 1. DRY: Needs Attention

**Observation A: Two finger-curl builders inside `grip_build`.**
`grip_build/builder.py` and `grip_build/full_builder.py` repeat each other:

- `_validate_grip_geometry` is copied verbatim (`builder.py:37-41`,
  `full_builder.py:74-78`).
- Hand-chain construction is repeated (`builder.py:70-82` and
  `full_builder.py:81-99`), and the `0.012` palm-depth factor appears in both.
- The finger-curl loop is repeated (`builder.py:88-118` and
  `full_builder.py:118-144`), and the copies have **already diverged**. GB-2
  curls in `lead_finger_curl_order(spec)`. GB-3 hard-codes
  `("index", "middle", "ring", "little")`, so the full build ignores
  `GripSpec.curl_order`. GB-2 records `gaps_m`, while GB-3 always writes
  `gaps_m=()` (`full_builder.py:223`). A full-grip receipt therefore never
  reports a gap, even when its stages report `GAP`.

**Observation B: Three curl-to-contact solvers across the epic.**

| Solver | Location | Consumers | Clearance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capsule bisection | `grip_build/builder.py:143` (`_curl_joint`) | Tests and goldens only | Capsule SDF; Critic finding 1 says it takes the farthest sample, not the nearest |
| Skin march then bisection | `hand_backends/posing.py:307` (`_curl_joint`, `curl_to_contact`) | Skinned human hands (shipped) | `d[segment].min()`, with contract docstring |
| MyoHand mesh solver | `hand_backends/contact_solver.py:628` | MyoHand backend (research-only) | Its own vertex cache |

The two copies have drifted in a way that matters. `grip_build` sizes the trail
hand from the **lead** hand's station (`full_builder.py:89`
`geometry.radius_at(spec.lead_below_butt_m)` is used for both hands). On a
tapered grip, `GripGeometry.radius_at` changes along the shaft, so the trail
hand curls around the wrong radius. The skinned pair queries the radius at the
trail station (`skinned_pair.py:393`, `scene.radius_at_m(spacing_m)`).

**Pathway:** One solver, several obstacles. `posing.curl_to_contact` already
takes an `ObstacleDistance` callable. A `ClubSDF` adapter that returns
per-point signed distance would let `grip_build` drop its own `_curl_joint`.
The capsule hand stays useful as a cheap, engine-free obstacle and hand model,
and the duplicated loop goes away. This fixes Critic finding 1 by construction
instead of by a second patch. It also removes the two GB-2/GB-3 divergences
above. Estimated net change: about −150 lines in `grip_build`, plus an adapter
of about 40 lines. If the Board would rather keep `grip_build` separate, it
should at least be marked research-only, as MyoHand is, so that GB-4 and GB-5
do not take it as the canonical builder.

### 2. Orthogonality: Needs Attention

- `builder.py` and `full_builder.py` import each other. `builder.py:242`
  re-imports from `full_builder` at module bottom (`# noqa: E402`), and
  `builder.py:212` imports it lazily. Meanwhile `full_builder.py:11-17` imports
  the **private** names `_curl_joint`, `_merge_status` and `_JOINT_NAMES`, and
  calls `builder._curl_joint(...)` on an instance (`full_builder.py:120`).
  Importing `builder` also registers the default builder as a side effect
  (`builder.py:247-248`). The cycle comes from GB-3 extending GB-2 by reaching
  into it, not through a shared seam. A small `curl.py` that both modules
  import would break the cycle.
- `thumb_radial_azimuth_deg` (`full_builder.py:378`) measures the thumb against
  `DEFAULT_HAND_MESH_PARAMS`, not the hand size the build used, so the measure
  depends on a global default. `replace(DEFAULT_HAND_MESH_PARAMS)` with no
  overrides is a no-op copy.
- Positive: `grip_loss_evaluator`, its load sources and `GripLossPhysicsConfig`
  are cleanly separated, and the hand backend registry (#15197) gives each
  visual backend one seam plus a conformance test.

### 3. Reversibility: Healthy

New behaviour arrives behind settings with the old behaviour as the default:
`subject.body_visual_backend` (#15338), the trigger forefinger (#15288), knee
axial rotation (#15318) and the bundle-adjust stage (#15348). Each can be turned
off without a revert. `GripLossPhysicsConfig` has no defaults for friction or
thresholds, so every caller must state them (`grip_loss_config.py:38-53`).

The counter-example is magic numbers in the grip builder that should be named
or specified:

- Joint limits of 90°, 100° and 80° (`builder.py:156-160`).
- The ten-finger axial offset of 0.012 m and the interlock infeasibility cut-off
  `hand_size.length_m <= 0.145` (`full_builder.py:289`, `full_builder.py:301`).
- The radius check sampled at a fixed 0.05 m (`builder.py:38`).

The skinned path moved its equivalents into `scripts/config/skinned_human_hand.json`.

### 4. Tracer Bullets: Healthy, Uneven

- **Exemplary:** #15296 bundle adjustment. #15332 adds an engine-agnostic core
  (448 lines with tests). #15337 shows it converges at fit size. #15346 adds the
  lane adapter. #15348 adds an opt-in stage in the pipeline (256 lines). #15351
  adds settings. Every step runs end to end, and each can be reverted alone.
- **Off pattern:** `grip_build` reached GB-3 and committed goldens
  (`grip_builder_goldens.json`, 385 lines) before anything consumed the
  builder. Goldens produced by the code they check also lock in the max-not-min
  defect. That is the cost of building in depth before the thin end-to-end
  slice exists.
- **Off pattern:** #15288 (14,973 insertions, 159 files, 13 change fragments
  covering issues #15148 to #15278). One squash commit makes `git bisect` and
  `git revert` useless for any one of those 13 behaviours.

**Pathway:** Keep to the ~500-line advisory size for feature work. When a lane
lands many issues, merge each issue's slice separately, as #15296 did.

### 5. Design by Contract: Exemplary in New Appearance Code, Healthy Elsewhere

- `trail_grip_qa.py:83-89` and `posing.py:365-372` state preconditions,
  postconditions and the exceptions they raise. The posing postcondition ("no
  point that a solved joint moves is deeper than `penetration_allowance_m`")
  can be checked by a test, and it is the exact invariant the capsule builder
  breaks.
- `GripBreakawayThresholds.__post_init__` enforces the hysteresis ordering
  (exit above enter, release below slip), and its error messages say which
  contract failed.
- **Gap:** `BuildReceipt` has no invariant that ties `overall_status` and
  `gaps_m` to its stages. With one, the GB-3 `gaps_m=()` drift above would
  fail at construction. `_full_grip_result` also promotes `overall` to `OK`
  when only the four critical stages are OK (`full_builder.py:210-212`). That
  hides finger-stage `GAP` results behind an `OK` receipt, and the rule is not
  documented.

### 6. Broken Windows: Needs Attention

- **Dead branch:** `builder.py:199-202` has two consecutive conditions that
  both return `BuildStatus.GAP`. The `tol + 1e-3` test can never change the
  outcome.
- **Duplicated final pass:** `_curl_joint` repeats the clearance loop after the
  bisection (`builder.py:186-193`).
- **Files at the cap on arrival:** `skinned_human.py` (1,185 lines), its test
  (1,197 lines), `grip_contact/topology_contact.py` (1,196 lines) and
  `grip_contact/parity.py` (1,181 lines). The next small feature in any of them
  will need a split under deadline. Splitting before that point is cheaper.
- **PR-body copies under `docs/`:** `PR_BODY.md`, `PR_BODY_EARLIER.md` and
  `PR_BODY_LEARNINGS.md` under
  `docs/development/full_body_models/evidence/hand_skinned_human/`. Plain text
  is good, but these copy what lives in the PR and change fragments. The
  learnings should go to `hand_handoff/skinned_human.md`, which already
  exists.

### 7. Good Enough Software: Healthy

The grip-loss evaluator says in its own name and disclaimer that it is offline
and not a fly-off simulation. The MyoHand backend is marked research-only. The
skinned-body series adds measured girths before visual polish. Scope is held.
The one sign of over-building is the grip builder registry: a pluggable
builder registry with exactly one registered builder and no consumers.

### 8. The Power of Plain Text: Exemplary

Settings are in reviewable JSON (`golf_grip.json`, `skinned_human_hand.json`,
`hand_visual_quality.json`). Receipts and goldens are JSON. Change fragments
are Markdown. `divergence_inventory.v2.jsonl` is now one line per path and
checked by `--check`, so the 31 PRs that touched it this week merged cleanly
(UD #12833 fixed this).

### 9. Use the Shell: Healthy

Evidence renders are scripted and committed next to their images
(`render_shaft_roll_evidence.py`, `render_skinned_human_evidence.py`,
`render_palm_on_thumb.py`), so anyone can reproduce them. Goldens come from
`scripts/generate_grip_builder_goldens.py`. The CI unit gate was split into
three shards (#15319).

### 10. Transforming Programming: Needs Attention in `grip_build`

`HandChain` is mutated in place during the search. `_curl_joint` calls
`setattr(angles, f"{joint}_rad", mid)` on every bisection step
(`builder.py:167`) and leaves the chain in its last state. GB-3 depends on that
hidden state when it reads `chain.joint_angles` later. `posing._apply` returns
a new angle mapping on each trial (`posing.py:300-304`) and caches clearances
by angle. It is easier to test and safe to run in parallel. This is one more
reason to keep the posing solver.

## Patterns Observed

1. **The second copy diverges first.** Every duplicated block in `grip_build`
   has already drifted: curl order, `gaps_m`, and the radius station. Duplication
   in this epic is causing defects now, not just adding maintenance cost.
2. **Depth before breadth on new packages.** The builder reached goldens
   without a consumer, while the skinned path reached users through a different
   solver.
3. **Lanes that bundle many issues into one squash.** Small, per-issue
   PRs (#15296) bisect and revert cleanly. A lane squash (#15288) does neither.
