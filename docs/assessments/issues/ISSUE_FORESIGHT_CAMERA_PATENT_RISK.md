## Description
The use of simulated photometric measurement methods has been identified as a **HIGH** patent infringement risk. While our system uses purely simulation and does not implement actual camera-based ball tracking logic, Foresight holds broad method claims on photometric measurements. Simulating these measurements using their patented methods could still be construed as infringement.

## Action Required
- Legal review needed to ensure simulation methods do not infringe on underlying method claims of photometric-based measurements.
- Document the distinctions between our simulated photometric models and Foresight's patented methods to establish a clear non-infringing use case.
