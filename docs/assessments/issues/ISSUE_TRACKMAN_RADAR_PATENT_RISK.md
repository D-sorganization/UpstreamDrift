## Description
The use of simulated radar ball tracking methods has been identified as a **HIGH** patent infringement risk. Even though our implementation relies purely on simulation and not actual hardware radar signal processing, TrackMan holds broad method claims on radar tracking algorithms that could be infringed if our simulation methods emulate their proprietary techniques.

## Action Required
- Legal review needed to ensure simulation methods do not infringe on underlying method claims of radar-based measurements.
- Document any differences between our simulated tracking algorithms and TrackMan's patented methods to establish a clear non-infringing use case.
