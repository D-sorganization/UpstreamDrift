# Fleet Critic Summary — 2026-11-20

**Scope:** First merged slices of the grip modelling epic (#15122): GripSpec
(#15133), the procedural grip builder GB-2/GB-3 (#15137), the offline
grip-loss evaluator (#15136) and per-hand shaft roll (#15138).

**Reviewer seat:** Bravo (Fleet Critic)

**Finding count:** 5 (2 High, 1 Medium, 2 Low)

## Headline

The strongest version of the work is a clean, engine-free split: a versioned
GripSpec, a geometry-only builder with swappable contact models, and a grip-loss
evaluator with swappable load sources and an explicit "not a fly-off
simulation" disclaimer. The architecture is sound. Two core claims do not hold
yet.

First, the builder's non-penetration gate measures the wrong quantity.
`ClubSDF.signed_distance_capsule` returns the **farthest** sampled point of a
finger segment from the grip, not the nearest. A phalanx that sinks into the
grip mid-segment therefore passes the `clearance >= -pad_tol` test, and the
committed goldens were generated by the same function.

Second, the grip-loss evaluator's estimated pad shear drops the shaft-axial
pull. That pull is the load that makes a club leave the hands. The committed
"no slip" receipts are also close to circular: the loads come from a MuJoCo run
at the same μ = 0.9 being tested, and the squeeze sensitivity axis does not
change the margin, because normal and tangential force are scaled together.

## Finding Summary

| # | Topic | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Builder capsule clearance returns the farthest sample (max, not min) | High |
| 2 | Estimated pad shear drops the axial pull that causes fly-off | High |
| 3 | Grip-loss null result is near-circular; squeeze sweep is degenerate | Medium |
| 4 | Hysteresis thresholds and dwell times have no stated basis | Low |
| 5 | "Neutral" grip defaults encode one golfer's preference | Low |

Findings 1 and 2 are flagged to the Board as High. Both sit on the p1 grip
priority (#15122) and should be fixed before the preview (GB-4) and clamp
controllers (GB-5) build on them.
