\section{Scope and Inputs}
Research diagnostic for issue 15493, Slice 3; separate from the canonical
engineering design manual. Recorded and reference joint angles $q,r$ are in
unwrapped radians, velocities $v,s$ in radians per second, on the same frame,
sign convention, ordered joints and finite increasing time grid $t_i$.
There must be at least two samples. Quaternion or translational coordinates
are outside this tool's contract. The caller supplies justified per-joint
bounds $l_j\le u_j$, speed limits $V_j\ge0$, and absolute tracking budgets
$E_j,F_j\ge0$; this tool does not calibrate anatomical limits.
\section{Numerical Method and Acceptance}
For each joint $j$, compute sampled signed margins
\begin{align*}
 M^q_j &= \min_i\{q_{ij}-l_j,\;u_j-q_{ij}\},\\
 M^v_j &= V_j-\max_i|v_{ij}|,\\
 M^r_j &= E_j-\max_i|q_{ij}-r_{ij}|,\\
 M^s_j &= F_j-\max_i|v_{ij}-s_{ij}|.
\end{align*}
Sampled feasibility requires every margin to be nonnegative, including exact
zero. No numerical slack is introduced. Invalid shapes, nonfinite inputs,
misordered limits or arithmetic overflow are rejected. Actual state limits
are independent of reference tracking. No physics model or integration is
performed. Output records solution identity, joint order and sample count.
\section{Verification and Limitations}
The unit suite exercises hand-calculated violations, inclusive boundaries,
tracking independence, finite contracts and strict JSON CLI round trips
through the existing Solution Bundle codec. The initial RED experiment
failed on the absent audit module. Reproduce from the repository root with
\texttt{py -3.12 -m pytest -o addopts='' tests/unit/scripts/}\allowbreak
\texttt{test\_audit\_optimization\_kinematics.py -q}.
This editable reference has not been compiled or publication-qualified.
Sample checks do not bound inter-sample motion, validate anatomical limits,
qualify forward dynamics, or audit torque, work, impact or flight.
