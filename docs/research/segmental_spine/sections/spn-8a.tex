\subsection{OpenSim Spine Inventory (SPN-8a)}

\paragraph{Purpose.} Before any OpenSim spine model is driven, each level's
joint, coordinates, neutral offset and axis orientation are read natively and
compared with the ADR-0055 conventions. Implemented in
\texttt{src/engines/physics\_engines/opensim/python/spine/inventory.py} with the
level maps in \texttt{src/config/spine\_model\_level\_maps.json}.

\paragraph{Frames and Neutral Offset.} Vertebral frames follow ISB: $X$
anterior, $Y$ cranial, $Z$ right. For level $i$, with joint parent frame on the
inferior body and child frame on the superior body, the neutral offset (pose
of the superior frame in the inferior frame at zero coordinates, metres) is
\begin{equation}
  T_{i0} = X_{\mathrm{parent}\to\mathrm{base}}\; X_{\mathrm{child}\to\mathrm{base}}^{-1},
\end{equation}
where each factor is the frame transform in its base body
(\texttt{findTransformInBaseFrame}). Matrices are $4\times4$ and read-only.
Assumption: the joint parent frame lies on the inferior body; a model built
with the opposite orientation needs an explicit map change before use.

\paragraph{ISB Axis Check.} The coordinates $(\mathrm{flex\_ext},
\mathrm{lat\_bend}, \mathrm{axial\_rot})$ must drive the \texttt{CustomJoint}
rotation axes aligned with the ISB $ZXY$ axes $(\hat z,\hat x,\hat y)$. With a
per-coordinate sign $s_k\in\{-1,+1\}$ (the explicit axis transform in the map)
and model axis $a_k$ (normalised), the misalignment is
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_k = \arccos\!\big(\mathrm{clip}(s_k\,\hat a_k\cdot \hat e_k,-1,1)\big),
\end{equation}
accepted when $\alpha_k\le 1^\circ$. A coordinate not driving a rotation axis
gets $180^\circ$. Failures are listed in \texttt{axis\_failures}; with
\texttt{strict=True} they raise \texttt{ValueError}. This is a nominal-axis
check only: it does not test the intrinsic composition (see the SPN-7a
composition test) nor the sign of physical motion.

\paragraph{Level Maps and Licensing.} The \texttt{fixture} entry is complete
and verified (invented names, SPN-7a). Entries for
\texttt{bruno2015\_fullbody\_os4\_v2} and the two Senteler lumbar models carry
\texttt{verified: false}, status \texttt{UNVERIFIED} and no levels: no joint
name is asserted until assets (\#12749) are present; \texttt{inventory\_spine}
refuses them. No model file or transcribed number is committed.

\paragraph{Deviation.} \texttt{observe\_native\_model} reports coordinate and
constraint facts only, not offset frames or transform axes, so single-hop
adapters were added here rather than extending it.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Only \texttt{CustomJoint} rotations are checked;
translation axes, couplers and range limits are out of scope.

\begin{verbatim}
python -m pytest tests/opensim/spine/test_inventory.py -q -n 0 -o addopts=""
\end{verbatim}
