\subsection{Translational Coordinates, Passive Elements and Bruno Names}

\paragraph{Purpose.} Issue SPN-8c, \#12755, adds three things to the inventory
of SPN-8a: optional per-level translational coordinates, the runtime
extraction of intervertebral bushings (\texttt{passive\_elements.py}, same
package as \texttt{inventory.py}), and a names-only level map for the Bruno
full-body model observed on the owner's local copy. No SimTK file and no value
transcribed from one is stored in the repository.

\paragraph{Translational Coordinates.} A level map entry may name three
translation coordinates ordered (antero-posterior, cranio-caudal,
medio-lateral). They drive the translation axes $1,2,3$ of the level's
\texttt{CustomJoint}, which must align with the ISB axes $X$, $Y$, $Z$ of the
inferior frame (metres). Alignment uses the rule of the rotations,
$\angle(a, e_k)\le 1^\circ$, without sign flip; a misaligned axis is listed in
\texttt{axis\_failures}. Rotation-only maps are unchanged
(\texttt{translation\_names} is empty).

\paragraph{Bushing Assignment.} A bushing is assigned to a level through the
bodies carrying its two frames. The level's inferior body is the base body of
the level joint's parent frame and the superior body that of the child frame
(ADR-0055). The unordered body pair must equal that of a mapped level.
Bushings with a frame outside the spine bodies are ignored. A bushing with
both frames on the same body or on spine bodies that are not a mapped pair,
two bushings on one level, or an unsupported class on a mapped level raise
\texttt{ValueError}. Frame order is recorded in \texttt{frame1\_on\_inferior},
never swapped.

\paragraph{Quantities and Units.} For \texttt{BushingForce} the translational
stiffness $k_t$ (N/m), rotational stiffness $k_r$ (N\,m/rad) and the damping
$c_t$ (N\,s/m), $c_r$ (N\,m\,s/rad) are the diagonal entries in the axes of
frame~1, read from the force set at runtime. The resisting load is
$F=-\mathrm{diag}(k_t)\,\delta-\mathrm{diag}(c_t)\,\dot\delta$ and
$M=-\mathrm{diag}(k_r)\,\theta-\mathrm{diag}(c_r)\,\dot\theta$ for relative
deflection $\delta$ and small-angle deviation $\theta$ of frame~2 in frame~1.
For \texttt{ExpressionBasedBushingForce} the six expression strings and the
damping vectors are returned and the stiffness vectors are \texttt{None}.

\paragraph{Assumptions and Limitations.} Stiffness is not rotated to the ISB
vertebral axes: the frame axes are model specific. Off-diagonal terms,
\texttt{FunctionBasedBushingForce}, multiple bushings per level and
nonlinear stiffness are not represented. A level without a bushing is omitted
unless \texttt{require\_all} is set. Per-model axis alignment and coupler
summaries for the licensed Senteler models, and the comparison of Bruno's
couplers with \texttt{bruno2015\_regional}, are PENDING: the Senteler assets
are not available (\#12749) and no Senteler level map is filled.

\paragraph{Bruno Names and Axis Order.} The Bruno map was generated by a
script that reads the joint set of the owner's \texttt{MaleFullBody.osim}; only
names and topology were kept. Chain: \texttt{pelvis}, a weld to
\texttt{sacrum}, then 17 \texttt{CustomJoint}s named
\texttt{<LEVEL>\_IVDjnt} (L5\_S1 to T1\_T2) with coordinates
\texttt{<LEVEL>\_FE}, \texttt{\_LB}, \texttt{\_AR}, from
\texttt{sacrum}/\texttt{lumbar5}\ldots\texttt{lumbar1}, then
\texttt{thoracic12}\ldots\texttt{thoracic1}; the terminal body is
\texttt{thoracic1}. Observed axis order and sign relative to ADR-0055, at
every one of the 17 levels: FE rotates about $+Z$ (rotation1), LB about $+X$
(rotation2), AR about $+Y$ (rotation3), so all signs are $+1$ and the
measured misalignment is numerically zero (limit $1^\circ$).
No translational coordinates are mapped. The checks were run on the male
model only; admission is UNPINNED (hashes are the owner's to pin), so this is
a names-and-axes check, not a scientific qualification.

\paragraph{Fixture.} The CI fixture has options
\texttt{FixtureSpec(translational\_dofs=True)} and \texttt{bushings=} in
\texttt{build\_fixture\_osim} (module \texttt{fixture\_passive.py}); every
stiffness, damping and expression is INVENTED.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python -m pytest tests/opensim/spine/test_passive_elements.py \
    tests/opensim/spine/test_inventory.py -q -n 0 -o addopts=""
UD_SPINE_BRUNO_SOURCE=<path to MaleFullBody.osim> python -m pytest \
    tests/opensim/spine/test_licensed_inventory.py -q -n 0 -o addopts=""
\end{verbatim}
Without the environment variable the licensed tests skip with a reason
starting \texttt{UNQUALIFIED}; a skip is not acceptance.
