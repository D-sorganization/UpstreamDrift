\subsection{Driving a Coupled Segmental Model}
\label{sec:spn-9b}

This section documents \texttt{drive.py} (issue \#12757, epic \#12735), which
distributes the golf trunk rotation of \S\ref{sec:spn-9a} over the spinal
levels of a segmental OpenSim model, frame by frame, and exports the result as
a coordinate \texttt{.mot}.

\paragraph{Chain Identity.}
For levels $1,\dots,n$ (caudal first) with neutral offsets $T_{i0}$ (from the
native inventory) and ISB ZXY angles $\theta_i$, the terminal body rotation in
the pelvis frame is
\[
  R(\varphi) = B\,R_1\cdots R_n\,E,\qquad
  R_i = R_{i0}\,R_z(\theta_{i,1})R_x(\theta_{i,2})R_y(\theta_{i,3}),\qquad
  \theta = \mathrm{law}(\varphi).
\]
$B$ is the first inferior body in the pelvis body frame and $E$ the terminal
body in the last superior vertebra frame, both read natively at zero
coordinates (\texttt{chain\_offsets\_from\_model}); they enter the solver as
\texttt{ChainOffsets}. The connections pelvis to first body and last vertebra
to terminal body are assumed rigid.

\paragraph{Per-Frame Distribution.}
With target $R_\star = R_{\mathrm{pelvis}\to\mathrm{torso}}\,A$ each frame solves
\[
  \varphi^\ast = \arg\min_\varphi \bigl\| \log\!\bigl(R_\star^{\mathsf T}
  R(\varphi)\bigr) \bigr\|
\]
by \texttt{distribute\_series\_detailed} (warm-started analytic-Jacobian
trust-region solve). The law is never altered. The geodesic residual
$\|\log(R_\star^{\mathsf T}R(\varphi^\ast))\|$ is stored per frame; a frame is
flagged invalid when the solve did not converge or the residual exceeds the
optional \texttt{tolerance\_rad}. Invalid frames are kept as solved and are
never repaired or rescaled. When no law is given it is extracted from the
model's own \texttt{CoordinateCouplerConstraint}s. The law must cover exactly
the model's levels.

\paragraph{Frame Alignment.}
The segmental terminal frame is assumed to coincide with the
\texttt{golf\_humanoid} torso frame ($A=I$ by default), and the pelvis body
frames likewise. A known fixed rotation $A$ (terminal frame expressed in the
golf torso frame) can be supplied as \texttt{torso\_alignment}. This
assumption is not validated by the code; a wrong $A$ biases the distribution
without raising the residual.

\paragraph{Coordinate Table.}
Level coordinates are named by the inventory and equal
$s_k\,\theta_{i,k}$ with $s_k=\pm1$ the level-map axis sign. The six pelvis
coordinates are intrinsic Euler angles (sequence \texttt{ZXY} for
\texttt{golf\_humanoid}, \texttt{XYZ} for the fixture free joint; unwrapped
over time) of the pelvis pose in ground plus the pelvis origin position; the
ground-pelvis offset frames are assumed to be identity. Units are radians and
metres. Arm and leg coordinates of the golf model are not part of the
segmental model and are not exported.

\paragraph{Export.}
\texttt{export\_mot} writes the table natively with \texttt{STOFileAdapter}
and \texttt{inDegrees=no}; the independent coordinates of the law can be added
on request. \texttt{DriveResult} carries the kinematics series
(\texttt{distribution\_source}, validity mask), residual and independent
coordinate series, the coordinate table, the law id and version, the model
SHA-256 and the input source (basename and SHA-256 only).

\paragraph{Verification.}
A synthetic input generated from the fixture's own law and a known
$\varphi(t)$ is recovered with residual below $10^{-8}$~rad. The exported
\texttt{.mot} is replayed natively on the coupled fixture, and the
thorax-proxy rotation in the pelvis frame and the pelvis pose agree with the
target to $10^{-8}$. A law that cannot reach the target yields reported
residuals above $10^{-3}$~rad and invalid flags under a $10^{-6}$~rad
tolerance. Evidence: the 654-frame swing
\texttt{os7\_moco\_g1/inputs/ik\_states\_full.sto} (model
\texttt{golf\_humanoid\_scaled\_tour\_markers.osim}) driving a 17-level
fixture with the \texttt{bruno2015\_regional} law gives a maximum residual of
$1.2\times10^{-13}$~rad with a maximum $|\varphi|$ of about 2.3~rad on the
largest axis. A small residual shows that the three-parameter law reaches this
swing's rotations on the fixture chain; it is not evidence that the
distribution is physiologically correct.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rotations only; no dynamics. Axis signs come from the level-map registry, not
from the inventory object. Models whose pelvis-to-first-body or
last-vertebra-to-terminal connection is not rigid are not supported by the
native offset reader.

\end{verbatim}
