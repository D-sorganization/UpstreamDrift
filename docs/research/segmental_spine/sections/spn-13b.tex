\subsection{Movement Variants of the Matched Swing}
\label{sec:spn-13b}

This section documents \texttt{spine/movement\_variants.py} (shared, pure
NumPy), the definitions in \texttt{src/config/spine\_movement\_variants.json}
and the OpenSim adapter \texttt{opensim/python/spine/variant\_inputs.py}
(issue \#12767, epic \#12735). A movement variant is a named, versioned and
reproducible perturbation of the pelvis-to-thorax rotation series $R(t)$ of a
matched swing, expressed through one golf-term registry id
(Section~\ref{sec:spn-4a}). It produces inputs for the existing pipeline
(drive, inverse dynamics, static optimisation, joint reactions); it changes no
coupling law and adds no load model.

\paragraph{Frames and Angles.}
$R(t)$ is the thorax frame expressed in the pelvis frame (ADR-0055: X
anterior, Y cranial, Z right). Its \texttt{isb\_spine} intrinsic ZXY
decomposition is $R = R_z(\mathrm{FE})\,R_x(\mathrm{LB})\,R_y(\mathrm{AR})$.
The registry terms are $x = s\,\mathrm{AR}$
(\texttt{x\_factor\_jcs\_axial}), $l = s\,\mathrm{LB}$
(\texttt{trunk\_lateral\_flexion\_jcs}) and $e = \mathrm{FE}$
(\texttt{trunk\_extension\_jcs}), with the handed sign $s=-1$ for a
right-handed and $s=+1$ for a left-handed golfer (ADR-0055 item 4). Terms and
parameters are in degrees, time in seconds.

\paragraph{Rotation-Space Operation.}
A variant changes its term by $\delta(t)$ degrees through a composition with a
rotation about that term's joint-coordinate-system axis, with
$a(t) = s\,\delta(t)$ ($s=1$ for extension):
\begin{align}
  e:&\quad R' = R_z(a)\,R, \\
  x:&\quad R' = R\,R_y(a), \\
  l:&\quad R' = R\,R_y(\mathrm{AR})^{\mathsf T} R_x(a)\, R_y(\mathrm{AR}).
\end{align}
The extension axis is the proximal (pelvis) Z axis, the axial axis the distal
(thorax) Y axis and the lateral-flexion axis the floating axis. Because these
are the axes of the Cardan factors, $R'$ has the declared coordinate shifted by
exactly $a$ and the two others unchanged; angles are never added to a rebuilt
matrix. Frames with $|\mathrm{LB}| = 90^\circ$ (gimbal lock) inside the window
make FE and AR inseparable and raise an error.

\paragraph{Time Profiles.}
With window event times $t_0 < t_1 \le t_2 < t_3$ the C1 raised-cosine window is
$w=0$ outside $[t_0,t_3]$, $w = \tfrac12(1-\cos\pi u)$ on $[t_0,t_1]$ with
$u=(t-t_0)/(t_1-t_0)$, $w=1$ on $[t_1,t_2]$ and
$w = \tfrac12(1+\cos\pi u)$ on $[t_2,t_3]$ with $u=(t-t_2)/(t_3-t_2)$. The
operations are
\begin{align}
  \text{scale } (k):&\quad \delta(t) = (k-1)\,q(t)\,w(t), \\
  \text{offset } (d):&\quad \delta(t) = d\,w(t), \\
  \text{shift\_peak } (m):&\quad \delta(t) = q(\tau(t)) - q(t),
\end{align}
where $q$ is the term and $\tau$ the monotone piecewise-linear warp with
$\tau(t_s)=t_s$, $\tau(t_{p+m}) = t_p$, $\tau(t_e)=t_e$ on the window
$[t_s,t_e]$ and $p$ the index of the term's maximum inside it. Frames outside
the window are returned bit-identical. The pseudo-events \texttt{start} and
\texttt{end} denote the first and last sample.

\paragraph{Shipped Variants.}
Version 1 of the definitions holds five variants. Parameter bounds and the
term bounds are declared study-design limits (assumptions), not cited ranges of
motion.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\hline
Id & Term, Operation & Window & Parameter (Default, Bounds) \\
\hline
\texttt{x\_factor\_top\_scale} & $x$, scale & address, top, top, impact & $k$ (0.8, [0.5, 1.5]) \\
\texttt{downswing\_crunch\_offset} & $l$, offset & top, impact, impact, end & $d$ (5, [$-15$, 15]) deg \\
\texttt{downswing\_crunch\_scale} & $l$, scale & top, impact, impact, end & $k$ (0, [0, 2]) \\
\texttt{impact\_extension\_offset} & $e$, offset & top, impact, impact, end & $d$ (5, [$-15$, 15]) deg \\
\texttt{x\_factor\_peak\_shift} & $x$, shift\_peak & address, impact & $m$ (5, [$-20$, 20]) samples \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The X-factor at the top of the backswing is scaled ($k=0.8$ or $1.2$ as in the
issue); lead lateral flexion (crunch) is added or removed through the
downswing with the full change at impact; trunk extension at impact is a
pelvis-relative proxy of the reverse-spine-angle posture; the peak X-factor is
moved relative to the pelvis by whole samples, keeping its value.

\paragraph{Targets and Undeclared Terms.}
The target of scale and offset variants is $q'(t)=k\,q(t)$ or $q(t)+d$ at
the plateau samples; that of the peak shift is $q'(t_{p+m}) = q(t_p)$ with the
maximum at $p+m$. \texttt{apply\_variant} re-evaluates the term with the
registry on the varied rotations and asserts the target to $10^{-6}$ deg.
Each variant lists its \texttt{affected\_terms} (its term and
\texttt{crunch\_factor\_rate} when it depends on it); every other term
computable from $R(t)$ and $t$ is unchanged within
\texttt{undeclared\_tolerance\_deg} $=10^{-8}$ in that term's units.
Lab-frame terms (line turn, side bend, reverse spine angle) follow the thorax
orientation in ground and are not held. \texttt{early\_extension} uses only
the pelvis position, which no variant changes.

\paragraph{Non-Trunk Segments and Range Checks.}
All shipped variants use \texttt{thorax\_relative\_hold}: arm and head
coordinates are thorax-relative and are held, so the arms follow the thorax;
the pelvis and legs are unchanged. The club path in ground therefore changes:
a variant is a perturbation, not a re-matched swing. \texttt{variant\_golf\_input}
replaces only the trunk rotation of a \texttt{GolfTrunkInput}, maps event times
to their nearest samples and records the variant source in
\texttt{source["variant"]}, which the drive stage carries into
\texttt{DriveResult.input\_source}. \texttt{check\_model\_ranges} compares the
driven level coordinates with the target model's coordinate ranges
($[\min, \max]$ read natively); violations are reported, never clipped, and
the coupling law is untouched.

\paragraph{Provenance and Seeds.}
Every result records the variant id and version, operation, term, parameter
name, units and value (the default when not given), handedness, window events
and indices, the non-trunk policy and the SHA-256 of the input rotations and
times, and carries
\texttt{distribution\_source} $=$
\texttt{movement\_variant:<id>@<version>;<term>:<operation>(<name>=<value>);handedness=<h>}.
\texttt{sample\_variant\_parameters(variant, seed, count)} draws parameters
uniformly inside the bounds with \texttt{numpy.random.default\_rng(seed)}; there
is no global random state.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Variants act on the global pelvis-to-thorax rotation only; per-level
distribution is that of the chosen coupling law. The peak shift is quantised to
whole samples. The bounds are assumptions. Variants are kinematic what-if
inputs with no claim of physiological realisability beyond the model range
check, and the study request (Section~\ref{sec:spn-13a}) does not yet accept a
variant id (a writer of the varied golf motion file is a follow-up).

\end{verbatim}
