\subsection{Study Aggregation Tables}
\label{sec:spn-13c}

This section documents \texttt{spine/study\_aggregation.py} (issue \#12768,
epic \#12735). It reduces SPN-13a receipts (Section~\ref{sec:spn-13a}) and their
hashed \texttt{\_loads.npz} artifacts to per-level tables. It adds no model.

\paragraph{Frames and Signs.}
Loads are the force $\mathbf F$ and moment $\mathbf M$ on the superior vertebra
by the inferior one, in the inferior ISB frame (X anterior, Y cranial, Z right).
Compression is $+F_y$, anterior-posterior shear $F_x$, medio-lateral shear
$F_z$; $M_x$, $M_y$, $M_z$ are lateral bending, axial and flexion moments.
Forces are in N, moments in N$\cdot$m, time in s.

\paragraph{Statistics.}
For every run, observable and level, each component $q(t)$ yields the peak
$q(t^\ast)$ with $t^\ast=\arg\max_t |q(t)|$ (signed, earliest tie), and the
impulse $\int_{t_0}^{t_1} q\,dt$ by the trapezoid rule over a caller-supplied
window $[t_0,t_1]$ (for example the downswing) with linear interpolation at the
window edges; the default window is the whole record. With a body mass $m$ the
peak compression is also reported as $F_y/(m g)$, $g=9.80665\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
Across runs, each key (observable, level, quantity, distribution source, law
id) reports the minimum, median and maximum peak and the mean impulse.

\paragraph{Fail-Closed Rules.}
An artifact is read only when its SHA-256 equals the receipt record; a missing
or altered file aborts the aggregate. Only receipts with status \texttt{pass}
enter the aggregate; \texttt{unqualified} and \texttt{failed} runs are listed
with status and reasons and never pooled. Generalized-effort and joint-reaction
observables stay separate (a passive-element observable is not stored by
SPN-13a and is not reported). Every context comparison must carry
\texttt{is\_acceptance} false. The table's scientific status is always
\texttt{unqualified}. Input order does not change the output.

\paragraph{Variant Comparison.}
\texttt{compare} subtracts baseline median peaks from variant median peaks per
(observable, level, quantity) only when each side holds exactly one
(distribution source, law) group; otherwise the key is reported as unmatched,
so distribution laws are never pooled.

\paragraph{Outputs and Limitations.}
\texttt{write\_csv} (units in every numeric header) and \texttt{write\_json}
(sorted keys, 10 significant figures) are byte-deterministic. Peaks are not
acceptance evidence; with few runs the median is only descriptive.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python -m pytest tests/opensim/spine/test_study_aggregation.py -q -n 0
\end{verbatim}
