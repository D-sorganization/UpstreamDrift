\subsection{Per-Level Joint Reactions With Muscle Forces}
\label{sec:spn-10c}

This section documents \texttt{spine/joint\_reactions.py} (issue \#12762, epic
\#12735). It turns the muscle forces of the trunk static optimization
(Section~\ref{sec:spn-10b}) into the intervertebral joint reaction wrench of
every mapped level, as a \texttt{SpinalLoadSeries} with observable
\texttt{JOINT\_REACTION}. It is the third of the three load observables of
ADR-0055 item 5 and is never conflated with the generalized effort of
Section~\ref{sec:spn-10a}.

\paragraph{Observable and Frame.}
The wrench is the force $\mathbf F$ and moment $\mathbf M$ exerted on the
superior vertebra by the inferior vertebra through the joint, expressed in the
inferior vertebra ISB frame ($X$ anterior, $Y$ cranial, $Z$ right) about the
mid-disc joint centre, in N and N$\cdot$m. Compression is $+F_y$, anterior
shear $+F_x$, right shear $+F_z$. The joint centre is the origin of the joint
child frame, which coincides with the origin of the parent offset frame for
every level because the translations of a level are constant zero; the
reference-point offset passed to the shared transport is therefore zero. With
$R_{gP}$ the ground orientation of the parent offset frame and $R_{i0}$ the
neutral rotation from the inventory (the convention of the ID moments),
\[
  \mathbf F_{\mathrm{isb}} = R_{i0} R_{gP}^{\mathsf T}\,\mathbf F_g , \qquad
  \mathbf M_{\mathrm{isb}} = R_{i0} R_{gP}^{\mathsf T}\,\mathbf M_g ,
\]
where $(\mathbf F_g,\mathbf M_g)$ is the output of
\texttt{Joint.calcReactionOnChildExpressedInGround}, the call behind the
OpenSim \texttt{JointReaction} analysis with \texttt{apply\_on\_bodies=child}.

\paragraph{What the Reaction Contains.}
Muscles are external forces on the bodies and are not part of the reaction.
The reaction is what the joint transmits: the translational constraint force
plus the torque of the residual (reserve) actuation at the joint, because a
reserve is a generalized force acting at the joint and not a muscle. Hence for
each level the generalized effort $\tau^{\mathrm{ID}}$ of
Section~\ref{sec:spn-10a} decomposes exactly as
\[
  \mathbf M^{\mathrm{ID}} = \mathbf M^{\mathrm{react}} + \mathbf M^{\mathrm{musc}},
  \qquad
  \mathbf M^{\mathrm{musc}} = \sum_{p\in\text{above}} (\mathbf x_p - \mathbf c)
  \times \mathbf f_p ,
\]
with $\mathbf c$ the joint centre, $\mathbf x_p$ the path points attached to the
bodies above the joint and $\mathbf f_p$ the muscle force on point $p$. Where
the muscles fully explain the effort the reserve is zero and the reaction
moment vanishes, because the three joint rotations are free.

\paragraph{Method.}
Per frame selected by the static optimization:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Coordinates, speeds and accelerations $(q,u,\dot u)$ come from the same
        motion, derivatives and mobility layout as the inverse dynamics
        (one shared private loader). The optional low-pass cutoff must equal
        the one used for the SO input and the sample times must match the SO
        frame times; otherwise a \texttt{ValueError} is raised.
  \item Each muscle is set to its SO tendon force $F_m$ with
        \texttt{overrideActuation} (rigid tendon, so no fibre equilibrium is
        solved). The model copy has couplers disabled (ADR-0055 item 6).
  \item \texttt{InverseDynamicsSolver} returns the residual generalized force
        $r$ on every coordinate that, together with gravity, external loads
        and the muscles, produces the measured $\dot u$. On the level
        coordinates $r$ is the reserve and must equal the SO reserve within
        \texttt{reserve\_tolerance\_nm} ($10^{-6}$\,N$\cdot$m); a larger
        mismatch means the SO result does not belong to this model, motion or
        filter and is refused. On the six ground-pelvis coordinates $r$ is the
        pelvis residual wrench (recorded, not part of a spine reaction).
  \item $r$ is applied through one unit-optimal-force
        \texttt{CoordinateActuator} per coordinate with override actuation.
        Forward dynamics at the state then gives accelerations equal to the
        measured ones; $\max|\dot u-\dot u_{\mathrm{meas}}|$ is verified
        against \texttt{acceleration\_tolerance} ($10^{-6}$) and recorded. This
        is the semantics of the OpenSim \texttt{JointReaction} analysis driven
        by CMC or static-optimization actuators plus residuals.
  \item The reaction at every level joint is read, rotated into the inferior
        ISB frame and assembled by \texttt{spine.loads.from\_wrench\_in\_frame}.
\end{enumerate}
A body with zero mass (the coupler driver body of the fixtures) makes the mass
matrix singular and the accelerations NaN, so such a body is given
$10^{-9}$\,kg and an isotropic inertia of $10^{-12}$\,kg\,m$^2$ in the run
copy; its weight is below $10^{-8}$\,N, negligible against the $10^{-6}$\,N
test accuracy, and the names are recorded in the provenance.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
The series carries \texttt{model\_id}, \texttt{model\_sha256}, \texttt{solver}
(JointReaction semantics), \texttt{cost\_exponent} (copied from the SO result),
\texttt{distribution\_source} and \texttt{external\_loads} (copied from the SO
result; the loads of the run must be the same ones, else a \texttt{ValueError}
is raised), plus \texttt{couplers}, \texttt{muscles}, \texttt{reserves},
\texttt{acceleration\_consistency}, \texttt{pelvis\_residual},
\texttt{massless\_bodies}, \texttt{filter\_hz}, \texttt{units}, \texttt{frame}
and \texttt{sign}.

\paragraph{Assumptions and Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Rigid-tendon muscles at the SO force; compliant-tendon equilibrium and
        activation dynamics are not represented.
  \item The output is defined only on the SO frames; no interpolation.
  \item Passive intervertebral elements (disc, ligaments) are not modelled in
        the fixtures and, when a model carries them, appear inside the
        reaction; they are not separated (the \texttt{PASSIVE\_ELEMENT}
        observable is a separate series).
  \item The exact pelvis residual shares of the golf humanoid and the native
        Bruno run on the owner workstation are deferred on \#12749.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Acceptance Tests.}
All fixture values are invented. A vertical stack (zero lordosis, sacral slope
and thorax tilt) gives compression equal to the weight above each level, zero
shear and zero moment to $10^{-6}$\,N. A tilted stack matches a rigid-body
sum built from the model body poses and mass centres. Rotating the whole model
about the ground vertical leaves the loads unchanged. A single muscle of
100\,N changes the reaction by the force and moment of its path points above
the joint, computed by hand from the path geometry. The effort decomposition
above holds to $10^{-6}$\,N$\cdot$m on a moving trunk with a real SO result.
An inconsistent reserve is refused, couplers are disabled, and the provenance
is complete.
