\subsection{Distribution of a Pelvis-to-Thorax Rotation Over Levels}
\label{sec:spn-2b}

\paragraph{Problem.}
A measured or matched pelvis$\to$terminal rotation $R_{\mathrm{target}}$ does not
identify the individual intervertebral rotations. The distribution is therefore
defined through a fixed coupling law $\theta_i = f_i(\phi)$ (SPN-2a) with
independent coordinates $\phi\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$:
\begin{equation}
  \phi^{*}=\arg\min_{\phi\in[\ell,u]}
  \left\lVert \log\!\left(R_{\mathrm{target}}^{\mathsf T}\,R(\phi)\right)\right\rVert_2,
  \qquad
  R(\phi)=B\,\Bigl[\textstyle\prod_{i}^{\text{caudal first}} N_i R_i(\theta_i(\phi))\Bigr]\,E .
\end{equation}
$N_i$ is the neutral rotation block, $R_i$ the ISB ZXY rotation of
$\theta_i=(\mathrm{FE},\mathrm{LB},\mathrm{AR})$ (rad), $B,E$ optional fixed base and
terminal rotations, and $\log$ the SO(3) rotation vector
(\texttt{scipy.spatial.transform.Rotation.as\_rotvec}). Composition and ZXY
construction reuse \texttt{SegmentalPose.compose} and
\texttt{level\_rotations}; frames are those of ADR-0055 (X anterior, Y cranial,
Z right). Units: radians.

\paragraph{Law Never Altered.}
The law, its matrices and neutral offsets are inputs only. An unreachable target
yields a positive geodesic residual that is reported, not repaired. The
residual returned equals the independently recomputed geodesic distance of the
returned pose (checked to $10^{-10}$ rad). \texttt{converged} is the optimizer's
flag and does not imply a small residual. \texttt{DistributionResult.check\_tolerance}
flags or raises on an acceptance tolerance without rescaling anything.

\paragraph{Analytic Jacobian.}
Let $E=R_{\mathrm{target}}^{\mathsf T}R$ and $r=\log E$. A perturbation of angle
$k$ of level $i$ rotates $R$ by $[a_{ik}]_\times R_i$ in the parent frame, where
the ISB ZXY axes are
$a_{1}=(0,0,1)^{\mathsf T}$,
$a_{2}=(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha,0)^{\mathsf T}$,
$a_{3}=(-\sin\alpha\cos\beta,\ \cos\alpha\cos\beta,\ \sin\beta)^{\mathsf T}$
with $\alpha=\mathrm{FE}$, $\beta=\mathrm{LB}$. The body-frame angular
perturbation is $w_{ik}=(R_iB_i)^{\mathsf T}a_{ik}$ with $B_i$ the product of the
factors cranial to level $i$ (times $E$). With the right-Jacobian inverse
\begin{equation}
  J_r^{-1}(r)=I+\tfrac12[r]_\times+\Bigl(\tfrac1{\theta^2}-\tfrac{1+\cos\theta}{2\theta\sin\theta}\Bigr)[r]_\times^{2},\quad\theta=\lVert r\rVert,
\end{equation}
the residual Jacobian is $\partial r/\partial\phi=J_r^{-1}(r)\sum_{i,k}w_{ik}\,(\partial\theta_i/\partial\phi)_{k,:}$,
with $\partial\theta_i/\partial\phi$ from \texttt{law.jacobian}. A Taylor series
replaces the coefficient for $\theta<10^{-4}$. The solver is
\texttt{scipy.optimize.least\_squares} (trust-region reflective, bounds as box
constraints). A central finite-difference test oracle verifies the Jacobian to
$10^{-7}$. The optimizer's termination tolerances are $10^{-5}\,\texttt{tol\_rad}$
(floor $10^{-15}$), so reachable targets end far inside \texttt{tol\_rad}.

\paragraph{Observability Report.}
At $\phi^{*}$ the $3\times n$ body Jacobian $W=\sum w_{ik}(\partial\theta_i/\partial\phi)$
is decomposed by SVD. Rank is counted above $10^{-8}\sigma_{\max}$. Reported:
rank, singular values, the null space of $W$ (coordinate directions the target
cannot identify, with the coordinate names carrying them), and the left-null
directions (rotations the law cannot produce at $\phi^{*}$). A law with $n>3$
or dependent columns is flagged \texttt{underdetermined}; the solver then
returns one minimizer near its start, not a unique one.

\paragraph{Series.}
\texttt{distribute\_series} solves frames in order, warm-starting each from the
previous $\phi^{*}$ (clipped to bounds); coordinates are linear so there is no
angle wrapping. A frame is \texttt{valid} when it converged and, optionally,
its residual is within \texttt{tolerance\_rad}. The series carries
\texttt{distribution\_source} \texttt{coupling:<law\_id>@<version>}.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rotation only (level translations are zero); local minimum possible for strongly
state-dependent laws; residuals near $\pi$ rad have a singular $\log$. Inverse
kinematics and measured distributions use other \texttt{distribution\_source}
prefixes (ADR-0055 item 7).

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python -m pytest tests/shared/python/spine/test_distribution.py -q -n 0
\end{verbatim}
Tests: identifiable round trip ($10^{-8}$ in $\phi$, residual $<10^{-9}$);
one-DOF axial law with target $R_y(a)R_x(b)$ whose analytic minimum is $b$;
law matrices bit-identical before and after; warm start versus cold start;
rank-deficient law; precondition failures.
