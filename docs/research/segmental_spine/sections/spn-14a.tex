\subsection{Distribution Sensitivity Within One Model}
\label{sec:spn-14a}

This section documents \texttt{spine/distribution\_sensitivity.py} and the
law restriction \texttt{coupling.restrict\_to\_levels} (issue \#12769, epic
\#12735). Per-level angles and loads depend on the assumed distribution
(coupling) law; this study quantifies that dependence inside one model before
any cross-model comparison, which would confound the law with geometry,
stiffness and muscles. Its scientific status is \emph{unqualified}; no spread
below is acceptance evidence.

\paragraph{Design.}
One SPN-13a request (Section~\ref{sec:spn-13a}) is run once per shipped law
$k\in K$ (default: every non-pending law in
\texttt{src/config/spine\_coupling\_laws.json}). Between runs only
\texttt{study\_id} and \texttt{coupling\_law\_id} change; motion, golf model,
segmental model, external loads, cost exponent, frame selection, residual and
reserve budgets are identical. After the runs the receipts must agree on every
input SHA-256 and on every other request field, otherwise the report is
refused (\texttt{SensitivityIntegrityError}). Couplers prescribe kinematics
only and are disabled for inverse dynamics, static optimisation and joint
reactions (ADR-0055), so the law enters the loads solely through the
prescribed per-level kinematics.

\paragraph{Law Restriction.}
A shipped law may cover more levels than a model. With
\texttt{restrict\_law\_to\_model} the study keeps the law's matrices $C_i$ for
the model's mapped levels $i\in\mathcal L$ only:
$\theta_i = C_i\,\varphi$ for $i\in\mathcal L$, bit-identical $C_i$, version
tagged \texttt{<version>+levels:<first>..<last>} and the citation marked as a
repository operation. Per-level ratios within $\mathcal L$ are preserved;
absolute shares are not, because the distribution solve
$\varphi^\ast=\arg\min_\varphi\|\log(R_{\mathrm{target}}^{\mathsf T}R(\varphi))\|$
rescales $\varphi$ until the composed range reaches the target. A law covering
fewer levels than the model fails the drive stage and is excluded. A request
without the flag hashes exactly as before.

\paragraph{Spread Definitions.}
With $p_k(q)$ the signed peak (value of largest magnitude over the record) of
quantity $q$ under law $k$,
\begin{equation}
  s(q)=\max_{k\in K}p_k(q)-\min_{k\in K}p_k(q),\qquad
  r(q)=\frac{s(q)}{\max_{k\in K}|p_k(q)|}\quad(r=0\text{ if all }p_k=0).
\end{equation}
Kinematic quantities are the per-level ISB ZXY angles
$(\theta_{FE},\theta_{LB},\theta_{AR})$ in rad, read back from the hashed
\texttt{.mot} artifact (Section~\ref{sec:spn-9d}); they also carry the largest
pairwise RMS difference $\max_{k<l}\sqrt{\overline{(\theta_k-\theta_l)^2}}$ of
the time series. Load quantities are the SPN-13c median peaks per observable
(generalized effort, joint reaction), level and component in the ADR-0055
frame (compression $+F_y$ in N, shears $F_x$, $F_z$ in N, moments in
N$\cdot$m), compared through \texttt{study\_aggregation.compare} so laws are
never pooled (Section~\ref{sec:spn-13c}). A quantity is labelled
\emph{robust} if $r(q)\le\tau$ and \emph{sensitive} otherwise; $\tau=0.1$ is a
declared reporting threshold, not a tolerance. Each law row carries its id,
version, citation, \texttt{verified\_from\_primary}, distribution source and
the drive's geodesic residual (maximum and RMS, rad).

\paragraph{Fail-Closed Rules.}
Only receipts with status \texttt{pass} enter a spread; \texttt{unqualified}
and \texttt{failed} runs are listed as excluded with their reasons and are
never tuned into passing. With fewer than two qualified laws no spread is
reported and the note says why. Static optimisation does not converge on the
first two samples of the SPN-13a synthetic motion on the invented fixture, so
the CI request uses the mid and late event frames exactly as the SPN-13a tests
do; no tolerance was changed.

\paragraph{Fixture Result (Deterministic, Invented).}
Five-level lumbar fixture (L5--S1 to L1--L2), five-sample synthetic motion,
cost exponent 3, all three shipped laws restricted to the lumbar range, drive
tolerance unset. All three runs pass. Geodesic drive residual (max/RMS, rad):
\texttt{bruno2015\_regional} $5.1\times10^{-13}/2.3\times10^{-13}$;
\texttt{uniform\_reference} $1.9\times10^{-16}/1.5\times10^{-16}$;
\texttt{fujii2007\_lumbar} $0.89/0.78$. Selected peaks:

\hline
L5--S1 & $\theta_{FE}$ [rad] & $-0.216$ & $-0.097$ & $-0.216$ & 0.55 \\
L5--S1 & $\theta_{AR}$ [rad] & $0.0845$ & $0.0819$ & $0.0845$ & 0.03 \\
L3--L4 & $\theta_{LB}$ [rad] & $0.024$ & $0.174$ & $0.024$ & 0.86 \\

\end{tabular}

Of the 60 per-level quantities, 3 axial-rotation angles (L5--S1 to L3--L4) and
3 load components are robust at $\tau=0.1$; the rest are sensitive. Every
sensitive entry is driven by \texttt{fujii2007\_lumbar}, which has a single
independent coordinate (trunk axial rotation) and therefore cannot reproduce
the three-axis golf target (residual 0.89 rad): its spread mixes the
distribution assumption with a different realised trunk motion and must not be
read as distribution sensitivity alone. With the drive tolerance set to
$10^{-6}$ rad the Fujii run is \texttt{unqualified} (five invalid drive frames)
and excluded; the two remaining laws give $r\le 8.5\times10^{-11}$ for all 60
quantities, because \texttt{bruno2015\_regional} is uniform within the lumbar
region and, restricted to a lumbar-only model, prescribes the same per-level
ratios as \texttt{uniform\_reference}.

\paragraph{Conclusion and Limitations.}
On the invented lumbar fixture no shipped pair of laws that reproduces the
input distributes it differently, so the fixture cannot yet show which
per-level quantities are robust to the assumption; the per-level ratio laws
that would discriminate (\texttt{bruno2015\_per\_level},
\texttt{pearcy1984\_lumbar}) are pending (\#12743). Axial rotation was the only
angle that stayed within $\tau$ even under the non-reproducing Fujii law.
Thoracic-versus-lumbar sensitivity requires a model with thoracic levels; the
owner-workstation run on the licensed Bruno model is blocked on the SimTK
download (\#12749), and its table is not yet available. Spreads are
descriptive, from five synthetic samples and invented inertia and muscles; they
are not physiological and not acceptance.

\paragraph{Failed Attempts.}
Running the shipped laws unchanged on the fixture fails in the drive stage:
every shipped law covers more levels than the five-level fixture (Bruno and
uniform: L5--S1 to T1--T2; Fujii: L5--S1 to T12--L1). This motivated the
explicit, recorded restriction instead of a silent truncation.
