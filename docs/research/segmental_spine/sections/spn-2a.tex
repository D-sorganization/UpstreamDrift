\subsection{Spinal Coupling Laws}
\label{sec:spn-2a}

This section documents \texttt{src/shared/python/spine/coupling.py} (issue
\#12741, epic \#12735), which implements item 6 of ADR-0055. Couplings
prescribe kinematics only; they are disabled for inverse dynamics, static
optimization and joint reaction, so constraint multipliers never enter disc
loads.

\paragraph{Definitions and Units.}
Independent coordinates $\phi \in \mathbb{R}^{n_\phi}$ map to per-level ISB ZXY
angles $\theta_i = (\mathrm{FE}, \mathrm{LB}, \mathrm{AR})$ in radians
(frames: X anterior, Y cranial, Z right). Linear law:
$\theta_i = C_i\,\phi$, $C_i \in \mathbb{R}^{3\times n_\phi}$, cross-axis terms
allowed (for example axial rotation inducing lateral bending with a sign flip
at L5-S1). State-dependent law: $\theta_i = C_i(s)\,\phi$, with $C_i$
piecewise-linear in the scheduling coordinate $s=\phi_k$ over breakpoints
$s_0<\dots<s_m$ and held constant outside $[s_0,s_m]$. The Jacobian is
\[
  \frac{\partial\theta_i}{\partial\phi} = C_i(s)
  + \Bigl(\tfrac{dC_i}{ds}\,\phi\Bigr)e_k^{\top},
\]
using the right-hand slope at interior breakpoints and zero slope outside the
range. The regional fraction of axis $a$ over a level set $R$ is
\[
  f_R = \frac{\sum_{i\in R}\theta_i[a]}{\sum_{i\in\mathrm{all}}\theta_i[a]},
\]
undefined (\texttt{ValueError}) when the denominator magnitude is below
$10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{MyoFullBody Adapter.}
\texttt{from\_joint\_coupling} expresses single-driver
\texttt{JointCoupling} equalities as a \texttt{PolynomialCouplingLaw}:
$\theta[a] = q_{0,\mathrm{dep}} + \sum_k c_k (q_{\mathrm{drv}}-q_{0,\mathrm{drv}})^k$,
which reproduces \texttt{JointCoupling.expand} exactly for the mapped dependent
joints. A \texttt{level\_map} sends qpos addresses to independent names
(\texttt{str}) or to \texttt{(SpinalLevel, axis)}. Chained drivers, unmapped
drivers, an independent name given to two drivers, duplicate level and axis
pairs and non-contiguous level sets raise \texttt{ValueError}. The module
\texttt{myofullbody} is unmodified and does not import \texttt{spine}.
\texttt{level\_rotations} builds a \texttt{SegmentalPose} through
\texttt{LevelTransform.from\_isb\_angles}.

\paragraph{Preconditions and Provenance.}
Every law needs non-empty id, version and citation (DOI plus table or page),
finite matrices of the stated shape, and a contiguous level set (checked with
the ordinal adjacency of \texttt{SpinalLevel}). Coefficients in the tests are
invented fixtures, not transcribed from any model or paper.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The laws are kinematic maps only: no stiffness, no limits, no muscle effects.
Linear interpolation in $s$ is $C^0$ in $C_i(s)$, so the Jacobian is
discontinuous at breakpoints. Angles are not summed across levels
(ADR-0055 item 3).

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python -m pytest tests/shared/python/spine/test_coupling.py -q -n 0
\end{verbatim}
Checks: regional fraction 0.17 for a lumbar axial example, cross-axis sign flip,
Jacobians against central differences ($10^{-7}$), adapter versus
\texttt{JointCoupling.expand}, and all preconditions.
