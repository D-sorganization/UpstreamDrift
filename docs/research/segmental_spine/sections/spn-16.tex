\subsection{Documentation Consolidation and Governance}
\label{sec:spn-16}

This section records the consolidation of the epic's documentation (issue
\#12773, epic \#12735). It adds no model, equation or number.

\paragraph{Surfaces.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The design-manual chapter
        \texttt{manuals/upstreamdrift/chapters/66-segmental-spine.qmd} (continued in
        \texttt{68-segmental-spine-loads-and-studies.qmd})
        summarises conventions, composition, coupling laws and their sign maps,
        distribution, golf terms, the load contract, inverse dynamics, static
        optimisation, joint reactions, studies, aggregation, movement variants,
        distribution sensitivity, visualisation, owner-blocked work and
        limitations. QMD is its only editable source.
  \item The calculation-registry blocker
        \texttt{UP-D1-segmental-spine-inventory} describes the implemented
        pathway and stays open; no calculation is registered.
  \item \texttt{docs/agents/shared-infrastructure.md} lists every public module
        of \texttt{src/shared/python/spine/} and
        \texttt{src/engines/physics\_engines/opensim/python/spine/}, plus
        \texttt{static\_opt\_core.py} and
        \texttt{injury/spinal\_load\_providers.py}.
  \item \texttt{docs/research/segmental\_spine/README.md} gives the study and
        distribution-sensitivity commands and a minimal request.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Status.}
Every result of the pathway is scientifically unqualified. The licensed SimTK
models (\#12749), primary PDFs (\#12737), licensed-model inventory (\#12755),
full-body Bruno driving (\#12758), Bruno reproduction (\#12763) and the
cross-model comparison (\#12770) are owner-blocked. A rendered chapter is not
approval.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -m scripts.check_design_manual_governance
python3 scripts/check_document_title_case.py
\end{verbatim}
The README example was executed on the invented fixture with a five-sample
synthetic swing: the study returned \texttt{unqualified} (pelvis residual over
budget without external loads, two frames using reserves), as documented.
