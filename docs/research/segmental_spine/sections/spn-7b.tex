\subsection{Fixture Coupler Constraints and Lumped Trunk Muscles}
\label{sec:spn-7b}

This section extends the synthetic fixture of Section~\ref{sec:spn-7a} with
optional kinematic coupling and lumped trunk muscles. All numbers are invented;
nothing is transcribed from a SimTK model. Generation is pure ElementTree and
needs no OpenSim import
(\texttt{fixture\_coupling.py}, called from \texttt{build\_fixture\_osim(spec, coupling, muscles)}).

\paragraph{Coupling Law.} Only the linear family
$\theta_i = C_i\,\phi$, $C_i\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times n_\phi}$, is representable
($\theta_i=(\mathrm{FE},\mathrm{LB},\mathrm{AR})$ in rad, $\phi$ in rad or m).
State-dependent and polynomial laws raise \texttt{TypeError}. Cross-axis terms
(off-diagonal entries of $C_i$) are exact because OpenSim's
\texttt{LinearFunction} is multivariate.

\paragraph{Massless Driver.} The independent coordinates are placed on a
massless, inertia-free body \texttt{spine\_coupling\_driver} joined to the pelvis
by the \texttt{CustomJoint} \texttt{spine\_coupling\_driver\_jnt} with identity
offsets. $\phi_0,\phi_1,\phi_2$ rotate about $Z$, $X$, $Y$ and
$\phi_3,\phi_4,\phi_5$ translate along $X$, $Y$, $Z$ (at most six, ranges
$\pm1$). Nothing hangs off the driver, so inventories exclude it by name and it
cannot contribute to inverse dynamics or joint reaction. Independent names must
be unique and must not clash with level coordinates.

\paragraph{Couplers.} For every level in the law and each row $r$ of $C_i$ one
\texttt{CoordinateCouplerConstraint} is added with dependent coordinate
$\theta_{i,r}$, independent coordinates the law's names and
\texttt{LinearFunction} coefficients $[C_{i,r,1},\dots,C_{i,r,n_\phi},0]$:
\begin{equation}
  q_{\mathrm{dep}} = \sum_k C_{i,r,k}\,\phi_k + 0 .
\end{equation}
The composed pose is still
$T_{\mathrm{base}}\,T_1\cdots T_n\,T_{\mathrm{term}}$ with $T_i$ built from
$\theta_i=C_i\phi$ (angles never add).

\paragraph{Muscles.} Eight bilateral \texttt{Millard2012EquilibriumMuscle}
objects (erector spinae, rectus abdominis, external and internal oblique, suffix
\texttt{\_l}/\texttt{\_r}) run \texttt{pelvis} $\to$ \texttt{L3} (via point)
$\to$ \texttt{thorax\_proxy}. Right-side points have $z>0$ and the left side
mirrors $z\to -z$. Invented values: maximum isometric forces 1500, 900, 700,
600~N; optimal fibre length 0.12~m; zero pennation. Each tendon slack length is the analytic neutral path length (sum of segment lengths between the path points in the pelvis frame) minus the optimal fibre length, so fibres sit at optimal length at neutral.
The two obliques cross the midline in opposite senses so that their axial
moment arms are non-zero and of opposite sign. No physiological claim is made.

\paragraph{Verification.} Native tests (OpenSim 4.6) load the coupled model,
prescribe $\phi$ (locked) and call \texttt{assemble}; every level coordinate
equals $C_i\phi$ to $10^{-8}$ and the thorax-proxy pose equals the
\texttt{level\_rotations} composition to $10^{-8}$. For the muscled model the
state derivatives are finite, moment arms about every level coordinate are
finite, and mirror exactly (flexion even, lateral bending and axial rotation
odd, to $10^{-9}$).

\paragraph{Limitations.} The coupled model is kinematic only: the massless
driver coordinates give a singular mass matrix, so forward dynamics returns
non-finite derivatives (asserted by a test); couplers are disabled for ID, SO
and joint reaction (ADR-0055 item~6), which use the uncoupled fixture. A
CoordinateCouplerConstraint prescribes the level coordinates, so their own
limits are not enforced independently. The muscle geometry is not anatomical and
passing the tests establishes software correctness only, not scientific
qualification.

\begin{verbatim}

\end{verbatim}
