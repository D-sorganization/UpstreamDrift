\section{Limitations}
Each subsection states its own limitations; the cross-cutting ones are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No Qualified Result.} Every receipt, aggregate, sensitivity report and plot carries \texttt{scientific\_status = unqualified}; context bands from the golf literature are comparisons, never acceptance criteria (\texttt{is\_acceptance = false}).
  \item \textbf{Invented Fixture.} All native tests run on a repository-authored five-level lumbar fixture whose geometry, inertia, stiffness and muscles are invented. Fixture numbers say nothing about physiology.
  \item \textbf{Owner-Blocked Validation.} The licensed Bruno 2015 model (SimTK download, \#12749), the primary-source PDFs (\#12737), and the children that need them (\#12755, \#12758, \#12763, \#12770, the native Bruno static-optimization run of \#12761 and the owner-workstation Bruno sensitivity table of \#12769) are deferred, not faked.
  \item \textbf{Distribution Is an Assumption.} Per-level angles come from a coupling law, not a measurement. The shipped laws are \texttt{bruno2015\_regional}, \texttt{fujii2007\_lumbar} and \texttt{uniform\_reference}; \texttt{bruno2015\_per\_level} and \texttt{pearcy1984\_lumbar} remain pending, so robustness to the distribution cannot yet be concluded (Section~\ref{sec:spn-14a}).
  \item \textbf{Fujii Flexion Form.} \texttt{fujii2007\_lumbar} (version 2) treats coupled flexion as even in the trunk rotation, linear in $|\phi|$ and extrapolated from a single $45^\circ$ measurement; the law is not differentiable at $\phi=0$ (subgradient 0) and cannot be exported as OpenSim linear couplers.
  \item \textbf{Golf Terms Are Path-Dependent.} Per-level contributions are path-dependent decompositions, not Cardan angles of the individual levels. \texttt{segmental\_contribution\_breakdown} (\#12822) attributes the base, terminal and alignment rotations $B$, $E$ and $A$ as separately named non-level entries, so level shares plus named entries close on $f(B\,C\,E\,A)$ to $10^{-9}$ degrees; callers must supply the same $B$, $E$ and $A$ as the global term. The chain-only helpers (\texttt{segmental\_contributions} and the SPN-15a stack) assume the identity. Level shares depend on $B$ and on every caudal level.
  \item \textbf{Receipts Pin Bytes, Not Meaning.} Hashes prove which inputs were used, not that they are anatomically correct. Every ExternalLoads data file referenced by the XML is hashed, and a missing one is an error (\#12826).
  \item \textbf{Drive Alignment Is Recorded, Not Validated.} The terminal-to-thorax alignment $A$ must be supplied with a calibration source; an unsupplied $A$ is recorded as \texttt{assumed\_identity} and makes the study \texttt{unqualified} (\#12823). A supplied $A$ is hashed but not validated, and a wrong $A$ biases every per-level angle without raising the drive residual.
  \item \textbf{Residual Height Is a Model Extent.} Without \texttt{body\_height\_m} the moment-budget height is the extent of the body origins along the model gravity direction at the first frame (\#12824), not a measured stature; it does not follow the pose during the swing, so $r_M$ is a conservative scaling, not a physiological normalisation.
  \item \textbf{Undeclared-Term Check Scope.} A movement variant is checked at run time (\#12825) only on the terms computable from the pelvis-to-thorax rotation and time; lab-frame terms (line turn, side bend, reverse spine angle) are not held, so a variant can change them without being flagged.
  \item \textbf{Stored Observables.} Study artifacts store generalized effort and joint reactions; passive-element loads and per-level poses are not yet stored, so aggregation and the viewer do not report them.
\end{itemize}

\section{Failed Experiments}
Failed and corrected attempts, with the test that now guards each:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Fujii Sign Map.} The first Fujii 2007 transcription used Fujii's flexion-positive convention directly; the ISB map is now explicit (\texttt{distribution.isb\_sign}: coupled flexion $-1$, coupled lateral bend $-1$, axial rotation $+1$). Guard: \path{tests/shared/python/spine/test_coupling_data.py}.
  \item \textbf{Coupler Closure at $10^{-9}$.} With OpenSim's default assembly accuracy ($10^{-9}$) the coupled fixture missed the $10^{-9}$ closure tolerance. The tolerance was kept; the fixture now assembles at $10^{-12}$. Guard: \path{tests/opensim/spine/}.
  \item \textbf{Muscles in Generalized Effort.} The first inverse-dynamics pass included muscle forces, so \texttt{GENERALIZED\_EFFORT} was not the net joint moment; muscles are now excluded. Guard: \texttt{test\_generalized\_effort\_excludes\_muscle\_forces}.
  \item \textbf{Fixture Tendon Slack.} Tests first retuned tendon slack lengths after building the fixture; the generator now sets slack $=$ neutral path length $-$ optimal fibre length analytically.
  \item \textbf{Legacy Golf Terms and NaN.} Delegating the legacy X-factor and crunch terms initially rejected NaN input, changing behaviour; they now propagate NaN.
  \item \textbf{Model Provider Reporting SAFE.} Model-based loads without a risk assessment initially reported \texttt{SAFE}; they now report \texttt{NOT\_ASSESSED}.
  \item \textbf{Static Optimization at Early Samples (Open).} On the invented fixture with the five-sample synthetic motion, static optimization does not converge at the first two samples; those runs are reported \texttt{unqualified} and the pass-path tests select the mid and late events. The cause has not been investigated.
  \item \textbf{Distribution Sensitivity With Fujii (Open).} Fujii 2007 has one independent coordinate and cannot reproduce a three-axis target (drive residual up to 0.89\,rad), so its spread confounds the law with a different realised motion; at a $10^{-6}$\,rad drive tolerance it is excluded, and Bruno and uniform then agree to $\le 8.5\times10^{-11}$ on the lumbar fixture (Section~\ref{sec:spn-14a}).
  \item \textbf{Gravity-Only Study Run.} The README study run on the fixture exits \texttt{unqualified} (pelvis residual over budget, reserve frames flagged). With no external support loads the pelvis residual carries gravitational support plus the prescribed whole-body inertial demand; only for a stationary model does its force equal body weight, so the failure is an observation on this run, not an identity for every motion. Budgets were not widened.
  \item \textbf{Fujii Parity Defect (Fixed in \#12821).} Version 1 of \texttt{fujii2007\_lumbar} was linear in one coordinate, so every coupled term was odd in the trunk rotation and a negative (right) rotation, as in a right-handed backswing, gave coupled extension although Fujii measured flexion for rotation to either side. The defect was in the linear single-coordinate form, not the transcription. Version 2 is a \texttt{ParityCouplingLaw}: coupled flexion is $-\bar\theta_{\mathrm{flex}}|\phi|/45^\circ$ (even), lateral bend and axial rotation stay odd, and the row numbers are unchanged. Guard: \path{tests/shared/python/spine/test_coupling_parity.py}.
\end{enumerate}
