\section{Scope and Conventions}
This editable research note describes a controller primitive, not the
engineering design manual, a qualified golfer model or a forward-dynamics
acceptance. Time is in seconds, generalized angles in radians and generalized
torques in newton metres where channels are rotational. The caller must bind
channel order, units and model, and must exclude or otherwise handle
unactuated coordinates according to the plant contract.

\section{Control and Trust Region}
At a caller-selected time, linearly interpolated reference samples and fixed
diagonal gains give
\[
 u_{\rm cmd}=u_{\rm ff,ref}+\sum_i B_i(t)c_i
 +K_p(q_{\rm ref}-q)+K_d(\dot q_{\rm ref}-\dot q).
\]
The cubic basis is open and clamped. Each pair of distinct breakpoints must
be at least the declared minimum spacing apart. Coefficients $c_{ij}$ are
accepted only if $\lVert c/s\rVert_2\le r$, using a positive per-channel scale
$s$ and stated radius $r$. This is a coefficient-space trust region, not a
total-torque, work, slew, reference-tracking or anatomical constraint.
Reference rate is supplied separately; this primitive makes no reference
shift and does not prove that the supplied rate is the derivative of position.

\section{Input and Limitations}
The ideal input is $u_{\rm ideal}=\min(u_{\max},\max(u_{\min},u_{\rm cmd}))$
componentwise. Command, ideal input and saturation flags are returned. The
caller specifies the control hold; clipping takes place after all feedforward
and feedback terms are summed. A downstream plant may mask or transform these
inputs, so the ideal input is not established as post-saturation actuator or
anatomical torque. No continuous-time bound or physical acceptance follows.
For an explicitly unactuated coordinate, both limits may be set to zero;
equal nonzero limits are rejected. A nonzero command on that coordinate is
recorded as saturated before the plant receives the zero ideal input.

\section{Declared Total-Input Accounting}
For a caller-declared total applied rotational input $\tau_{kj}$ held on
$[t_k,t_{k+1})$, the accounting uses independent endpoint joint rates and
linearly interpolates each rate within that hold. The signed work sums
$\int\tau_{kj}\omega_j\,dt$ by joint. Positive work sums the positive part
per joint before aggregating, splitting a linear power sign change at zero;
generation on one joint therefore cannot cancel absorption on another.
Quadratic effort is $\sum_{kj}\tau_{kj}^2(t_{k+1}-t_k)$. The checked update
rate is $|\tau_k-\tau_{k-1}|/(t_k-t_{k-1})$, not a continuous slew bound:
zero-order-held inputs jump, and the transition before the first supplied hold
is unobserved. A single hold provides no update-rate evidence.
An audit pass covers only this declared schedule and interpolation. Units,
rotational coordinate mapping, limits and applied-input provenance are
caller assertions; neither intervening motion nor actuator or anatomical
feasibility is certified. No value is clipped by this audit.

\section{Verification}
The focused Python tests cover the total-command saturation, coefficient trust,
minimum spacing, finite inputs, cubic continuity and interaction with the
existing held-step integrator on a synthetic unit-mass plant. They do not test
an accepted reference, contact, optimization or a physical model. Run
\texttt{python -m pytest -q tests/unit/impact\_optimization/test\_option\_b\_prototype.py}
and \texttt{tests/unit/impact\_optimization/test\_total\_applied\_zoh\_audit.py}
from the repository root with the pinned Tools submodule initialized.
