\section{Scope and Input Authority}
This separate editable research reference supports issue 15494, slices B/D.
It is not the canonical engineering manual or a new allocation solver.
The calculation is implemented in
\path{scripts/research/nullspace_motion_limits.py}.
Input is a complete caller-selected angular history $q_{ij}$ at strictly
increasing times $t_i$ in seconds. Each named coordinate is in unwrapped
radians in the declared model's coordinate order and sign convention.
The audit does not convert frames, unwrap angles, infer anatomy, or choose
which arm, wrist, trunk, or lower-body coordinates must be present.
The caller declares constant lower/upper limits $l_j<u_j$ in radians,
positive speed limits $v_j$ in radians/second, a source string for these
model-specific priors, and an interior margin $d\geq0$ in radians.
The source is retained verbatim; its scientific validity is not verified.
Optional dimensionless rows $A_k$ and bounds $b_k$ in radians declare
coupled posture restrictions $A_kq\leq b_k$; neither input may be missing.
There is no subject-specific default, calibration, or clinical safety claim.
\section{Calculation and Between-Sample Meaning}
Assume $q(t)$ is piecewise linear between the supplied knots. Define
\begin{align*}
 r_{ij}&=\min(q_{ij}-l_j,u_j-q_{ij})-d,\\
 s_{ij}&=v_j-\left|\frac{q_{i+1,j}-q_{ij}}{t_{i+1}-t_i}\right|,\\
 c_{ik}&=\frac{b_k-A_kq_i}{\lVert A_k\rVert_2}-d.
\end{align*}
ROM and coupled margins are radians; speed margins are radians/second.
Normalizing the coupled rows makes their interior distance independent of
positive row rescaling. Zero rows and empty effective ROM intervals are rejected.
Every affine posture inequality attains its worst value at a segment endpoint;
segment speed is constant. Thus these checks cover the declared interpolant
between knots. They do not cover a spline, engine replay, acceleration at knots,
time-varying limits, nonlinear anatomy, or omitted coordinates.
\section{Report and Acceptance Controls}
The report retains the timebase, names, units, source, priors and interpolation.
It lists every negative margin with its sample or segment and axis index,
the per-axis minimum, and each constraint family's narrowest location.
Zero margin passes; no tolerance is silently added and no violation is clipped.
Coupled posture is null if undeclared; this is not a coupled-posture pass.
The aggregate only means that all supplied limits hold. Replay and physiological
feasibility remain explicitly false. Strict JSON contains no NaN or infinity;
invalid shapes, nonfinite channels and overflowing arithmetic raise ValueError.
\section{Reproduction and Limitations}
Run \texttt{python -m pytest} on\\
\path{tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_motion_limits.py}\\
with \texttt{-m unit -q -p no:xvfb} from the repository root after initializing
the pinned Tools submodule. The tests are synthetic algebraic controls,
not admitted golfer data: nonuniform timing gives peak speeds $(1,0.5)$;
ROM minima are $(0.5,0.25)$. A fast segment violates speed despite admissible
positions. For $A=(1,1)$ and $b=0.5$, pose $(0.2,0.5)$ violates coupled
posture despite passing individual ROM. Further controls cover boundary
margins, positive row rescaling, invalid declarations and numerical overflow.
No physical swing, measured ROM, contact mode, closure, torque capacity,
ground-load policy, forward replay or panel-ready video is qualified here.
