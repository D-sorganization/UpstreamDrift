# Hit-and-Run Sampling of the Feasible Set on a Low-Rank Temporal Basis

Research spike for NSX-70 (issue #12588, epic #12593). Status: exploratory.
Nothing here is a claim about any golfer, about which allocations occur, or
about physical plausibility.

## Question

The deterministic stack (NSX-21 feasible sets, NSX-22 and NSX-25 envelopes)
returns the extreme values of a quantity over the admissible null-space
coordinate `z(t)`. This spike asks a different question: can the admissible
set of whole trajectories `z(t)` be explored distributionally, and how well
does a plain hit-and-run chain mix when `z(t)` is restricted to a low-rank
temporal basis?

## Model

- Per frame, `x = x_p + N z` with `x = (tau, lambda, f_g)` (NSX-02 and NSX-20
  decomposition). `FeasibleSet` gives the polyhedral rows `g . z <= h` and the
  exact second-order-cone blocks `b - A z` in the cone.
- Low-rank trajectory: `z(t_i) = sum_k B[i, k] C[k, :]`, with `B` the `(T, K)`
  clamped B-spline position basis of MOSAIC (`BSplineBasis.uniform`, degree 3
  in the spike). The unknown is the coefficient matrix `C`, of dimension
  `D = K n_z`.
- Admissible set in `C`: all rows of all frames, which are linear in `C`, and
  all cone blocks, which are kept exact. A full-rank basis (`K = T`, identity)
  is the unrestricted case.
- Sampling measure: uniform on this convex set **in coefficient space**. That
  is a declared, convenient measure and not a statement about swing statistics.
  A different parametrisation gives a different measure.

## Method

Hit-and-run (Smith 1984): from a strictly interior point pick a direction
`d`, intersect the line `C + s d` with the set, and jump to a uniform point of
the chord. Rows give `s` bounds by division of slack by rate. A cone gives the
quadratic `|w_v - s u_v|^2 <= (w_t - s u_t)^2` with `w = b - A z`,
`u = A dz`, solved by closed-form roots plus the `t >= 0` half-line.

- The direction is a normalised Gaussian vector. An optional matrix
  `direction_scale` multiplies it first; the direction law stays symmetric
  under `d -> -d`, which keeps the uniform law invariant.
- Start: the maximum inscribed margin (SOCP) as centre; each chain starts 90 %
  of the way to a random boundary point (deliberately over-dispersed).
- Seeds: chain seeds are spawned from one integer with `SeedSequence`; a chain
  is a pure function of its inputs and seed.
- Code: `src/shared/python/loop_nullspace/hit_and_run.py` (sampler),
  `mixing_diagnostics.py` (autocorrelation, split R-hat, effective sample
  size), `sampled_marginals.py` (comparison against envelopes).

## Mixing Diagnostics

- Autocorrelation by FFT, biased estimator.
- Split R-hat: each chain cut in halves, Gelman and Rubin with the
  `((n-1)/n) W + B/n` variance estimate.
- Effective sample size: multi-chain, Geyer initial monotone sequence on
  paired autocorrelations, Stan floor on the integrated time.
- A passing diagnostic is evidence of non-failure only. Diagnostics are
  checked against closed-form AR(1) oracles in the tests.

## Oracle Validation

`tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_hit_and_run.py` checks the sampler on sets with
known uniform laws: the unit box, the standard 3-simplex, the unit disk through
the exact cone chord, and two frames sharing one constant coefficient (the
uniform law on the intersection of two intervals). Sample mean and covariance
match closed form within five standard errors, where the standard error comes
from the spread of 16 independent chain means and not from the module's own
effective-sample-size code. Every stored state satisfies every constraint to
`1e-9`; chains are bit-for-bit reproducible from the seed; thinning subsamples
a fixed stream; preconditioned directions still pass the box oracle.

## Spike Scenario

Fixture: planar two-arm golfer, NSX-00 generic configuration, full actuation.
Decomposition: `dim_x = 7` (five torques, two hand forces), `n_z = 2`.
The same decomposition is reused at each of 21 frames on `t in [0, 1]`; only
the **declared** symmetric torque capacity varies,
`cap(t) = 25 (1 + 0.4 sin 2 pi t)` N m on all five coordinates. This capacity
profile is invented for the spike, so each frame is a polygon in the `z` plane
whose size breathes in time. Hand-force quantities `lam_1, lam_2` are
unconstrained except through the torque rows. No friction cone is declared in
this scenario (cones are covered by the disk oracle). No anthropometric window
was used: the integration test file named in the task does not exist in this
tree.

Run: 8 chains, 1000 burn-in steps, 4000 recorded draws per chain, seed 12588.
Preconditioned runs use the Cholesky factor of the covariance of a 3000-draw
pilot chain (seed 12589), fixed before the main run.

Total draws are 32000 per row. The lag-1, lag-10 and lag-50 autocorrelations of

and 1.00, 0.97, 0.89 for the full basis.

Comparison with the static envelopes of the same per-frame sets:

- Containment: the largest excursion of any sample outside its envelope is
  `0.0` in every case (all rows listed in the JSON). This is a consistency
  check, since a feasible `z` must lie inside the envelope.
- Reach (sample range divided by envelope width, minimum over the seven
  quantities of the frame-mean): 0.967 (`K = 4`), 0.966 (`K = 6`), 0.969
  (`K = 8`), 0.986 (full). The worst single frame and quantity is 0.931,
  0.932, 0.934 and 0.949 respectively (unpreconditioned runs).
- Central reach (5th to 95th percentile range divided by envelope width) is
  0.62 to 0.80 for `K = 4` and 0.70 to 0.88 for the full basis. The samples
  touch nearly the whole envelope range but most of the mass sits inside it.
- Mean envelope widths are 49.9, 50.0, 19.6, 50.0, 27.9 N m for `tau_1` to
  `tau_5` and 246.1, 156.1 N for `lam_1, lam_2`.

## Observations

These describe the measurements above and nothing more.

- With `n_z = 2` the low-rank restriction did not visibly limit the reach:
  reach is 0.97 or more for `K = 4` as well as for the full basis. The
  per-frame regions are two-dimensional and the cubic spline with four
  coefficients still moves across them. This says nothing about larger
  `n_z`.
- Mixing degrades with dimension. At `K = 21` the minimum coefficient ESS is
  9.4 from 32000 draws and the maximum split R-hat is 1.47, so the full-basis
  run is not converged by these diagnostics. At `K = 4` to `8` R-hat stays
  below 1.14 unpreconditioned.
- Pilot-covariance preconditioning helped at `K = 4, 6, 8` (minimum ESS up by
  a factor of 19, 6.6 and 4.5) and hurt at `K = 21` (R-hat 2.05), because the
  3000-draw pilot itself had not mixed, so the covariance was wrong. This is
  a failed experiment for the full basis; no alternative was tried.
- Under a coefficient-uniform measure, the 5th to 95th percentile ranges are
  well inside the envelope. Reporting the sample range alone would overstate
  how much of the set the measure actually weighs.

## Limitations and Non-Claims

- The capacity profile and the repetition of one decomposition across frames
  are synthetic. Frame-to-frame changes in `N`, `x_p` and the closure are
  absent, so temporal coupling through the dynamics is not exercised.
- Only polyhedral torque rows are used in the planar scenario. The exact-cone
  path is tested on a disk, not on a golf contact cone.
- The measure is uniform in the spline coefficients, not in `z(t)` function
  space and not in any physical prior. Results depend on the basis.
- Diagnostics were computed from 8 chains of finite length. They can reveal
  slow mixing; they cannot establish convergence.
- Torque capacities are population priors; no scientific qualification is
  claimed and no design-manual pathway is changed.
- Product integration is out of scope.

## Reproduce

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. python3 docs/research/loop_nullspace/hit_and_run_spike.py \
    --out docs/research/loop_nullspace/hit_and_run_results.json
python3 -m pytest tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_hit_and_run.py \
    tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_mixing_diagnostics.py -n 0
```

The spike runs for about one minute on a four-core shared machine. Seeds are
fixed, so the JSON is reproducible on the same library versions.
