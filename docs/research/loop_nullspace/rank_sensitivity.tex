\section{Scope and Contract}
This is a separate editable research reference for issue \#15494, not the
engineering design manual. The research module is
\texttt{scripts.research.nullspace\_rank\_audit}; its entry point is
\texttt{audit\_rank\_history}.
It reuses the existing decomposition dimension accounting without a new solver.
Inputs are closed-loop snapshots with identical topology and ground-load
dimension, strictly increasing finite times in seconds, and at least two unique
relative tolerances in $(0,1)$. Callers must separately pin model and asset
versions, input hashes, coordinate frames, contact regime and ground-load policy.
The tool checks dimensions, not semantic equivalence of these assumptions.

\section{Numerical Method and Units}
For each supplied tolerance and snapshot, the existing accounting evaluates
$\sigma_i > r\max(\sigma_{\max},1)$ on its scaled load matrices.
Characteristic force and moment defaults are 100 N and 5 N\,m; custom scales
are recorded. Counts are dimensionless. Closure rank, unactuated load rank,
balance rank, nullity, multiplier gauge and attainable torque variation are
reported independently. Changed-frame indices identify the later sample in
an adjacent count change; tolerance-sensitive indices identify any count
disagreement across the declared grid. Raw basis coordinates are never compared.

\section{Controls and Acceptance}
The synthetic diagonal closure has rows $e_1^T$ and $\epsilon e_2^T$, with
$\epsilon=1,10^{-7},0$ at times $0,0.1,0.2$ s. For tolerances $10^{-9}$ and
$10^{-5}$, expected closure ranks are $(2,2,1)$ and $(2,1,1)$.
Full actuation keeps balance rank five. A partially actuated control makes
balance rank tolerance-sensitive. An inconsistent unactuated load is rejected
by the existing decomposition while the rank audit remains diagnostic only.
These are algebraic fixtures, not valid sampled golfer kinematics or replay.

\section{Reproduction and Limitations}
Run \texttt{python3 -m scripts.research.nullspace\_rank\_audit} for strict JSON.
Run \texttt{python3 -m pytest tests/unit/loop\_nullspace/test\_rank\_sensitivity.py}.
Tests compare custom-scale results against existing accounting and check
analytic counts, invalid input rejection and JSON serialization.
No admission, contact feasibility, continuous-time rank guarantee, physical
invariance, capacity-prior robustness or forward replay is established.
No recorded baseline, private capture or rendered evidence is included.
