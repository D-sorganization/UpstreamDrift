## Conclusion and Scope Limits

No new undisclosed numerical requirement gap was found. The material remaining work is final publication: repair standalone caption context, finish and reconcile complete render runs, build the full narrative and editable findings, review the final PDF, and perform repository delivery gates. The documented empirical and compiler limitations must remain explicit rather than silently reducing acceptance criteria.

The correlation example is a model-conditional association: at zero lean, shaft-only Straight curve1 shows positive lateral/downrange correlation for Driver and negative correlation for 7-Iron/PW. It is not a universal closed-face carry law. Matched nominal landings coexist with different noisy mean carries. Neither a narrower pattern nor an interval excluding zero establishes universally better player scoring.
