# Control Remaining Graphics Review

Reviewed all 90 PNGs through 12 contact sheets: 40 social detail images, 30 direct comparison images and 20 matrix images. Seven representative originals were also inspected. The exact file inventory, full-size selections, hashes and source-caption checks are in `review.json`. All completed-manifest output hashes remained unchanged before and after review.

No additional clipping, adjacent-tick collision, legend collision or misleading density/sample-population label was found. Social detail captions match all 20 saved configurations and supported counts; the 30 completed comparisons match their two source populations and fixed targets. Density uses every supported endpoint while disclosing 600 plotted dots. Overhead images disclose six sampled trajectories, raw aim and lateral magnification. Matrix captions identify fixed-target control, delivery-noise units, conditional scoring context and the 15 m containment radius. Shared scales apply within a figure, not between separate files.

The existing standalone SD-variable ambiguity remains in exactly 18 comparison PNGs: “SD 1° vs SD 2°” does not name shaft twist. This is already repaired and independently qualified in caption commit `c9c0fb194f`; the present v4 images are therefore not accepted as the final shareable set. The other 12 curve comparisons identify fixed shaft-twist SD explicitly.

The 40 social detail images are the intentional 20-cell selection. Original PW curve-2 control cells complete only at lean 10°, outside the social selector's lean-0/lean-5 preference; they remain visible in academic details and matrices. Six original PW curve-comparison cards are explicitly unavailable in the ledger. The separately resolved supplemental PW comparisons must remain discoverable in the final publication; their production receipt was still pending during this review.

All current-path visual work is complete. The reviewed v4 directories are safe for the parent to relocate with a complete byte/hash receipt. No production source or asset was edited. This graphical review does not repeat the independent numerical audits or establish empirical model accuracy.

The executable caption check initially encountered two harness assumptions, not production defects: overhead SVG uses a semicolon in “Raw trajectories; no aim-line rotation,” and matrix exports have no SVG companion. The corrected check uses actual social SVG text and records matrix inspection as visual rather than automated SVG validation.
