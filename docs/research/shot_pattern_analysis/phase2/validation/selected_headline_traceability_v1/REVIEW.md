This independent reading checks 24 zero-lean cells against immutable saved summaries. It does not qualify the unfinished publication or replace the full factorial results. Four PW curve-2 cells use the separately labeled face-bounds supplement; their original unavailable records remain unchanged.

All nominal patterns match the physical target within the unchanged 0.05 m criterion, with no aim rotation. The noisy downrange distributions have nonzero SD; matching the nominal landing therefore does not mean every shot travels the same distance. Correlation below uses right-positive lateral position and downrange position: positive indicates right-long/left-short association; negative indicates right-short/left-long association. These are model-conditional associations, not measured player tendencies.
