# Final Scientific Narrative Acceptance Checklist

This is a methodology checklist for the eventual complete report, not acceptance of currently incomplete datasets or the moving report builder. It supplements the software pipeline findings without modifying frozen sources or immutable source-transition evidence.

## Distance and Matching

Distinguish three comparisons. Within a club/lean cell, Straight, Draw and Fade share the same zero-error raw landing target. This removes a difference in **nominal** range as an explanation. Across the primary lean grid, targets may differ; direct spread comparisons there retain range differences. The fixed-target control compares complete rematched deliveries at the same nominal range, including changes in solved face, path and speed.

A suitable conclusion is: “The fixed-target comparison tests whether the model's pattern difference persists at a common nominal range. Random delivery errors can still change the realized carry distribution, and the comparison does not isolate a pure causal effect of curvature or shaft lean.” State whether it persists only after the actual completed results are known.

Do not subtract primary/control spread percentages or marginal standard deviations to estimate a causal fraction “explained by distance.” Report the common target, solved nominal delivery and realized downrange distribution alongside lateral spread, target bias and radial error. Any equal-range projection is a geometric diagnostic, not a separate physical flight experiment or causal decomposition. If using the exact target-MSE identity (coordinate variances plus squared biases), label it descriptive accounting and use a consistent population and variance denominator; it does not assign causes.

## Geometry Versus Player Accuracy

The rigid geometry establishes a local face/loft coupling for the stated static loft, lie and shaft orientation. Opening/closing the face through shaft rotation changes delivered loft in that construction. It does not establish a measured golfer distribution, shaft deflection, equipment calibration or universal carry response. At the zero-twist reference, the documented derivative provides the geometric mechanism; the sign and size of the carry response depend on the complete launch/spin state and club model.

Endpoint left-long/right-short quadrant frequencies do not themselves identify closed-face causation. Path, strike, speed and spin also affect lateral and longitudinal outcomes. Use the existing deterministic derivative/sensitivity evidence for mechanism, and actual endpoint distributions for what the simulation produced. Do not rename primitive shaft-twist SD 1°/2° as face-angle SD, “half the player's precision,” or an empirical accuracy estimate. Derived face/path covariance need not equal the primitive shaft/path correlation.

## Minimum Result Contract

- Cover every club, lean, curve strength and shaft-SD condition through a complete linked inventory; distinguish a selected illustrative stratum from the full factorial results. Retain original unavailable bounded searches, supplemental protocol identifiers and non-execution aliases. Unattempted outcomes are unavailable, not zero performance.
- Answer SD 1→2 and curve 1→2 with the direct paired change in curve-versus-Straight benefit, including direction, magnitude and interval. Use the six-population intersection and normalized primitive-error pairing; do not infer an interaction by subtracting separately conditioned table means or confidence limits.
- State the analysis population for every statistic. Individual supported-pattern geometry, three-population within-cell SG, and six-population between-condition contrasts can have different counts. Show common count/attempt count and exclusions. A benefit among mutually supported shots is not a benefit for all attempted deliveries when unsupported mass is nonzero.
- Separate lateral SD, downrange SD, target bias, target RMSE and SG. A narrower pattern may score worse through centering, longitudinal error or lie geometry. State which metric supports each conclusion and its desirable sign. Avoid declaring a universal “best shape” from one metric or selected cell.
- Provide absolute and paired SG context with the actual benchmark, starting lie/distance, hypothetical course dimensions, and carry-as-final-lie assumption. Driver tee and iron/wedge approach values represent different opportunities. Historical benchmark SG is not measured player handicap or validation of the illustrative club model. “Per 100 identical opportunities” must not become “per round.”
- Report practical effect sizes before statistical direction claims. Bootstrap intervals quantify Monte Carlo sampling only. Shared random draws correlate grid contrasts; cells are not independent experimental replications. Pointwise intervals and counts of favorable cells are descriptive, not simultaneous/multiplicity-adjusted discoveries. Do not call a model effect empirically proven because its Monte Carlo interval excludes zero.
- Keep course-geometry sensitivity, deterministic head/contact-law sensitivity, numerical convergence and empirical validation separate. The ±20% parameter checks are chosen sensitivity settings, not measured uncertainty intervals. Claims of robustness must name the tested settings; numerical refinement does not validate the physical assumptions.
- Describe density regions as simulated-sample highest-density regions, not confidence regions for the mean or empirical player shot zones. A 95% HDR and a 95% bootstrap confidence interval answer different questions. Keep conditional support and displayed trajectory subsampling explicit.
- Link usable academic and social density/overhead assets for all three clubs and the relevant contrasts. Captions must identify the actual condition, population and protocol. A single attractive driver preview does not qualify every final chart or its narrative.

## Final Review Gates

The narrative is ready only after independent complete data audits, qualified cross-snapshot references, paired-estimand checks, complete inventory reconciliation, and rendered figure/PDF inspection. Every headline must trace to an exact table/contrast and denominator. State remaining empirical validation explicitly (issue #11984); no measured player/equipment qualification is claimed. Any universal or causal language requires evidence beyond this conditional simulation study.
