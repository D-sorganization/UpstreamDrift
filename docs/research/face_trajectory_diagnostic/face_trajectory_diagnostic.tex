\section{Sampled Finite-Face Trajectory Diagnostic}
For a caller-supplied state advance, let $g(t)$ be the static sphere-to-patch
gap in metres. Initial $g(0)\leq0$ is rejected. The first sampled pair with
$g(t_a)>0$ and $g(t_b)\leq0$ is refined with Brent's method using the supplied
time tolerance; a candidate also needs a residual within the declared distance
tolerance, a front-side point strictly inside one triangle, and positive
material velocity projected toward the ball. The ball is fixed and all
positions and velocities are in one frame in SI units. Triangle-boundary and
shared-seam events are conservatively excluded.

These conditions do not certify initial outside-solid placement, trajectory
continuity, rigid topology, global closest approach or the first contact root.
Finite samples may miss arbitrarily narrow or tangent crossings. Both a
candidate and a miss remain unqualified with uncertified interval coverage; no
impact or flight may be inferred. The reported minimum is over inspected grid
samples only. A synthetic pinned-v2 MuJoCo advancing fixture compares two
integration steps and is not a matched-swing or physiological validation.
