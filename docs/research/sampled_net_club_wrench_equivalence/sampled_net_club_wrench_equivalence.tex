\section{Scope and Preconditions}
This separate research reference documents the comparison prerequisite of
epic 15494, slice B. It is not the governed engineering design manual.
Two allocations must already share one motion, model, timebase, contact regime
and ground-load policy. The caller supplies aligned samples; this diagnostic
does not verify those upstream conditions or interpolate missing samples.
No admitted golfer baseline or Solution Bundle from epic 15493 is supplied here.

\section{Frames, Units, and Equations}
At each sample the shared frame is world-from-grip rotation $R$ and world
origin $o$, the midpoint of distinct lead and trail grip points $p_L,p_R$.
Each hand-on-club wrench is force $f$ in N and free couple $c$ in N\,m,
expressed in grip axes at its hand point. Positions are in metres.
Using the existing canonical grasp map from \texttt{grip\_split},
\[
 w=Gy,\quad y=(f_L,c_L,f_R,c_R),\qquad
 F=f_L+f_R,\quad
 M=R^T(p_L-o)\times f_L+c_L+R^T(p_R-o)\times f_R+c_R.
\]
For candidate minus baseline, $\delta w=G(y_c-y_b)$.
The comparison passes exactly when every supplied sample satisfies
$\|\delta F\|_2\leq\epsilon_F$ and
$\|\delta M\|_2\leq\epsilon_M$.
The caller declares finite non-negative absolute tolerances separately in
N and N\,m. There is no mixed-unit norm or default tolerance.

\section{Evidence and Negative Controls}
\texttt{compare\_hand\_wrench\_histories} returns a JSON-compatible diagnostic
with sample times, conventions, tolerances, both residual series, their maxima,
and failed sample indices. Missing or non-finite channels, inconsistent shapes,
unordered times and invalid geometry are errors, never implicit zero loads.
The tests construct two distinct splits through the existing canonical
\texttt{hand\_wrenches} map on moving and rotating geometry.
An independent negative control applies opposite transverse forces of 3 N
at points separated by 0.2 m: net force is unchanged but the moment changes
by 0.6 N\,m. Pure axial-force and free-couple perturbations check dimensional
tolerances separately. These are analytic structural fixtures, not recordings.

\section{Limitations and Reproduction}
Agreement of sampled net wrenches is a necessary comparison gate only.
It does not establish joint-torque balance, closure, contact admissibility,
capacity, uncertainty, between-knot feasibility, or forward replay.
A passing sample comparison is neither an across-family invariant nor proof
that these allocations produce the same motion. Both feasibility and replay
verification flags remain false; later qualification must supply that evidence.
No inference of co-contraction, stiffness, clinical safety or golfer optimality
is made. Run from the repository root with its pinned Tools tree initialized:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -m pytest tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_allocation_comparison.py
python3 -m pytest tests/unit/loop_nullspace/test_grip_split.py
\end{verbatim}
