\section{Scope and Assumptions}
This standalone research reference specifies the deterministic, small-sample
perturbation audit for the moving-base, two-hand, flexible-club model tier. It
does not establish population uncertainty, physiological validity, or a human
forward-dynamics match. The study uses 24 global parameter samples, six held-out
samples, a 0.24\,s horizon, and a 0.004\,s step. Physical parameter units and
bounds, output units, actuator definitions, and the model's coordinate frames
are defined in \texttt{data/uncertainty\_control\_study.json} and its producer
\texttt{scripts/research/proximal\_distal\_energy/run\_uncertainty\_control\_study.py}.
The audit does not transform frames or units; its standardized sensitivity
matrix and rank fractions are dimensionless.

\section{Inputs and Provenance}
The primary JSON and NPZ array artifact are immutable inputs to this audit.
Before computation, their canonical SHA-256 digests and byte lengths must equal
their entries in \texttt{release\_manifest.json}. The manifest's canonical text
hashing normalizes CRLF to LF, while NPZ bytes are hashed without conversion.
The audit separately pins the published primary JSON to SHA-256
\texttt{101b9be2bccf705af9e856364312f797f0c725fab521b0c7357830e9e778715b}
and 18,917 canonical bytes, and the NPZ to SHA-256
\texttt{b051d567fcf7d0c03060f058887b2342ca46a85e78a3f1e5108d1c469ed6be9d}
and 16,911 bytes. These fixed identities remain authoritative if a later
release-inventory refresh changes manifest entries. The binding rejects any
change to the underlying sensitivity matrix even when the effective ranks
happen to be unchanged. The primary JSON contains the six
singular values saved by the original study producer. The committed stability
JSON is historical output and is never regenerated solely to accommodate a
different linear-algebra runtime.

\section{Numerical Method}
For each output metric, the audit computes partial rank correlations (PRCCs)
against the global physical design. It repeats the calculation after deleting
each of the 24 samples, then records the absolute-PRCC leader frequency and
sign reversals. For each held-out candidate program, it minimizes the vector
of five objectives: negative tenth-percentile delivery speed, mean face-path
error, ninetieth-percentile hand force, mean squared-torque effort, and
delivery-speed sample standard deviation. Pareto membership is recomputed after
deleting each of the six held-out samples.

Let $S$ be the standardized sensitivity matrix, and let
$\sigma_1\geq\cdots\geq\sigma_6$ be its singular values. The threshold rank is
\begin{equation}
 r_{\tau}=\sum_{i=1}^{6}\mathbf{1}\{\sigma_i>\tau\sigma_1\},
 \qquad \tau\in\{0.01,0.05,0.10,0.20\}.
\end{equation}
The audit reports the exact published singular values from the hash-bound
primary study JSON. It independently computes singular values from the NPZ
matrix and requires the four derived ranks to agree. This rank comparison
checks the scientific conclusion without demanding bit-for-bit agreement in
last-place singular-value digits across BLAS implementations.

\section{Acceptance and Limits}
Acceptance requires valid input hashes and lengths, agreement of all four
threshold ranks, and exact equality between the rebuilt audit and the committed
\texttt{data/uncertainty\_control\_stability\_audit.json}. On the registered
study, the ranks at 1\%, 5\%, 10\%, and 20\% are 6, 6, 6, and 5.
The six singular values identify only six observable directions among twelve
coupled parameters. The leave-one-out and jackknife results diagnose local
sample sensitivity; they are not confidence intervals or external validation.

not to overwrite the historical audit during verification.
