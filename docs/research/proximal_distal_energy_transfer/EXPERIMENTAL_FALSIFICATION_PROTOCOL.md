# Experimental Falsification Protocol

## Status and Scope

Protocol `proximal-distal-human-falsification-v1` is frozen before access to
human outcomes. The machine-readable authority is
[`data/experimental_protocol_v1.json`](data/experimental_protocol_v1.json).
The committed dry run is synthetic and qualifies only the intake, provenance,
participant-split, and fail-closed contracts. It is not human evidence.

## Primary Questions

The protocol tests registered signs, event ordering, work partitions, and
timing intervals. It does not treat trajectory fit, kinematic peak order, force
magnitude, or skill-group separation as confirmation of a transfer mechanism.

1. **EXP-H1 — Drift-Work Sign.** Is the participant-level late-window distal
   drift-work estimate positive after inverse-dynamics and measurement
   uncertainty are propagated?
2. **EXP-H2 — Geometry Response.** Does transported wrench power cross zero in
   the registered force-velocity projection interval, with force magnitude
   alone failing to explain the crossing?
3. **EXP-H3 — Negative-Couple Interval.** Is a negative equivalent club couple
   observable beyond timing and inverse-dynamics uncertainty after measured
   active and sensitivity-bounded passive contributions are inventoried?
4. **EXP-H4 — Activation Timing.** When EMG is available, does opposing distal
   activation precede the modeled reversal by more than synchronization
   uncertainty and predict held-out delivery outcomes?

EXP-H3 is observational and cannot prove muscular passivity. EXP-H4 is
observational and cannot prove that a neural strategy caused an outcome.

## Acquisition Contract

The primary H1--H3 analysis requires synchronized full-body kinematics,
bilateral hand wrenches, club kinematics, and ground reaction. Launch-monitor
delivery and surface EMG are additionally required for H4. Minimum sampling
rates, SI units, and maximum synchronization uncertainties are fixed in the
manifest. Each stream must carry a source SHA-256, coordinate frame, reference
point, calibration record, and acquisition-system identity.

The governed data authority must remain outside the public repository. Public
evidence bundles contain pseudonyms, aggregates, residual summaries, and
approved derived arrays only. Names, contact details, birth dates, medical
identifiers, and identity inferred from filename, directory, session, row
order, club, or device are prohibited.

## Processing Contract

- The laboratory frame is right-handed and SI-valued. Club and wrench
  reference transforms are measured and retained.
- The primary low-pass filter is selected before held-out outcomes are opened.
  Residual-analysis and ±20% cutoff variants are mandatory sensitivities.
- No primary-window force or wrench value is imputed. Short kinematic gaps are
  bounded at 10 ms and reported.
- Force residual above 10% body weight, or moment residual above 5% body
  weight-times-height, fails the primary inverse-dynamics gate.
- Event uncertainty combines device synchronization, resampling, and event
  localization. A predicted ordering smaller than that interval is
  inconclusive, not supported.
- Filtering, coordinate calibration, segment inertias, joint centers, contact
  reference, and residual thresholds are varied in the declared sensitivity
  analysis without selecting a favorable variant post hoc.

## Held-Out and Hierarchical Analysis

The split unit is the participant, never the swing. At least 25% of eligible
participants remain held out. The split is generated by governed random
assignment and frozen before model fitting or threshold selection. Training
swings may estimate nuisance quantities and hierarchical priors; held-out
participants evaluate the fixed predictions and equivalence regions.

Repeated swings are nested within participants. Participant-level contrasts
are the primary units; hierarchical partial pooling reports within-participant
and between-participant uncertainty without pretending swings are independent.
Skill can be used only when supplied as a governed declared variable. It must
never be inferred from speed, filename, row order, session, or club label.

## Negative Controls and Decision Rules

- Properly rotate wrench and twist together and transport both to a second
  reference point; power must remain invariant within uncertainty.
- Shuffle event timing within the admissible noncausal control window.
- Replace bilateral forces by net wrench only and confirm that the registered
  individual-hand null mode becomes non-identifiable.
- Apply sign-reversing geometry controls only to matched synthetic or model
  states; do not describe them as human interventions.
- Report all registered outcomes as supported, contradicted, inconclusive, or
  untested. Residual failure, missing required modality, or timing ambiguity
  produces inconclusive/untested status rather than silent exclusion.

## Data Needed Before Evaluation

Human evaluation remains unexecuted until the following are supplied through a
governed private authority:

- ethics/oversight reference and consent/reuse basis;
- pseudonym map held by the data steward, not in the repository;
- synchronized source streams meeting the modality contract;
- calibration, filtering, event, missingness, and quality-control records;
- frozen participant split and analysis-release authorization.

### Current Acquisition Boundary

As recorded in UpstreamDrift
[#8556](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/8556), the
participant-held-out registration, null test, adverse-load test, required
sensitivities, and fail-closed validators are implemented. The local inventory
and public-data search did not identify a governed participant-level deposit
that contains the required synchronized bilateral six-axis grip wrenches with
the remaining modalities and provenance. Instrumented-grip publications by
[Choi and Park](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/sensors/sensors-20-03672/article_deploy/sensors-20-03672-v2.pdf)
and [Koike](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/cpa/article/download/6828/6125)
are acquisition references, not qualifying data authorities. Therefore #8556
remains open and all human outcomes remain untested. Synthetic traces, digitized
publication figures, and aggregate paper results must not be substituted.

The executable validator rejects identity-bearing records, unit drift, missing
hashes, inadequate sampling, excessive synchronization uncertainty, participant
split overlap, and a public-repository data authority.
