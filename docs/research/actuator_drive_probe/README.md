# MuJoCo Actuator Drive Research Probe

This experimental seam compiles a copy of the existing plant MJCF with caller-supplied memoryless joint motors. For each explicit `(q, v, control)` it obtains MuJoCo `qfrc_actuator` after control and force limits and transmits that generalized force exactly once to the existing constrained KKT solve. It rejects a missing motor set, stateful actuator definitions, nonfinite inputs, shape mismatch, and force applied to a passive shaft coordinate.

The committed v2 model compiles with zero native actuators; this probe adds no actuators to the specification and provides no clinical limits, transmission parameters, or source provenance. Unit gears and ±1 limits in the tests are synthetic. The seam is a point-evaluation experiment, not a rollout, anatomical admission, complete actuator-state replay, or a production-ready hardware model. It does not authenticate caller-provided motor definitions. External loads remain the existing plant's independent forces.

Run headlessly from the repository root: `python -m pytest -q tests/unit/motion_matching/same_input/test_mujoco_actuator_probe.py`. See [calculation note](actuator_drive_probe.tex).

Fixed-hold research rollout now records a distinct `mujoco_post_saturation_generalized_motor` label for every accepted RK4 stage. The held vector contains motor controls in explicitly mapped coordinate slots, not generalized torque; `applied_drive` contains MuJoCo's measured post-saturation generalized force. The no-impact, no-projection prototype rejects mismatched evidence semantics and mapping. A synthetic zero-state, two-step test uses 0.1 microsecond steps and 16 RK4 stages; its artificial motors and limits are not physiological. A 1 ms attempt diverged. This verifies a small numerical plumbing path, not a matched swing, stable long rollout, physical feasibility, contact, or continuous-time bounds.

The typed numeric research archive can preserve the distinct motor-stage label and rejects a label mismatch when the expected semantics are supplied. The existing v5/v6 ideal-drive envelopes reject motor traces rather than reclassifying them. A force-only verifier hashes the exact supplied archive, model specification bytes, and motor XML against separately trusted digests, constructs a fresh MuJoCo motor model from those bytes, and compares post-saturation generalized force at every recorded numerical stage state. Tampered force arrays fail even when their archive digest is recomputed for the test. This is a same-runtime re-evaluation of saved states and commanded controls, not deterministic forward replay, a proof of continuous extrema, or a verified physiological actuator model; the caller must establish the provenance of trusted hashes and the motor parameters.
