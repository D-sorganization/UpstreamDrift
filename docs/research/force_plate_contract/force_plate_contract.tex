\section{Model Topology and Assumptions}

The record is implemented in \path{src/shared/python/force_plates/}
(\path{contracts.py}, \path{placement.py}, \path{conversion.py}). It describes
two rigid, level plates of vertical load cells, one under each foot. Per foot
it observes the normal load $N$ and the centre of pressure (CoP) of that
load. It does \emph{not} observe shear $F_x, F_y$ or the free moment about the
vertical, and these are never reported as zero.

\section{Frames and Units}

\paragraph{Stance Frame.} Metres, viewed from above. The origin is midway
between the plate centres, $+x$ points from the left plate toward the right
plate, $+y$ toward the ball, and $+z$ up. The golfer faces $+y$, so the left
plate is under the left foot for either handedness; handedness only decides
which foot leads (right-handed: left leads). Loads are exerted by the ground
on the foot, positive upward, in N. Times are in seconds.

\paragraph{World Placement.} A \texttt{PlatePlacement} maps a stance point to
the world as $p = o + R\,(x, y, 0)^\top$. $R$ must be orthonormal, proper and
keep $+z$ fixed (a yaw), all to an \emph{absolute} tolerance of $10^{-9}$ with
no relative tolerance. A tilt would make the measured load non-vertical, a
reflection would silently swap the feet, and a scale would stretch stance
distances. $o_z$ is the height of the plate top surface. OF-03 derives $R$
from a model's declared target frame.

\section{Data Validity}

Checked on construction, with each failure raising an error that names the
field:
\begin{itemize}
  \item times finite and strictly increasing; every series has the same
        length;
  \item loads finite, and not below $-3\,\sigma_{\mathrm{floor}}$. An unloaded
        plate reads slightly negative from noise; the value is kept
        unclamped, because clamping biases it;
  \item total load within the declared capacity;
  \item a foot's CoP is NaN exactly where its load is below the source CoP
        threshold, with both components NaN together;
  \item the net CoP equals the load-weighted CoP of the feet above the
        threshold, $c = \sum_k N_k c_k / \sum_k N_k$, within $0.1$~mm, and
        is NaN where neither foot is above it;
  \item force events stay in the source's own names. They are force events,
        not swing positions.
\end{itemize}

\section{Uncertainty Model}

Declared, with no defaults (\texttt{PlateUncertainty}). The 1-sigma load
uncertainty is $\sigma_N = \operatorname{hypot}(\sigma_{\mathrm{floor}},
\phi N)$; the CoP uncertainty is $\sigma_c$ per horizontal axis; the
sensing-plane depth uncertainty is $\sigma_h$; and \texttt{source} names
where the numbers come from. Provenance records the device, source format,
pinned source revision, file digests, calibration id and qualification. A
missing qualification means the trial is unqualified measurement.

\section{Observed Ground-Reaction View}

\texttt{to\_ground\_reaction\_series(trial, placement)} fills the shared
\texttt{GroundReactionSeries}. Each foot force is $(\mathrm{NaN}, \mathrm{NaN},
N)$ and the net is $(\mathrm{NaN}, \mathrm{NaN}, N_\ell + N_r)$. The CoP is
placed on the plate surface. Free moment, both moments about the centre of
mass, and the centre of mass itself are NaN, each in its own array. Contact
is true where a foot's load reaches the CoP threshold, and for the net where
either foot does. An explicit observability record (only $F_z$ and CoP
observed) travels with the series, so plots and comparisons show only
observed components.

\section{Acceptance Evidence}

Unit tests in \path{tests/unit/force_plates/test_contracts.py} and
\path{test_placement_and_series.py} cover each rule above: hand-computed
corner and centre point loads, the threshold and net-CoP rules, refusal of a
tilted, mirrored, scaled or nearly scaled rotation, NaN shear and moments in
independent arrays, and the package boundary that excludes engines, GUI
toolkits and the AGPL-licensed source.

\section{Limitations}

The record cannot recover shear or free moment. The surface height and the
plates' placement in a capture volume are declared, not estimated. Nothing
here is evidence until OF-HW qualifies a device.
