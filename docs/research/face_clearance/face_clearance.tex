\section{Static Finite-Face Clearance}
For finite triangles $T_i$ and sphere centre $c$, in the same SI frame, the
instantaneous gap is $g=\min_i\min_{p\in T_i}\lVert c-p\rVert-r$ with radius
$r>0$. The implementation uses the Voronoi-region point--triangle method
extracted from the experimental contact-event work. Empty, nonfinite, nearly
degenerate or insufficiently resolved geometry fails closed. The initial guard
requires $g>m$, where the caller supplies a nonnegative margin $m$; zero is a
numerical convention, not a measured tolerance.

This is distance to an open surface, not signed distance to a closed solid.
A positive gap is not evidence of initial exterior placement, front-side
geometry, approach, a bracketed first root, or physical feasibility. No time
interval is searched or certified. An overlapped initial state and any
unexamined interval cannot be admitted as contact. Tests cover analytic
interior, edge and vertex cases, independent constrained minimization, a
synthetic overlap against the pinned v2 rendered face and invalid geometry.
