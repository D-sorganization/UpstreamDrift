\section{Scope and Convention}
Issue 15821, parent 15814. This record is separate from the engineering
manual. The selected published priors and conflicting reports are in
\texttt{player-models.tex}. Neither golfer's segment anatomy was measured.
We use an explicit construction convention: world-$z$ head-skin maximum
minus the lowest procedural shoe-sole vertex at all-zero joint coordinates.
Hair, cap and club are excluded. Shoes are included, so matching this span
does not establish measured barefoot stature or a two-foot contact pose.
\section{Bounded Construction}
For target stature $h$ and body mass $m$, retain the de Leva thigh and shank
lengths $L_i(h,m)$. Compose the existing anthropometric model with trunk
scale $s$, exact subject-mass matching and those explicit leg lengths.
Let $T_b(s)$ be each body's zero-pose transform and $V_b$ its relevant mesh.
The shared-geometry measurement is
\[
 H(s)=\max_{v\in V_{\rm head}}[T_{\rm head}(s)v]_z
      -\min_{b\in\rm soles,\ v\in V_b}[T_b(s)v]_z.
\]
Solve $H(s)-h=0$ with a bracketed Brent solve, $0.6\leq s\leq1.2$,
parameter tolerance $10^{-7}$ and at most 32 iterations. Invalid inputs,
unreachable brackets or a finalized residual above 0.1 mm fail explicitly.
Bounds are modeling guardrails, not a statistical anatomical interval.
Apply the current grip, wrist, body and flexible-shaft revisions; recalculate
mass properties through the existing builder policy. No custom inertia
scaling is introduced. The appearance-only export never invokes this build.
\section{Evidence and Interpretation}
Tiger's solution is $s=0.9039311964$, $H=1.8542$ m; Hogan's is
$s=0.8945047692$, $H=1.7272$ m. Uncalibrated spans were 1.91593 m and
1.79034 m respectively. Tests independently compile emitted native MJCF
and transform actual head/sole mesh vertices: both pass within 1 mm.
They also check subject mass and both legs' actual hip--knee and knee--ankle
distances. The spec's \texttt{anthropometry.segments} remains its reference
table; the calibration receipt records the selected resolved leg priors.
These calculations change physical torso geometry and invalidate old motion
fits. Preview poses must be regenerated. They do not establish anatomical
fidelity, dynamics qualification, exact facial likeness or historical motion.
Panel \texttt{th\_dc9214ce804e} unanimously supported bounded torso calibration
with independent native verification and exact skin-only physics preservation.
