\section*{2. Camera and Anthropometric Identifiability}
For a world point $X$, the canonical pinhole projection uses $X_c=RX+t$ followed by intrinsic projection and homogeneous division. The orientation experiment holds the camera center $C=-R^Tt$, intrinsics, model, saved coordinates and complete Hermite curve fixed:
\[
 R'=Q(\delta)R,\qquad t'=-R'C,\qquad \delta\in\{-2^\circ,+2^\circ\}.
\]
The declared axes are camera X and Y. This is conditional sensitivity, not an optimized camera search or a confidence interval. References are unchanged saved cameras. Fresh image metrics were independently recomputed; inherited scalar-bound and contact certificates were not recertified by the camera-only audit.

All perturbations worsen body RMS at fixed poses. Shaft preferences differ among training, seen and diagnostic buckets; the Hogan frame-200 angular mismatch remains approximately $85.6^\circ$. These findings do not establish the saved camera as physically correct: pose, scale, morphology and camera must eventually be assessed together. Earlier fixed-pose focal perturbations also worsened body RMS.

A monocular scale gauge remains: replacing $(X,t)$ with $(sX,st)$ leaves homogeneous image projection unchanged for positive $s$. Known source scale, credible anthropometric priors or additional calibrated views are needed to separate world scale from camera distance. Segment ratios and orientation may also trade off against perspective. Image alignment alone cannot determine body mass.

The saved variants use population stature/mass inputs of 1.71 m and 78 kg, arm factor 1.1 and rebuilt trunk factor 1.15. Those inputs do not measure standing FK height or verified subject mass. The saved \code{anthropometric_geometry} construction rebuilds trunk geometry; the separate \code{anthropometric_candidate} helper retains supplied trunk/leg joint lengths. Their limitations must not be conflated.

The official 2005 PGA Tour guide lists Tiger as 6-2 / 180; a dated 2009 fact sheet repeats those entries. His current official profile reports 6 ft 1 in / 185 lb. These inconsistent contextual records are not year-2000 measurements or an empirical uncertainty distribution. Hogan's Hall of Fame narrative gives 5 ft 7 in / 140 pounds in early-career context, not a measurement at the footage date. No value is adopted automatically. A source-only sum of all 30 saved solids is 79.3584924 kg, including 0.313 kg of explicitly named club components; subtracting those components does not verify human subject mass.

\section*{3. Shaft Imaging and Human Motion Constraints}
A rolling-row hypothesis evaluates a moving model at
\[
 t(y)=t_{\mathrm{PTS}}+d\tau\left(\frac{y}{H-1}-\frac12\right),
\]
where $H$ is the full encoded raster height, $d$ is the authored readout direction and $\tau$ is the authored readout duration. Source-row coordinates must not be replaced by cropped display rows. Zero readout must exactly recover the global-shutter projection. Unsupported temporal endpoints and missing observations require explicit abstention.

Neither $d$ nor $\tau$ is measured here. Frame PTS does not identify exposure timing, physical playback speed, capture sensor readout, telecine/interlace history or later retiming. Shaft flexure, motion blur and occlusion may also explain apparent shaft geometry. A row-time fit must not be interpreted as identified rolling-shutter correction without additional capture evidence. The completed study produced only modest shaft changes in supported cases and does not resolve the current large mismatches. The method follows a hypothesis-testing approach consistent with rolling-shutter calibration literature [1]; it does not retrofit a measured sensor model.

The next local diagnostic uses the canonical shaft reference points and public native marker derivatives. For $d_w=(X_b-X_a)/\ell$,
\[
 \frac{\partial d_w}{\partial q}=
 \frac{I-d_wd_w^T}{\ell}\left(J_b-J_a\right).
\]
Image derivatives chain through the canonical camera projection Jacobian. Six grip-closure rows are selected by semantic position/rotation labels. Raw position residuals remain metres and rotation-log residuals radians; dimensionless weighted conditioning is reported separately using the saved scales. Finite differences at $10^{-5},10^{-6},10^{-7}$ rad must respect authored bounds. Locked-coordinate probes are off-curve model sensitivities. Projected degeneracy and unavailable capabilities remain null; principal-log behavior near $\pi$ must be flagged. Eighteen synthetic checks pass, but no historical twelve-pose diagnostic has yet run.

Scalar limits cannot be copied directly into anatomical claims. The forearm primitive uses local Rz; wrist primitives use Rx/Rz even where a name ends in Y. Shoulder Euler coordinates are not automatically clinical elevation, plane of elevation and axial rotation. The model attaches the club through the left wrist and imposes continuous right-hand closure; observed holding-hand contact and release remain hypotheses.

The ISB recommendations define anatomical coordinate conventions [2]. As an engineering inference, adopting physiological limits requires verified landmarks, reference frames, signs and a coordinate mapping before numerical envelopes can be applied. Pose-dependent limit research [3] further shows why independent scalar intervals can admit implausible configurations. Its learned prior is not a clinically validated envelope for either historical player. A suitable provider must retain versioned evidence, coupled domains, validity conditions and clear unsupported states; no new limits are inserted or relaxed in this checkpoint.

\section*{4. Repeatable Execution and Turnover}
Each experiment declares a fixed cohort, original starting curves, exact identities for the source, capture, model, camera and observations, objective options, output identities and failure behavior before native loading. All source, runtime, library, project, configuration, input and helper facets are authenticated. SDK loading follows full source admission and precedes repository/native facades. Public providers own FK, projection, grip constraints and rendering; external controllers must not implement alternative versions.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Freeze SDK-free preparation with failing-first contract tests. Keep execution disabled until the exact implementation, inputs and command are reviewed.
\item Require a clean published producer and complete writer-closed file maps. Use fresh exclusive output directories. Preserve prior failures rather than overwriting or blindly resubmitting them.
\item Load one canonical binding per candidate. Verify exact rational PTS, source pixel identities, native coordinate order/units and complete saved curve identity, including source and midpoint $q,\dot q,\ddot q$ where applicable.
\item Run only the declared work. A first fault stops later arms. Explicit session cleanup must preserve a primary error when cleanup also fails. A cleanup-only fault must propagate.
\item Capture every preservation facet in the finally path even after another facet fails. Retain incomplete states and actual child telemetry. Do not substitute parent waiting time for solver time or invent unavailable evaluation counts.
\item Independently assess outputs, full file-map changes and reported metrics; visually inspect exported overlays and rendered report pages. Deliver source, PDF, media and verification receipts to an exclusive ControlTower Desktop folder.
\end{enumerate}
