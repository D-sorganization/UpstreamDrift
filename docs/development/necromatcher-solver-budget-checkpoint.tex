\section{Next Stage and Qualification Limits}
The fixed row-time protocol software has 53 checked cases. Actual row-time and focal
trials have not been executed at this checkpoint. Software verification does not
establish exposure duration, rolling shutter, focal length, anatomical morphology,
hand contact state or clinical range of motion. Published model limits are authored
generic ranges, not patient or historical-player measurements.

Preregister source-supported interior shaft coverage, visible straight fragments and
abstentions before adding evidence. Preserve previously seen roles; do not select
labels from fit residuals or silently convert them into unseen validation. A future
balanced budget comparison must retain the same starts and scientific controls,
persist actual evaluation/elapsed counters through a reviewed canonical boundary,
and explicitly report any wall-limit failure.

The research candidates remain rejected and nonconverged. Infinite shaft-axis
diagnostics, finite geometry checks and generic surface proxies do not measure club
heads, physical endpoints, validated anatomy or dynamics. No issue closure, public
deployment or historical-motion completion is asserted. The earlier 9-, 12- and
