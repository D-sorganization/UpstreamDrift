\section{Scope and Evidence}
This separate editable research reference accompanies epic 15832 and issue
15849. Five visual appearances are applied to both the prior sized candidates
and the common reference model. Every physical JSON byte is retained.
Seve follows the R\&A compilation close-up at 121.5 s (event date unknown).
Jack follows the circa-1980 Lucas portrait and 1986 Masters profile.
Bobby follows the USGA instructional close-up at 578.7 s, with an undated
Emory portrait as supplementary evidence. Sam follows the May 1984 Lucas
portrait; its older facial appearance is combined with undated body priors.
Byron follows the 1945 Los Angeles Daily News portrait, without the old cap.
The source manifest records reference hashes and rights caveats. Source
images remain private; their availability does not authorize republication.
\section{Method and Invariants}
The shared CC0 head is deformed in its canonical frame: forward $x$, left $y$,
up $z$, in head-length units. Bounded authored fields described in
\texttt{portrait-sculpt.tex} move skin and accessories together. No field
changes the neck anchor or skull vertex. Hair silhouettes and moustache are
visual meshes with no collision or mass contribution. Optional age detail
$a\in[0,1]$ adds four closed skin-colored folds: bilateral under-eye and
nose-to-mouth strips, conformed to the skin before the common morph. Their
vertical half-width is $0.003a$ head lengths; zero omits every fold. This
control is artistic detail, not a chronological age estimate. Hair and hats are
excluded from neutral shoe-to-head stature. No anatomical fit is inferred.
For old and revised native models, acceptance requires exact equality of
body/joint/collision arrays, mass and inertia, and at each of three fixed
states, $M(q)$, bias forces, accelerations, and body transforms. Semantic
coordinates are mapped by name because native traversal order can differ.
The Necromatcher XML and appearance bindings must use the refreshed hashes.
\section{Reproduction and Limitations}
Extract the previous delivery, initialize the pinned Tools submodule, and run:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -m scripts.refresh_player_skins --root PATH/sized
\end{verbatim}
Repeat for \texttt{reference-skins}. Use the player export renderer with
\texttt{--render-existing}, the five IDs, and the extracted pose root.
Review poses retain their coordinates and are rebound to the new XML only.
Initial trials produced an excessively raised forelock and swollen lower
cheeks; lowered hair offsets and a localized chin field replace those trials.
These are stylized likeness studies, without a face scan, fine skin texture,
measured facial anatomy, expression fitting or fine reconstructed wrinkles.
Historical motion training, two-foot contact, cuff alignment and forward
simulation qualification are outside this appearance-only revision.
