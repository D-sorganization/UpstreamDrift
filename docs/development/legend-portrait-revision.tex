\section{Scope and Evidence}
Five stylized appearances retain byte-identical sized/reference physical JSON.
Sources: Seve, The R\&A close-up at 121.5 s (event undated); Jack, circa-1980 Lucas portrait and 1986 Masters profile;
Bobby, USGA instructional close-up at 578.7 s and undated Emory portrait; Sam,
May 1984 Lucas portrait with undated body priors; Byron, 1945 Los Angeles Daily
News portrait, without the cap. The manifest records hashes/rights; source images
remain private and their availability does not authorize republication.
\section{Method and Invariants}
The shared CC0 head uses forward $x$, left $y$, up $z$ in head-length units.
Bounded fields in \texttt{portrait-sculpt.tex} deform skin and accessories together, not the
neck anchor or skull vertex. Hair/moustache meshes add no collision or mass.
Age detail $a\in[0,1]$ adds four closed skin-colored bilateral under-eye/nose-to-mouth folds
before morph; vertical half-width is $0.003a$ head lengths and zero omits them.
This is artistic detail, not an age estimate. Hair/hats are excluded from neutral
shoe-to-head stature; no anatomical fit is inferred.
Acceptance requires exact body/joint/collision arrays, mass/inertia, and exact
$M(q)$, bias, acceleration and body transforms at three fixed states. Coordinates
map by name when native traversal order differs. Height allows $10^{-7}$ m for
float32 recentering; the initial $10^{-9}$ m check failed on Seve by
$3.4239\times10^{-9}$ m. Physical/dynamics stay exact; Necromatcher bindings
use refreshed XML/appearance hashes.
\section{Reproduction and Limitations}
Extract the prior delivery, initialize the pinned Tools submodule, then run:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -m scripts.refresh_player_skins --root PATH/sized
python3 -m scripts.refresh_player_skins --root PATH/reference-skins
\end{verbatim}
Use \texttt{--render-existing} with all five IDs and pose root; keep q fixed and rebind XML hashes.
Raised forelocks and swollen cheeks in early trials were corrected with lower hair
offsets and localized chin deformation. Face scans, measured anatomy, expression fits,
fine wrinkles, motion training, contact, cuff alignment and forward dynamics remain unqualified.
