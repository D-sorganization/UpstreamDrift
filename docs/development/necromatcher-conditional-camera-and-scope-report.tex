\section*{Public Conditional Camera Method}
The existing public \texttt{initialize\_camera\_hypothesis} performs SQPNP followed by RefineLM. It receives fixed assumed camera intrinsics and world correspondences produced through the canonical native marker provider from saved poses. At least six nondegenerate positive-confidence correspondences are required. Missing and zero-weight records are excluded rather than filled; nonfinite values, inadequate geometry and invalid depth fail admission.

For native world point $X$, the camera convention is
\[
 X_c=RX+t,\qquad C=-R^{\mathsf T}t,\qquad
 \pi(X_c)=\left(f_xX_{c,x}/X_{c,z}+c_x,\;
 f_yX_{c,y}/X_{c,z}+c_y\right).
\]
The reported camera center $C$ is conditional on the current native model-world coordinate system and scale. Its units and orientation must not be interpreted as measured historical camera placement. Intrinsics $K$ are held exactly fixed and are assumed, not inferred sensor calibration.

Correspondences are selected source-major then marker-major. Tiger supplies 260 eligible pairs distributed 91/78/91 across early/middle/late training strata; Hogan supplies 286 distributed 104/91/91. The source thirds are predefined by original retained index domain. A single static camera is fitted per player; no per-third camera, grid search or best-stratum selection occurs.
\section*{Unweighted Initialization and Weighted Diagnostics}
The public camera initializer uses eligible correspondences without confidence weighting in its numerical objective. Canonical confidence $w_i>0$ selects inclusion, but does not become an SQPNP or RefineLM solver weight. The separately reported canonical body diagnostic uses a confidence-weighted point RMS:
\[
 \mathrm{RMS}_{\mathrm{body}}=
 \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal I}w_i
 \lVert\pi(RX_i+t)-u_i\rVert_2^2}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal I}w_i}}.
\]
The denominator is the sum of point weights, not twice that sum. Consequently this unweighted pose initialization is not guaranteed to reduce the weighted reported metric. The evaluation preserves the same positive rows and weight denominators between camera states. Body, shaft training, shaft seen evaluation and newly reviewed diagnostic-seen metrics remain distinct scientific quantities.

Confidence and localization uncertainty are authored and uncalibrated. A small numerical difference is not an uncertainty interval or statistical significance claim. No uncertainty propagation, clinical ROM inference or physical-time calibration is provided by this experiment.
