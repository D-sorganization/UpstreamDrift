\section{Definitions}
Eye point $e = R_{h} o_{\mathrm{eye}} + t_{h}$ with $o_{\mathrm{eye}} = (0.09, 0, 0.128)$~m
(head frame: $+x$ anterior, $+y$ left, $+z$ up, origin at the neck joint).
Gaze direction $g = R_{h} a$, with $a$ the head gaze axis calibrated at address as the
head-frame direction of the line of sight to the ball. Gaze error
$\theta_{\mathrm{gaze}} = \angle(g,\, b - e)$.
Ball at address $b = c + r\hat n$ with $r = 0.021335$~m, lowered so that $b_z = z_{\mathrm{ground}} + r$.
\section{Schedule}
The target direction is $(b-e)/\lVert b-e\rVert$ until $t_i + t_{\mathrm{hold}}$
($t_{\mathrm{hold}} = 0.03$~s), then a great-circle blend to the target-line direction
$\hat x_t$ with fraction $s(u) = 10u^3 - 15u^4 + 6u^5$,
$u = (t - t_i - t_{\mathrm{hold}})/T_{\mathrm{rel}}$, $T_{\mathrm{rel}} = 0.35$~s.
The blend is $C^2$ at both ends. The impact time $t_i$ is the lowest clubhead point within
0.05~s of the peak clubhead speed.
\section{Neck Inverse Kinematics}
$\min_q \lVert R_{p} R_x(q_1) R_y(q_2) R_z(q_3)\, a - d \rVert^2 + \epsilon \lVert q - q_0 \rVert^2$
subject to the neck ranges $\pm 45^\circ, \pm 60^\circ, \pm 80^\circ$; clamping is reported.
\section{Soft Residual}
Axis row $\sqrt{w}\,(R_{h} a - d_k)$ per frame in the matching inverse kinematics.
Weight $w = 0$ (default) is marker faithful; $w > 0$ is a prior, never measured data.
\section{Gaze Weight Selection}
A sweep point $(w, m_w, \gamma_w, f_w)$ holds the marker RMS $m_w$, the
address-to-impact $\theta_{\mathrm{gaze}}$ RMS $\gamma_w$ and the face-fit RMS $f_w$.
It is feasible when $m_w \le 1.1\, m_0$ and $f_w \le 5^\circ$. The knee of the feasible
Pareto front maximises $1 - \tilde m - \tilde\gamma$, with both quantities normalised to
$[0, 1]$ over the front. The reporting weight is the smallest per-capture knee among the
weights feasible in every capture. On the capture-A driver and capture-B iron sweeps
(MuJoCo matching inverse kinematics, reference trajectory) both knees are $w = 0.1$, and
the common feasible set is $\{0, 0.1, 0.2\}$. At $w = 0.1$, $\gamma$ falls from
20.2$^\circ$ to 8.5$^\circ$ (driver) and from 18.6$^\circ$ to 6.1$^\circ$ (iron), for
0.6 and 1.3~mm of marker RMS. Qualified receipts keep $w = 0$. The table and the
reproduction commands are in \texttt{HEAD\_GAZE\_REFERENCE.md}.
\section{Forward-Dynamics Neck Tracking}
With \texttt{--fd-neck gaze}, after the feasibility filters, each frame of the tracked
reference solves
$\min_{x} \lVert R_h(q_k; x)\, a - d_k \rVert^2 + \epsilon^2 \lVert x - q_{k,\mathrm{neck}} \rVert^2$,
$\epsilon = 0.01$, inside the neck ranges, with $R_h$ from the model's own forward
kinematics and $d_k$ the scheduled direction from the eye point (evaluated twice, the
second time from the solved neck). The neck columns are low-passed at the tracking cutoff
and clipped into range. The computed-torque replay then tracks this neck; head markers do
not drive it, and it is never measured data. Replay schedule error RMS from address to
impact falls from 20.3$^\circ$ to 1.8$^\circ$ (driver) and from 19.0$^\circ$ to
1.2$^\circ$ (7-iron). Through the release it is 15.4$^\circ$ and 6.3$^\circ$, because the
neck reference is open loop and the replay's torso error after impact reaches the head.
The neck yaw saturates at $\pm 80^\circ$ on 213 and 114 of 654 frames. Head-marker RMS
doubles. Published head rotation at the top of the backswing is
$-33.6 \pm 9.1^\circ$ (driver) and $-25.4 \pm 11.1^\circ$ (7-iron) for 12 female
professionals (Zhang, Liu and Liu 2026, doi:10.3389/fspor.2026.1867187), so tour heads
turn with the torso. The table, evidence file and commands are in
\texttt{HEAD\_GAZE\_REFERENCE.md}.
