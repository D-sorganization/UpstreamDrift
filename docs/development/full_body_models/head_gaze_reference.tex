\section{Definitions}
Eye point $e = R_{h} o_{\mathrm{eye}} + t_{h}$ with $o_{\mathrm{eye}} = (0.09, 0, 0.128)$~m
(head frame: $+x$ anterior, $+y$ left, $+z$ up, origin at the neck joint).
Gaze direction $g = R_{h} a$, with $a$ the head gaze axis calibrated at address as the
head-frame direction of the line of sight to the ball. Gaze error
$\theta_{\mathrm{gaze}} = \angle(g,\, b - e)$.
Ball at address $b = c + r\hat n$ with $r = 0.021335$~m, lowered so that $b_z = z_{\mathrm{ground}} + r$.
\section{Schedule}
The target direction is $(b-e)/\lVert b-e\rVert$ until $t_i + t_{\mathrm{hold}}$
($t_{\mathrm{hold}} = 0.03$~s), then a great-circle blend to the target-line direction
$\hat x_t$ with fraction $s(u) = 10u^3 - 15u^4 + 6u^5$,
$u = (t - t_i - t_{\mathrm{hold}})/T_{\mathrm{rel}}$, $T_{\mathrm{rel}} = 0.35$~s.
The blend is $C^2$ at both ends. The impact time $t_i$ is the lowest clubhead point within
0.05~s of the peak clubhead speed.
\section{Neck Inverse Kinematics}
$\min_q \lVert R_{p} R_x(q_1) R_y(q_2) R_z(q_3)\, a - d \rVert^2 + \epsilon \lVert q - q_0 \rVert^2$
subject to the neck ranges $\pm 45^\circ, \pm 60^\circ, \pm 80^\circ$; clamping is reported.
\section{Soft Residual}
Axis row $\sqrt{w}\,(R_{h} a - d_k)$ per frame in the matching inverse kinematics.
Weight $w = 0$ (default) is marker faithful; $w > 0$ is a prior, never measured data.
\section{Results and Limitations}
See \texttt{HEAD\_GAZE\_REFERENCE.md} for the weight sweep on the capture-A driver
(marker RMS 28.0 to 33.8~mm; $\theta_{\mathrm{gaze}}$ RMS 21.3$^\circ$ to 0.8$^\circ$ at $w = 10$),
the failed first attempt with the nominal $+x$ axis, and the open items.
