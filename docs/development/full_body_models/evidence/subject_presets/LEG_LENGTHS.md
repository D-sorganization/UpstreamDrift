# Leg Lengths in the Tour-Average Subject Preset

The builder reads its legs from the OpenSim model file, so thigh and shank are that file's stored lengths: they do not follow `stature_m` and differ left to right. `thigh_length_m`, `shank_length_m` and `hip_half_spacing_m` (`execution/osim_leg_lengths.py`) are optional settings that state them, the same on both sides; absent, the build is unchanged. The capture has belt markers and no thigh markers, so it fixes the sum of thigh and trunk but not the split: the fit is equally good from 399 to 441 mm of thigh when the trunk gives back what the thigh gains. The preset therefore takes the 441 mm thigh of the de Leva proportions at 1.82 m as a prior and shortens the trunk to 623 mm; the 433 mm shank is the mean of the stored pair and agrees with the 422 to 425 mm between the knee and ankle markers. Refitted with these values the marker RMS is unchanged (address / to impact / swing 7.8 / 9.4 / 11.5 mm against 7.8 / 9.5 / 11.6 mm), the address knee flexion becomes 25 / 23 deg left / right from 24 / 29 deg and the hip flexion 35 / 35 deg from 33 / 36 deg. This is a gain in symmetry and proportion, not in fit. Standing at the zero pose the neck joint is 1.579 m and the de Leva vertex 1.833 m above the ground (1.587 m and 1.841 m before, by the same measure), which supersedes the standing heights and the statement about the legs in the next paragraph. The belt markers fit 110 to 118 mm above the hip joints with this thigh (158 to 167 mm before), so a belt seed has to follow it.
