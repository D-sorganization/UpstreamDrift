\section{Realistic CC0 Head for the Shared Head Slot (R7, \#12687)}

\subsection{Defect and Decision}
The procedural head (skull ellipsoid, blob features, one hair dome) reads
cartoonish. The owner chose a CC0 head mesh with short hair over a better
procedural loft. Appearance never feeds dynamics: no mass, inertia, collision or
degree of freedom is added.

\subsection{Asset and Provenance}
Source: the MakeHuman base mesh \texttt{hm08} (\texttt{makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj}),
whose header states it was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020
(copyright holders at release: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier);
\texttt{LICENSE.md} section C lists the base mesh among the CC0 assets.
\texttt{assets/head\_cc0/PROVENANCE.json} records the source URL and commit,
source sha256, author, licence text, download date and the asset sha256.
\texttt{build\_head\_asset.py} re-derives the asset deterministically
(\texttt{--check} compares content).

\subsection{Derivation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Keep \texttt{body} faces whose centroid is above the \texttt{joint-neck}
  height; snap the open neck ring to that plane and cap it with a fan: one
  closed skin shell (4307 vertices, 8610 triangles).
\item Hair: scalp faces above a hairline $y_h(z)$ (high on the forehead, above
  the ears, low at the nape) offset along area-weighted vertex normals by
  $t(y)=3\to7$\,mm, an inner shell with reversed winding and a stitched rim: a
  closed shell (390 vertices, 776 triangles).
\item Frame: MakeHuman ($y$ up, $z$ forward, $+x$ left, decimetres) to the
  canonical head frame (origin at the neck plane, $x$ forward, $y$ left, $z$
  up), divided by the neck-to-vertex height, so the skin vertex is exactly one
  head length. Stored as \texttt{int16} multiples of $10^{-4}$ head lengths.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Placement and Scale}
\texttt{build\_head\_parts(length, settings)} multiplies the unit vertices by
\texttt{length}\,$\times$\,\texttt{settings.scale} (the spec's de Leva head
segment length), then \texttt{head\_frame\_in\_body} places the frame exactly as
for the parametric head. MuJoCo, the OBJ export and \texttt{resolve\_appearance}
therefore share one code path.

\subsection{Contracts and Budgets}
Fail closed: missing provenance, a licence other than CC0-1.0, an asset hash
mismatch, a non-closed or inward shell or a head outside its envelope raise
\texttt{ValueError}. Budgets: asset $\le150$\,kB (measured 63\,kB), total
$\le12\,000$ triangles (measured 9386).

\subsection{Limitations}
Skin is one flat colour: the base mesh has no eye, lip or brow texture, so the
eyes and mouth read through geometry only. The hairline follows quad
boundaries (slightly jagged); no sideburns. Hair is short only. Evidence stills
(front, three-quarter) come from
\texttt{evidence/appearance/cc0\_head/render\_cc0\_head.py}.
