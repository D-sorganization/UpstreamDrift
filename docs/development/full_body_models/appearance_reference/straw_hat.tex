\section{Geometry and Assumptions}
The shared CC0 head frame has $x$ forward, $y$ left and $z$ up, with all
construction coordinates expressed in head lengths $L$. This visual
interpretation is inspired by a traditional straw golf hat; it is not a
measured replica or a claim about the player's anatomy. The shell is clipped
from the existing CC0 skin at $z=0.73L$ and offset by $0.075L$ along the skin
normals, with a $0.006L$ wall. Its hair crop has top thickness $0.020L$ and
no fringe; sampled outward rays check that the crown covers the cropped hair.

The circumferential brim follows the clipped crown rim in azimuth order. At
azimuth $\theta$, the outer point is $p_{\rm rim}+0.32L(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)
-0.025L\hat z$. Top and bottom faces are separated by $0.012L$, and their
inner and outer boundaries are joined to form a closed mesh. The dark band is
a closed normal-offset shell between $z=0.76L$ and $0.82L$, $0.006L$ thick.
The existing bounded visual head morph is applied identically to all parts.

The MuJoCo visual geoms carry zero mass and no collision; the physical model,
frames and joint coordinates are unchanged. Geometric tests check positive
oriented volume, closed edges, hair clearance, and existing cap identity;
native tests compare mass, inertia and dynamics at the same state. Stills at
1280 by 960 and 390 by 844 are visual evidence, not physical qualification.
