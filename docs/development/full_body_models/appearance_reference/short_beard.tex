\section*{Short Beard Visual Geometry}
The optional \texttt{short\_beard} and \texttt{dense\_short\_beard} styles
draw on the actual CC0 head skin, not on the mass or collision model. The
PGA TOUR Jon Rahm profile portrait
(\texttt{https://www.pgatour.com/player/46970/jon-rahm}, accessed
2026-11-28) shows dense dark cheek, jaw, chin and moustache coverage. It is a
current profile image, so it is only a modern-era visual reference. Both
procedural results are stylized surface approximations, not measured facial
geometry, individual hair strands or exact texture.

The head mesh uses dimensionless head-length coordinates, $x$ forward, $y$
lateral, $z$ upward. With $a=|y|$ and $t=\operatorname{clip}((a-0.09)/0.14,0,1)$,
the short stubble cheek boundary is $z<0.295+0.085t^2(3-2t)$. The dense short
beard uses a lower, more gradual cheek boundary, with an outer-cheek top at
0.345 head lengths at the rear cheek, tapering forward with the face profile.
Its upper rear edge also shifts forward as it rises to soften the sideburn
transition. The stubble boundary peaks at 0.380. The dense style's elliptical lip opening is
narrower and shorter (0.130 by 0.064 head lengths versus 0.180 by 0.090 for
stubble), leaving more of the lip area covered. The skin is locally subdivided
and clipped at the boundary, requiring
$x>0.13+0.10u^2(3-2u)$ plus an upward sideburn taper,
$u=\operatorname{clip}((a-0.20)/0.12,0,1)$,
$z>0.075$ and an elliptical lip opening; the dense style uses
$(y/0.065)^2+((z-0.27)/0.032)^2>1$. The shell's inward surface is offset
$0.0004$ head lengths along vertex normals; the outward surface adds $0.003$
for stubble or $0.004$ for the denser beard, except the $0.001$ rim. Boundary
walls and a reversed inner layer close the mesh. Two tapered moustache strips
sit above the lip; the dense style widens them and extends their lower edge to
meet the beard shell, while leaving a narrow lip opening. Stubble keeps the original opaque 75\%
skin / 25\% hair swatch. Dense beard uses a 45\% skin / 55\% hair swatch to
soften the color boundary while keeping it distinct from stubble.
Skin, moustache, beard and cap are subject to
the same optional head morph, so the ornament follows the base head. Front and
profile renders at desktop (1280x720) and phone (390x844) aspect ratios are
stored under
\texttt{docs/development/evidence/appearance/dense\_short\_beard/}.

The coarse skin triangles limit boundary fidelity and each uniform material
represents a coverage level rather than individual hair; facial hair is visual
only. Rendering must be checked
for each selected face, hair color and pose. Reproduce the renders with:
\begin{verbatim}
MUJOCO_GL=egl PYTHONPATH=.:vendor/ud-tools/src python3 docs/development/evidence/appearance/dense_short_beard/render_preview.py --out docs/development/evidence/appearance/dense_short_beard
\end{verbatim}
Reproduce the closedness, orientation, lip-clearance, schema and native physics checks with:
\begin{verbatim}
PYTHONPATH=.:vendor/ud-tools/src python -m pytest tests/unit/model_appearance/test_short_beard.py
\end{verbatim}
