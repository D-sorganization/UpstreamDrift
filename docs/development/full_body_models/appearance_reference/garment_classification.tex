\section{Garment Classification}
\label{sec:garment-classification}

\subsection{Rule}
A body name $n$ is split on ``/'' into path components $c_1, \dots, c_k$
($c_k$ is the leaf segment). Each rule $(p, \pi)$ in
\texttt{model\_appearance.library.PART\_RULES} is a lowercased \texttt{fnmatch}
pattern $p$ over \emph{one} component and an anatomical part $\pi$. The
specificity of $p$ is
\[
  s(p) = \begin{cases}
    0 & \text{no wildcard (exact segment name)},\\
    1 & \text{single trailing wildcard (prefix)},\\
    2 & \text{otherwise (substring)}.
  \end{cases}
\]
For component $c$ let $H(c) = \{(s(p), \pi) : p \text{ matches } c\}$ and
$s^\ast = \min s$ over $H(c)$. The component's part is the unique $\pi$ with
$(s^\ast, \pi) \in H(c)$; two different parts at $s^\ast$ raise
\texttt{ValueError} (no silent first match). The body's part is that of the
first component, scanning $c_k, c_{k-1}, \dots, c_1$, that matches any rule,
else ``other''. Hence the leaf segment always beats its parent subsystem, and
the result is invariant under any permutation of \texttt{PART\_RULES}.

\subsection{Root Cause of the Navy Pelvis}
The previous rule list was first-match over the whole lowercased path with
\texttt{*torso*} before \texttt{*pelvis*}/\texttt{*hips*}. The GolfSwing3D
pelvis \texttt{.../Hips and Torso Inputs/LowerTorso} therefore classified as
\emph{torso} and wore the shirt (\texttt{polo\_navy}). Its folder names both
the pelvis and the torso, so the three trunk segments are named exactly.

\subsection{Pants Rule (Presets)}
Each outfit is built from one shirt material $m_{\mathrm{top}}$ and one pants
material $m_{\mathrm{pants}}$; with $P$ the pants part (\texttt{thigh}),
\[
  \mathrm{garment}(\text{pelvis}) = \mathrm{garment}(P) = m_{\mathrm{pants}}
\]
for every preset that dresses $P$. \texttt{check\_clothing} raises on any
violation, and \texttt{check\_references} applies it to the active preset.
\begin{tabular}{lll}
  Preset & Shirt & Pants (pelvis, thigh) \\ \hline
  \texttt{golf\_polo\_shorts} (default) & \texttt{polo\_navy} & \texttt{shorts\_khaki} \\
  \texttt{golf\_polo\_trousers} & \texttt{polo\_white} & \texttt{trousers\_charcoal} (also shin) \\
\end{tabular}

The pelvis now also takes the pelvis cross-section entries of
\texttt{PART\_RADIUS\_M} and \texttt{PART\_ASPECT}, which were never reached
for the anthropometric golfer. Appearance only: physics is bit-identical.

\subsection{Owner Decision}
The default outfit stays \texttt{golf\_polo\_shorts}. Whether the default
switches to \texttt{golf\_polo\_trousers} is an open owner decision; either
way the pelvis wears the active outfit's pants, with no colour hard-coded.
