\section{Purpose and Scope}
The \texttt{flat\_cap} style is a visual accessory for the CC0 mesh head. It
adds no body, joint, collision, or physical-model geometry. The cap uses the
existing closed crown shell and peak builder, with a style-specific crop.

\section{Crown Shape}
Let $(x,y,z)$ be vertices in canonical head-length coordinates, with $+x$
pointing toward the face. The flat-cap crop shifts both surfaces of the crown
shell forward by
\begin{align}
  x' &= x + \delta\,F(x)R(z)A(z), & y'&=y, & z'&=z, \\
  \delta &= 0.12, &
  F(x)&=S\!\left(\operatorname{clip}\frac{x-c_x}{0.24}\right),\\
  R(z)&=S\!\left(\operatorname{clip}\frac{z-0.79}{0.09}\right), &
  A(z)&=S\!\left(\operatorname{clip}\frac{0.99-z}{0.07}\right),
\end{align}
where $S(t)=t^2(3-2t)$ on $[0,1]$ and $\operatorname{clip}$ clamps to that
interval. The smooth gates keep the cap line and apex fixed while adding a
small front overhang. Moving both surfaces together preserves the shell
thickness. The inner surface retains its normal stand-off from the scalp;
short-cropped hair is trimmed to remain below it.

The Hogan appearance sets the peak length to $0.12$ head lengths through the
existing \texttt{cap\_brim} setting. Other appearances may choose any supported
peak length. The crown is an approximate silhouette style, not a fitted
historical headwear reconstruction.

\section{Verification}
The crown and peak tests check closed positive-volume meshes. A ray-triangle
clearance check samples hair vertices against the crown. The native MuJoCo
front/profile stills are visual review only and make no motion or physics
qualification claim. Reproduce the focused checks from the repository root:
\begin{verbatim}

  tests/unit/model_appearance/test_head_cap.py \\
  tests/unit/model_appearance/test_schema_and_library.py \\
  tests/unit/model_appearance/test_head_mesh_asset.py -q
\end{verbatim}
