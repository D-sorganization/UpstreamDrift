\chapterpage{Fixed-Mass Body-Frame Offset Construction}
Only six declared arm segments are selected: left/right upper arms, their proximal forearm spherical solids and left/right lower forearms. Names must resolve uniquely. Shoulder hubs, trunk, legs, hands and club are not selected. The public \code{scale_segments} owner operates on independently owned copied documents.

For a selected parent body $b$ and factor $s_b$, the construction scales the distal joint translation, solid placement translation, centre of mass and native marker/contact offsets:
\begin{align}
 t'_{b\to j}&=s_b t_{b\to j}, & c'_b&=s_b c_b, & a'_b&=s_b a_b,\\
 m'_b&=m_b, & I'_b&=s_b^2 I_b, & R'&=R.
\end{align}
The child-to-follower transform and primitive rotation identities stay exact. Separate sparse fit-marker offsets are also scaled, through declared body frames or identity frame aliases. Unknown aliases or nonidentity affected sparse frames reject rather than supply guessed transforms.

Fixed mass with squared inertia scaling is a body-frame geometric approximation. It is not an anatomical mass redistribution, a fitted density field or a mechanically validated dynamic model. No torque, force, acceleration-to-load inference or replay was computed here. Unchanged coordinate motion cannot imply unchanged dynamics under changed inertia.

The four detached candidate document triplets were generated and root-reviewed before native registration. This admission consumes their exact definition, sparse-attachment and experiment bytes. It does not regenerate documents inside the native operation. Candidate upper-body hashes are recomputed by the canonical owner; old metadata is retained as parent provenance, not copied as new native authority.

Club-local shaft endpoints and grip rotations remain unchanged. The club is driven through the left-wrist universal attachment, with continuous authored right-hand closure.
