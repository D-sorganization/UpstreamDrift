## Counterfactual Meaning and Hard Numerical Boundary

Authority is AffineDrift/NOTATION.md, docs/development/terminology-decision-record.md
and \_includes/mechanics-counterfactual-algorithm.qmd. Older closed epic8458 is not
current semantic authority. Pointwise ZTCF evaluates zero declared control at the
achieved q/v/internal state. A stitched trace is not an integrated trajectory.
Forward/branched ZTCF must be explicitly selected. ZVCF is instantaneous zero
velocity AND zero control at fixed configuration/internal state. A control-retained
zero-velocity diagnostic has its own name. Passive effects remain as declared.

For the rigid loop, solve acceleration and reaction together under each intervention:
M a + h = B u + J^T lambda; J a + Jdot v =0. Recompute the velocity-dependent
constraint bias for ZVCF. Do not use unconstrained ABA/M solves or infer hand
forces from qdd times segment length. Preserve frame and moment-origin metadata,
force allocation/rank limits, and all internal state on success and failure.

Extend existing planar biomechanics/drift_control_transfer.py accounting for3D
spatial wrenches; reuse shared frame transforms. Power is F dot v + M dot omega.
Separate applied torque, generalized force, interface wrench and acceleration.
Native Simscape compute_ztcf/zvcf is deferred; generic Pinocchio uses unconstrained
ABA; legacy Drake selectors can expose torque as acceleration; OpenSim needs
exception-safe full-state restoration and acceleration qualification. Existing
pendulum tests do not qualify these native models. CF-1 through CF-8 in #10286
provide tests, adapter work and capability gates. UI must show unavailable values,
not zeros, when a backend cannot deliver a qualified quantity.

WSCG source authority: docs/research/proximal_distal_energy_transfer/sources/
wscg_2024/SOURCE_REGISTER.md. Reuse the existing two_hand_wrench, analysis/export
scripts, registered tables and tests. Import as a named legacy run. Reproduce its
plots separately from analysis of the new tour-average candidate; preserve DELTA,
reference-point and biological interpretation limits.
