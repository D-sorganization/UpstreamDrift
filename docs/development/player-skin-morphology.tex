\section{Scope and Provenance}
This editable research reference accompanies issues 15814, 15815, 15832 and
15833; it is
separate from the governed engineering design manual. The existing CC0
MakeHuman-derived head and its provenance remain unchanged. Morphs are artistic
controls, not measured player anatomy, fitted video evidence or biometric scans.
They operate only on the shared visual meshes, leaving bodies, joints, marker
attachments, masses, inertias, collision geometry and motion coordinates intact.
The same full-body MuJoCo exporter remains authoritative.
\section{Frames, Units and Equations}
The input vertex $(x,y,z)$ uses the canonical unit-head frame: origin at the
neck plane, $x$ forward, $y$ left and $z$ up. Output coordinates are multiplied
by the existing head length in metres. Heights are unchanged.
Let $u=\mathrm{clip}(z/0.2,0,1)$, $n=u^2(3-2u)$,
$f=\mathrm{clip}((x+0.05)/0.35,0,1)$ and
$G(c,r)=\exp(-((z-c)/r)^2)$. For width $w$, jaw $j$, nose $a$,
mouth $m$ and eye-spacing $e$:
\begin{align*}
y' &= y[1+n(w+jG(0.28,0.16)
       +f(mG(0.276,0.08)+eG(0.532,0.08)))],\\
x' &= x+0.12nafG(0.43,0.12)\exp(-(y/0.10)^2),\\
z' &= z.
\end{align*}
Every parameter must be finite and lie in $[-0.2,0.2]$; missing parameters
are zero. All-zero parameters return an exact copy. Skin, eyes, brows, mouth,
hair and cap receive the identical spatial mapping, preserving attachment.
The neck plane is fixed. Triangle indices are preserved; no remeshing or
physical parameter estimation occurs. Bounds constrain artistic edits but do
not establish anatomical accuracy or certify every possible morph combination.
\section{Source-Attested Legend Candidates}
Imperial priors use exact factors of 0.0254 m per inch and 0.45359237 kg per
pound; conversion precision does not remove source uncertainty. Seve
Ballesteros uses 1.83 m / 80 kg from the Japan Golf Tour profile
\url{https://www.jgto.org/en/player/10161/profile}; its measurement date is
not stated. The PGA Tour Seve profile also lists 195 lb at the same rounded
height, with no measurement date. Jack Nicklaus uses 1.778 m / 83.91458845 kg from the PGA Tour
biography \url{https://www.pgatour.com/player/01869/jack-nicklaus/bio}
(5 ft 10 in / 185 lb), while the Nicklaus Museum
\url{https://www.nicklausmuseum.org/} lists 5 ft 11 in / 190 lb.
Bobby Jones uses a nominal 1.7272 m / 74.84274105 kg from the Encyclopedia.com
entry \url{https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/sports-and-games/sports-biographies/bobby-jones}'s
approximate 5 ft 8 in / 165 lb at the height of his golf career; no primary
measurement record was located. Sam Snead uses 1.8034 m / 86.1825503 kg
from the PGA Tour biography \url{https://www.pgatour.com/player/02119/sam-snead/bio}
(5 ft 11 in / 190 lb), while the World Golf Hall of Fame
\url{https://worldgolfhalloffame.org/members/sam-snead.html} lists 5 ft 10 in
/ 185 lb. Byron Nelson uses 1.8542 m / 79.37866475 kg from the PGA Tour
biography \url{https://www.pgatour.com/player/01863/byron-nelson/bio}
(6 ft 1 in / 175 lb); a retrospective
\url{https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2006-sep-27-me-nelson27-story.html}
describes him at 170 lb in 1945.
The source catalog links reviewed video for each candidate: an R\&A Ballesteros
Open compilation and the 1979 official film, the 1986 Nicklaus Masters clip,
the USGA Jones instructional archive, a Snead swing clip and 1949 newsreel,
and a Nelson frame-by-frame clip plus PGA Tour retrospective. Clothing,
hair and face morphs are source-inspired illustrations, not frame-by-frame
outfit identification, measured anatomy or likeness qualification.
\section{Acceptance and Reproduction}
Schema round-trips, invalid-input rejection, identity, unchanged heights and
neck, closed outward mesh topology and native physics equivalence are tested.
The native gate compares compiled model parameters and forward computations
with and without nonzero morphs. Visual review remains a separate gate:
passing numerical tests does not establish resemblance to a named player.
From the repository root with the pinned Tools dependency initialized:
\begin{verbatim}
PYTHONPATH=.:vendor/ud-tools/src MUJOCO_GL=egl python3 -m pytest \
 tests/unit/model_appearance/test_head_morph.py -q -o addopts=''
\end{verbatim}
\section{Limits and Failed Experiments}
The initial red test failed because the morphology module was absent.
The old generic head controls could change colors and headwear but not face
shape. A shared deformation was selected so accessories follow the same
surface transformation. No historical motion training, physical playback
qualification, player scan, period-specific mass measurement or precise
reconstruction is claimed by this change.
