# DV-13061: Open-FP Force Plate Hardware Qualification for Measured Vertical GRF

Source: [UpstreamDrift#13061](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/13061). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **split**. Kind: physical-measurement.

## Rationale and Expected Value

The open-fp integration plan (`docs/plans/open_fp_integration/`) imports measured
per-foot vertical force and centre of pressure (CoP) from open-fp force plates. At the
reviewed open-fp commit `53d682ce70b34bd2d85918b210ebdc81937e33f8`, only four of eight
load cells read on the bench, the production MCU firmware is unwritten, and neither the
M4 calibration checks nor the M6 real-swing capture checks have been run on hardware.
Imported data is therefore unqualified measurement. Qualifying a device would let the
model-versus-measured comparisons (OF-08) and the contact-wrench research (OF-10) rest
on measured vertical ground reaction instead of display-only data.

## Existing Capabilities and Evidence

- No open-fp reader, client or comparison exists in UpstreamDrift yet; the software is
  planned as OF-01 to OF-09 under epic #13061.
- open-fp's own acceptance targets are in its `docs/PROJECT_INIT.md` section 12, and its
  bench results are in its `docs/VERIFIED.md`. Neither is evidence for any specific device.
- No measurement of any kind was made for this record. This repository has no plates.

## Resources and Prerequisites

- An assembled open-fp plate pair with all eight cells reading and production MCU
  firmware (open-fp M2/M3) — **unavailable**.
- A per-device calibration file from the open-fp M4 wizard — **unavailable**.
- Reference masses and a reference scale for the static checks — **unverified**.
- A golfer and capture session for the M6 swing checks — **unverified**.

## Acceptance

1. A per-device calibration file, and on that device the open-fp M4 checks: total force
   within 1 %, CoP within 10 mm at marked spots, body weight within 1 % of a reference scale.
2. On the same device and production firmware, open-fp M6: 20 real swings captured with
   none missed and no false triggers during 2 minutes of quiet standing, plus M3 200 Hz
   frames with no sequence gaps over 10 minutes.
3. Recorded temperature, auto-zero drift and tare movement over a session, with the
   open-fp commit, firmware and calibration ids used.
4. Only then may OF-08 drop the unqualified label or OF-10 use measured wrenches.
   Bench-only, simulator or synthetic data is never acceptance evidence.
