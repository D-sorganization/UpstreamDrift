# DV-11542: MOSAIC-10 Multi-Swing Acquisition and Registration Across Real Golfer Trials

Source: [UpstreamDrift#11542](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/11542). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **split**. Kind: human-validation.

## Rationale and Expected Value

While synthetic multi-swing trials verify algorithmic monotonicity, piece-wise phase warping, event anchoring, and variance reduction on synthetic fixtures, genuine multi-swing physiological validation requires real human golfers across varied handicap tiers and swing tempos. Real human trials exhibit biomechanical variation, marker occlusion, club-face deflection, and individual neuromuscular variability that cannot be synthetically generated or self-certified by coding agents.
