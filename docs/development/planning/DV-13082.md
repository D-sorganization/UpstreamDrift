# DV-13082: Qualified open-fp Device Record for Measured Vertical Ground Reaction

Source: [Issue #13082](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/13082) (OF-HW, child of epic [#13061](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/13061)). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **defer**. Kind: physical-measurement.

## Rationale

UpstreamDrift can now read open-fp swing files and live frames, place them in the
model's world, read them at model times with declared uncertainty, overlay them on the
ground reaction plots and screen candidate swings against them. None of that makes a
reading accurate. Only a physical device that passes open-fp's M4 static calibration and
M6 real-swing capture checks, with production firmware, can do that. No plates exist for
this repository to measure, so this qualification cannot be done in software and is
parked here. Until it passes, every open-fp trial is **unqualified measurement**: it may
be shown and compared, but it is never acceptance evidence for any model.

## Existing Capabilities and Evidence

The software that consumes a qualification has landed. These items are software
evidence only; none of them is a measurement.

- `PlateProvenance.qualification` in
  `src/shared/python/force_plates/contracts.py` holds the durable reference to a passed
  qualification; `None` means unqualified. The reader never sets it, so every trial
  read today stays unqualified (OF-01 #13065, OF-02 #13066).
- `src/shared/python/force_plates/overlay.py` and
  `src/shared/python/plotting/renderers/ground_reaction.py` publish `measured_qualified`
  and draw the "Unqualified measurement" banner on the PyQt6 tile and the web charts
  (OF-07 #13087).
- `src/shared/python/force_plates/comparison.py` keeps model-versus-measured results
  labelled unqualified (OF-08 #13086).
- `src/shared/python/loop_nullspace/plate_screen.py` reports plate eliminations as
  conditional on the declared, unqualified measurement (OF-12 #13070).
- `docs/research/open_fp_contact_qp/open_fp_contact_qp.tex` keeps measured wrenches out
  of the contact-wrench QP until a device is qualified (OF-10 #13089).
- open-fp's own targets are in its `docs/PROJECT_INIT.md` section 12, and its bench
  results are in its `docs/VERIFIED.md`. Neither is evidence for any specific device.
- No measurement of any kind was made for this record.

## Resources and Prerequisites

- An assembled open-fp plate pair with all eight load cells reading and production MCU
  firmware (open-fp M2/M3) — **unavailable**.
- A per-device calibration file with gains, tares, date and residuals from the open-fp
  M4 wizard — **unavailable**.
- Reference masses and a reference scale for the M4 static checks — **unverified**.
- A golfer and a capture session for the M6 real-swing and false-trigger checks —
  **unverified**.
- A named owner accountable for the device and the session — **unassigned**.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A per-device calibration file, and on that device the open-fp M4 checks pass: total
   force within 1 %, CoP within 10 mm at marked spots, body weight within 1 % of a
   reference scale.
2. On the same device and production firmware, open-fp M6 passes: 20 real swings
   captured with none missed, no false triggers during 2 minutes of quiet standing, and
   M3 200 Hz frames with no sequence gaps over 10 minutes.
3. Temperature, auto-zero drift and tare movement are recorded over a session, together
   with the open-fp commit, firmware and calibration ids used.
4. A qualification reference for that device is recorded in
   `PlateProvenance.qualification` for its trials, and this record is updated through the
   deferred-validation process (publish, verify, close, in that order).

Bench-only, simulator or synthetic data is never acceptance evidence. Only after these
pass may OF-08 drop the unqualified label for that device's trials, OF-10 use measured
vertical wrenches as targets, or OF-12 report eliminations as conditional on a qualified
measurement.
