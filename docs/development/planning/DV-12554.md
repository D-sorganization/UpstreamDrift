# DV-12554: NSX-05 External Validation Protocol for Closed-Loop Null-Space Exploration (Instrumented Grip, Surface EMG, and Held-Out Swings)

Source: [UpstreamDrift#12554](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/12554) (Parent Epic: [UpstreamDrift#12593](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/12593)). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **split**. Kind: physical-measurement.

## Rationale and Expected Value

Motion capture and rigid-body inverse dynamics fix the generalized load $b = M(q)a + h(q, v)$ and the net bare-club wrench $W_{\text{net}}^{\text{bare}}$, but cannot uniquely resolve the six internal grip-split coordinates $s_1 \dots s_6 \in \ker G$ between the lead and trail hands. Testing whether the declared physiological hypothesis sets (`H-TORQUE-CAPACITY-BOX`, `H-GRIP-FRICTION-CONE`, `H-EFFORT-CAPACITY-QUADRATIC`, `H-TORQUE-RATE-SMOOTHNESS`) bracket real human load sharing requires independent physical measurements from a 6-axis dual-section instrumented golf grip, synchronized optical motion capture, dual force plates, and surface electromyography (sEMG) across held-out golfer swings. Fixing this falsification protocol before any experimental data exist prevents post-hoc threshold tuning (Rule R13).

## Measurands and Observation Models

1. **Bare-Club Per-Hand Wrenches From Instrumented Grips (`hand_on_bare_club`):**
   - Dual 6-axis force/torque transducers embedded in split lead-hand and trail-hand grip sleeves measure the contact wrenches $W_L^{\text{meas}}(t) = [F_L^\top, \tau_L^\top]^\top$ and $W_R^{\text{meas}}(t) = [F_R^\top, \tau_R^\top]^\top$ exerted **by each hand on the bare club** in the canonical grip-midpoint frame (`C-FRAME-GRIP`, per NSX-00 and NSX-01).
   - **Interface alignment (NSX-01):** Transducer tare and dynamic inertial compensation must subtract only the bare transducer sleeve mass outboard of the sensing element, corresponding to the `"hand_on_bare_club"` interface ($0.313\,\mathrm{kg}$ bare club in `full_body_spec_anthro_driver.json`, excluding the $1.1082\,\mathrm{kg}$ welded hand solids `LHand`, `LHandStandoff`, and `RHand`).
   - Measured split coordinates are obtained via the exact NSX-00 inverse map:
     $$s_f^{\text{meas}}(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(F_R^{\text{meas}}(t) - F_L^{\text{meas}}(t)\right), \qquad s_m^{\text{meas}}(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\tau_R^{\text{meas}}(t) - \tau_L^{\text{meas}}(t)\right).$$
2. **Surface EMG Onset Timing and Activation Proxy Observation Model:**
   - Bilateral surface EMG channels over wrist flexors/extensors, biceps/triceps, and pectoralis/latissimus dorsi are recorded synchronously ($\ge 1000\,\mathrm{Hz}$).
   - **Explicit Observation Model:** Surface EMG is an electrical neural-drive and muscle-activation proxy, **never** a direct ground-truth measurement of hand contact wrench or net joint torque (because co-contraction increases EMG amplitude without changing net joint torque $\tau_i$).
   - EMG comparisons are therefore restricted to pre-registered **timing and directional activation consistency**: high-pass filtered, rectified, low-pass enveloped (%MVC) signals shifted by a pre-declared electromechanical delay window ($\Delta t_{\text{EMD}} \in [30, 60]\,\mathrm{ms}$) are tested only against sign-determinate (`POSITIVE` or `NEGATIVE`) agonist/antagonist dominance windows certified by NSX-25 affine probes.

## Pre-Registered Predictions on Held-Out Swings

Before unblinding instrumented-grip channels on any held-out swing trial $k \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{test}}$:

1. **Dynamic Balance Gate (Invariant Check):** The sum of measured hand wrenches $W_L^{\text{meas}}(t) + W_R^{\text{meas}}(t)$ (transported to the grip midpoint $r_M$) must agree with the inverse-dynamics bare-club Newton–Euler resultant $W_{\text{net}}^{\text{bare}}(t)$ within the propagated kinematic + sensor noise band throughout the non-impact downswing window $[t_{\text{top}}, t_{\text{impact}} - 10\,\mathrm{ms}]$. Any trial failing net-wrench conservation indicates marker-derivative or sensor-calibration failure and is flagged before split evaluation.
2. **Feasible Envelope Containment (`envelope_only` Preset):** At every admitted non-impact frame $t$, the six measured split coordinates $s_1^{\text{meas}}(t) \dots s_6^{\text{meas}}(t)$ are tested for containment within the static feasible envelope $[s_{j,\min}^{\text{stat}}(t), s_{j,\max}^{\text{stat}}(t)]$ and trajectory-coupled envelope $[s_{j,\min}^{\text{traj}}(t), s_{j,\max}^{\text{traj}}(t)]$ expanded by the $95\%$ propagated sensor + kinematic uncertainty margin.
3. **Certified Sign and Timing Windows (NSX-25 Probes):** For every swing phase window (`takeaway`, `transition`, `mid_downswing`, `pre_impact`) where the NSX-25 affine probe `certify` returns `Certificate.HOLDS` for the sign of axial pull/push $s_1$ or transverse shear-couple $s_2, s_3$, the measured coordinate $s_j^{\text{meas}}(t)$ must share that sign across $\ge 95\%$ of samples in the window.

## Acceptance Criteria and Pre-Registered Analysis Plan

- **Primary Falsification Criterion (Hypothesis Set):** A declared hypothesis preset $\mathcal{H}$ is **falsified** on a held-out trial if more than $5\%$ of admitted pre-impact frames exhibit measured split coordinates $s_j^{\text{meas}}(t)$ outside the uncertainty-inflated feasible envelope $[s_{j,\min}(t) - \delta_{j,95\%}(t), \; s_{j,\max}(t) + \delta_{j,95\%}(t)]$, or if a certified `HOLDS` sign predicate is violated on held-out trials.
- **No Post-Hoc Fitting on Held-Out Swings:** Subject-specific isometric torque scaling or inverse optimal control objective weights (NSX-71, #12589) may only be calibrated on a strictly disjoint training split $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}$ and locked (SHA-256 manifest) prior to evaluating $\mathcal{D}_{\text{test}}$.
- **Synthetic Data Exclusion:** Synthetic or simulation-generated wrench data qualify software contracts only and can never close or satisfy this external validation record.

## Data Provenance and Anonymization Requirements

- **Participant Anonymization:** All human subject identifiers must be replaced at ingest by salted cryptographic pseudonyms (`SHA-256` subject token); raw video faces and identifying metadata remain in the institutional secure store outside Git.
- **Sensor and Calibration Provenance:** Every deposited validation bundle must record transducer serial hashes, factory/bench $6\times 6$ calibration matrix SHA-256, optical camera extrinsics/intrinsics receipts, hardware trigger synchronization drift ($< 0.5\,\mathrm{ms}$), and bare-club mass/CG/MOI bench measurements.
