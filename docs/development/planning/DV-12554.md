# DV-12554: NSX-05 External Validation Protocol for Closed-Loop Null-Space Exploration (Instrumented Grip, Surface EMG, and Held-Out Swings)

Source: [UpstreamDrift#12554](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/12554) (Parent Epic: [UpstreamDrift#12593](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/12593)). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **split**. Kind: physical-measurement.

## Rationale and Expected Value

Motion capture and rigid-body inverse dynamics fix the generalized load $b = M(q)a + h(q, v)$ and the net bare-club wrench $W_{\text{net}}^{\text{bare}}$, but cannot uniquely resolve the six internal grip-split coordinates $s_1 \dots s_6 \in \ker G$ between the lead and trail hands. Testing whether the declared physiological hypothesis sets (`H-TORQUE-CAPACITY-BOX`, `H-GRIP-FRICTION-CONE`, `H-EFFORT-CAPACITY-QUADRATIC`, `H-TORQUE-RATE-SMOOTHNESS`) bracket real human load sharing requires independent physical measurements from a 6-axis dual-section instrumented golf grip, synchronized optical motion capture, dual force plates, and surface electromyography (sEMG) across held-out golfer swings. Fixing this falsification protocol before any experimental data exist prevents post-hoc threshold tuning (Rule R13).

## Measurands and Observation Models

1. **Bare-Club Per-Hand Wrenches From Instrumented Grips (`hand_on_bare_club`):**
   - Dual 6-axis force/torque transducers embedded in split lead-hand and trail-hand grip sleeves measure the contact wrenches $W_L^{\text{meas}}(t) = [F_L^\top, \tau_L^\top]^\top$ and $W_R^{\text{meas}}(t) = [F_R^\top, \tau_R^\top]^\top$ exerted **by each hand on the bare club** in the canonical grip-midpoint frame (`C-FRAME-GRIP`, per NSX-00 and NSX-01).
   - **Interface alignment (NSX-01):** Transducer tare and dynamic inertial compensation must subtract only the bare transducer sleeve mass outboard of the sensing element, corresponding to the `"hand_on_bare_club"` interface ($0.313\,\mathrm{kg}$ bare club in `full_body_spec_anthro_driver.json`, excluding the $1.1082\,\mathrm{kg}$ welded hand solids `LHand`, `LHandStandoff`, and `RHand`).
   - Measured split coordinates are obtained via the exact NSX-00 inverse map:
     $$s_f^{\text{meas}}(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(F_R^{\text{meas}}(t) - F_L^{\text{meas}}(t)\right), \qquad s_m^{\text{meas}}(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\tau_R^{\text{meas}}(t) - \tau_L^{\text{meas}}(t)\right).$$
2. **Surface EMG Onset Timing and Activation Proxy Observation Model:**
   - Bilateral surface EMG channels over wrist flexors/extensors, biceps/triceps, and pectoralis/latissimus dorsi are recorded synchronously ($\ge 1000\,\mathrm{Hz}$).
   - **Explicit Observation Model:** Surface EMG is an electrical neural-drive and muscle-activation proxy, **never** a direct ground-truth measurement of hand contact wrench or net joint torque (because co-contraction increases EMG amplitude without changing net joint torque $\tau_i$).
   - EMG comparisons are therefore restricted to pre-registered **timing and directional activation consistency**: high-pass filtered, rectified, low-pass enveloped (%MVC) signals shifted by a pre-declared electromechanical delay window ($\Delta t_{\text{EMD}} \in [30, 60]\,\mathrm{ms}$) are tested only against sign-determinate (`POSITIVE` or `NEGATIVE`) agonist/antagonist dominance windows certified by NSX-25 affine probes.

## Pre-Registered Predictions on Held-Out Swings

Before unblinding instrumented-grip channels on any held-out swing trial $k \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{test}}$:

1. **Dynamic Balance Gate (Invariant Check):** The sum of measured hand wrenches $W_L^{\text{meas}}(t) + W_R^{\text{meas}}(t)$ (transported to the grip midpoint $r_M$) must agree with the inverse-dynamics bare-club Newton–Euler resultant $W_{\text{net}}^{\text{bare}}(t)$ within the propagated kinematic + sensor noise band throughout the non-impact downswing window $[t_{\text{top}}, t_{\text{impact}} - 10\,\mathrm{ms}]$. Any trial failing net-wrench conservation indicates marker-derivative or sensor-calibration failure and is flagged before split evaluation.
2. **Feasible Envelope Containment (`envelope_only` Preset):** At every admitted non-impact frame $t$, the six measured split coordinates $s_1^{\text{meas}}(t) \dots s_6^{\text{meas}}(t)$ are tested for containment within the static feasible envelope $[s_{j,\min}^{\text{stat}}(t), s_{j,\max}^{\text{stat}}(t)]$ and trajectory-coupled envelope $[s_{j,\min}^{\text{traj}}(t), s_{j,\max}^{\text{traj}}(t)]$ expanded by the $95\%$ propagated sensor + kinematic uncertainty margin.
3. **Certified Sign and Timing Windows (NSX-25 Probes):** For every swing phase window (`takeaway`, `transition`, `mid_downswing`, `pre_impact`) where the NSX-25 affine probe `certify` returns `Certificate.HOLDS` for the sign of axial pull/push $s_1$ or transverse shear-couple $s_2, s_3$, the measured coordinate $s_j^{\text{meas}}(t)$ must share that sign across $\ge 95\%$ of samples in the window.

## Acceptance Criteria and Pre-Registered Analysis Plan

- **Primary Falsification Criterion (Hypothesis Set):** A declared hypothesis preset $\mathcal{H}$ is **falsified** on a held-out trial if more than $5\%$ of admitted pre-impact frames exhibit measured split coordinates $s_j^{\text{meas}}(t)$ outside the uncertainty-inflated feasible envelope $[s_{j,\min}(t) - \delta_{j,95\%}(t), \; s_{j,\max}(t) + \delta_{j,95\%}(t)]$, or if a certified `HOLDS` sign predicate is violated on held-out trials.
- **No Post-Hoc Fitting on Held-Out Swings:** Subject-specific isometric torque scaling or inverse optimal control objective weights (NSX-71, #12589) may only be calibrated on a strictly disjoint training split $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}$ and locked (SHA-256 manifest) prior to evaluating $\mathcal{D}_{\text{test}}$.
- **Synthetic Data Exclusion:** Synthetic or simulation-generated wrench data qualify software contracts only and can never close or satisfy this external validation record.

## Data Provenance and Anonymization Requirements

- **Participant Anonymization:** All human subject identifiers must be replaced at ingest by salted cryptographic pseudonyms (`SHA-256` subject token); raw video faces and identifying metadata remain in the institutional secure store outside Git.
- **Sensor and Calibration Provenance:** Every deposited validation bundle must record transducer serial hashes, factory/bench $6\times 6$ calibration matrix SHA-256, optical camera extrinsics/intrinsics receipts, hardware trigger synchronization drift ($< 0.5\,\mathrm{ms}$), and bare-club mass/CG/MOI bench measurements.

## Variability Sensitivity Experiments

Added by NSX-101 (#12730). These five experiments are preregistered here, before any data exist, so that none of the variability claims in `docs/research/loop_nullspace/publication_gate.md` (section `Variability Sensitivity Claims`, rows `VAR-01` to `VAR-10`) can be unblocked by post-hoc choices. They reuse the apparatus, provenance and anonymisation rules above and the Synthetic Data Exclusion: simulated or fixture data never satisfy any of them. Hypotheses are the registry entries `H-NOISE-ADDITIVE`, `H-NOISE-SIGNAL-DEPENDENT`, `H-GEOMETRIC-PRELOAD`, `H-DECLARED-JOINT-IMPEDANCE`, `H-SENSORIMOTOR-DELAY` and `H-MUSCLE-SHORT-RANGE-STIFFNESS` in `src/config/loop_nullspace_registry.json`.

Numbers that cannot yet be justified are written `TBD` with the reason. They must be fixed, with a rationale, in a dated commit before the first held-out trial is unblinded. No model output exists for a real golfer, because the only `sensitivity_provider` is a pendulum test fixture and no real-engine provider is wired (NSX-99, #12728). A prediction for each experiment is therefore made only after a provider is wired and its inputs are fitted on training sessions alone.

### EXP-V1 Calibrated Face and Launch Measurement

- **Hypothesis:** The model-predicted face-angle standard deviation at impact, and the order of the strategy and disturbance effects on it, lie inside the preregistered prediction interval of the measured values on held-out sessions. Falsified when the measured standard deviation falls outside the interval.
- **Measurement:** Face angle, club path, clubhead speed and launch direction at impact from an independently calibrated instrument (calibrated launch monitor or calibrated high-speed imaging), with its own uncertainty budget and calibration receipt. Swings are synchronised to the motion capture trigger. Impact semantics follow `DV-10382`, so an unobservable face rotation is reported unavailable, never filled in.
- **Sample and Power:** Number of golfers TBD (cohort not recruited). Swings per golfer TBD: the relative error of a sample standard deviation falls roughly as 1 over the square root of twice the swings less one, so the count must be set from the smallest effect worth detecting, which is TBD because no real effect size exists. Sessions are split into training and held-out by session, never by swing.
- **Pass and Fail:** Pass requires the measured value inside the interval for the primary outcome (face angle) on held-out sessions, with the instrument uncertainty propagated. Interval width TBD (set from the instrument budget). Fail on any held-out miss of the primary outcome; secondary outcomes cannot rescue a primary fail.
- **Unblocks:** `VAR-01`, `VAR-02` (together with EXP-V3), `VAR-07` and, with EXP-V5, `VAR-10`. It does not unblock any coaching claim.

### EXP-V2 Instrumented Grip

- **Hypothesis:** On held-out swings the measured left and right hand wrenches follow the declared minimum-variance allocation more closely than a flat (objective-invariant) allocation does. Falsified when the flat allocation matches at least as well.
- **Measurement:** The dual 6-axis grip of the main protocol above, same frame and interface (`hand_on_bare_club`), after the dynamic balance gate passes. The comparison is on the six split coordinates and the geometric preload channel, with the noise model fitted on training sessions only.
- **Sample and Power:** Golfers and swings TBD (no instrumented grip exists). Within-golfer swing counts must also support a noise fit, so the minimum is TBD until the fit's identifiability is assessed on real data.
- **Pass and Fail:** Pass requires the predicted allocation to beat the flat comparator on the preregistered distance by a margin TBD (set from sensor and kinematic noise), on held-out sessions, with the dynamic balance gate passing. Fail otherwise. A fail does not show that golfers use a different strategy, only that this model class does not describe the data.
- **Unblocks:** `VAR-03`, and with EXP-V5 `VAR-04`.

### EXP-V3 Perturbation-Derived Impedance

- **Hypothesis:** The joint impedance and sensorimotor delay measured from small controlled perturbations are consistent with the declared impedance and delay used by the model (`H-DECLARED-JOINT-IMPEDANCE`, `H-SENSORIMOTOR-DELAY`), and substituting the measured values reproduces the measured dispersion change within EXP-V1's interval.
- **Measurement:** Joint stiffness and damping from the response to small, repeatable perturbations applied at preregistered phases, and the latency of the corrective response. Whether an in-swing perturbation is feasible and safe in a downswing of roughly a quarter second is unknown (TBD); a static or pre-swing posture proxy may be the only option and must then be labelled as a proxy.
- **Sample and Power:** Golfers, perturbation count and phases TBD (no protocol or rig). Power for impedance estimates is TBD pending a pilot that reports the estimate variance.
- **Pass and Fail:** Pass requires the measured impedance and delay inside the declared prior bounds and the dispersion reproduction criterion above. Bounds TBD (no verified measured range for this task). Fail when either is outside. Human-subject safety review applies before any perturbation.
- **Unblocks:** `VAR-02` (with EXP-V1), `VAR-05` (with EXP-V4 and EXP-V5) and `VAR-06`.

### EXP-V4 EMG Co-Contraction Ratios

- **Hypothesis:** The antagonist to agonist activation ratio ordering across preregistered conditions matches the ordering of the declared co-contraction levels, using EMG only as an activation proxy.
- **Measurement:** Bilateral surface EMG under the main protocol's observation model (at least 1000 Hz, %MVC envelope, electromechanical delay window of 30 to 60 ms). Ratios are compared across conditions in the same golfer and session. They are never converted to joint stiffness or muscle force, because amplitude is not impedance and EMG is not a wrench measurement.
- **Sample and Power:** Golfers, repetitions and MVC protocol TBD (no EMG rig or cohort). Crosstalk and electrode placement repeatability are unknown and must be measured in a pilot.
- **Pass and Fail:** Pass requires the preregistered condition ordering reproduced in a preregistered fraction of golfers; the fraction and the minimum ratio difference are TBD (set from the pilot repeatability). Fail otherwise. A pass supports only the ordering of activation, not any stiffness value.
- **Unblocks:** `VAR-05`, only together with EXP-V3 and EXP-V5.

### EXP-V5 Randomized Strategy Interventions

- **Hypothesis:** A strategy the model ranks as reducing face-angle variance produces a lower measured face-angle standard deviation than the control condition, and the measured order of strategies reproduces the model's order. Falsified when the interval for the difference includes zero or reverses the predicted order.
- **Measurement:** Face-angle standard deviation at impact from the EXP-V1 instrument under randomized, counterbalanced strategy instructions (for example, a changed grip preload, a changed effort setting, a speed-matched condition), with clubhead speed recorded as a covariate. Analysts are blind to condition until the analysis plan is locked.
- **Sample and Power:** Golfers, swings per condition and washout length TBD. Order, learning and fatigue effects are unknown; the minimum effect of interest is TBD because no real effect size exists. Multiplicity across strategies must be controlled; the method is fixed in the locked plan.
- **Pass and Fail:** Pass requires the preregistered primary contrast to exclude zero in the predicted direction and the predicted order to hold, replicated in a second cohort. Both thresholds TBD. Fail on any reversal. A pass would support a ranking for the tested population and setting, not a prescription for an individual golfer.
- **Unblocks:** `VAR-04`, `VAR-05`, `VAR-08` and `VAR-10`, each only together with the experiments the gate rows name.
