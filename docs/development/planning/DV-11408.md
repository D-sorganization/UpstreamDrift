# DV-11408: OpenCap: Golf Accuracy Qualification Against a Marker Reference

Source: [UpstreamDrift#11408](https://github.com/D-sorganization/UpstreamDrift/issues/11408). Status: **deferred**. Board: **pending**.
Disposition: **split**. Kind: physical-measurement.

## Rationale and Expected Value

OpenCap was validated in literature on gait, squats and jumps, rather than on a high-speed (~0.25 s downswing) golf swing at 60 fps with significant motion blur and rapid wrist uncocking. Rigorous qualification requires simultaneous optical marker-based mocap and multi-camera video of identical swings to report empirical joint-angle error across swing phases. Synthetic or unmeasured approximations cannot serve as scientific acceptance.

- Measured joint-angle error report by swing phase (backswing, downswing, impact) from real simultaneous capture, with wrist and forearm angles broken out.
- Never report physical accuracy claims or synthetic values as empirical acceptance.

snapshot. Predeclare methods, tolerances, exclusions, data custody, and
