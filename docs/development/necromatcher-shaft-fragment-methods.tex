and let $p_a=\pi(x_a)$ and $p_b=\pi(x_b)$ be saved-camera image projections.
For $\delta=p_b-p_a$, $L=\|\delta\|$, define
\[
 t=\delta/L,\qquad n=(-t_y,t_x)^\mathsf T,\qquad
 d_i(q)=n(q)^\mathsf T\bigl(u_i-p_a(q)\bigr),\quad i=1,2.

\section*{3. Analytic Chain and Independent Central Steps}
The native endpoint Jacobian and saved camera projection Jacobian give
$\partial p/\partial q=(\partial\pi/\partial x)(\partial x/\partial q)$.
Differentiating the normalized image line gives
\[
 \frac{\partial d_i}{\partial q_j}
 =\left(\frac{\partial n}{\partial q_j}\right)^\mathsf T(u_i-p_a)
 -n^\mathsf T\frac{\partial p_a}{\partial q_j}.
\]
The diagnostic compares this analytic two-entry column with a separately
evaluated central difference of the same raw observable:
\[
 D_{j,h}=\frac{d(q+h e_j)-d(q-h e_j)}{2h},\qquad
 E_{j,h}=\max_{i=1,2}|D_{j,h,i}-J_{ij}|.
\]
Selected coordinate units are authenticated as radians. Therefore both columns
and their errors are measured in pixels per radian. Central perturbations must
remain within authored bounds; an unsupported perturbation is unavailable rather
than clamped, extrapolated or replaced with zero. Degenerate projected axes and
explicit abstentions retain their distinct null/reason contracts.
