\chapterpage{Authored Native Geometry and Closure Audit}
The execution used the four immutable Tiger V16 and Hogan V13 control/variant
fits. Every saved source pose was checked: 210 poses per Tiger fit and 750 per
Hogan fit. The independent verification bound 14,738 tracked source files and
318 unchanged library files. Full output includes camera matrices, all native
coordinates/units, source identities and free/locked values.

Public native body poses return rotation then translation, $(R_b,t_b)$.
For a local marker $\ell$ and authored attachment transform $A$,
\[
x_w=R_b\ell+t_b,\qquad T_w=\begin{bmatrix}R_b&t_b\\0&1\end{bmatrix}A.
\]
Compare marker FK against these placed body frames without refitting an
attachment or changing local offsets. The maximum observed marker difference
was $8.89\times10^{-16}\,\mathrm{m}$.

For right-hand/club attachment frames, compare the signed world separation
vector against the native plant's metre vector. Compare translation and
principal rotation norms separately against public IK closure diagnostics.
Never combine metres and radians into an unexplained norm. The left wrist is a
joint; its relative rotation is not a weld requirement.

\chapterpage{Joint Sensitivity and Human Motion Constraints}
Compute canonical central differences at native-coordinate steps $10^{-6}$
and $2\times10^{-6}$. Retain both Jacobians and their difference; this is a
numerical local check, not analytic proof or a globally identified motion.
\[
J_j\simeq\frac{f(q+h e_j)-f(q-h e_j)}{2h}.
\]
Partition rows into five authored club references and thirteen body landmarks.
Report world sensitivity in m/native-unit and image sensitivity in px/native-unit.
Native coordinates comprise 3 translations and 41 rotations; do not merge
metres and radians or interpret a total Jacobian norm as a clinical quantity.

Both \code{LFInput} and \code{RFInput} were locked at zero radians at every
saved pose. At first/middle/last samples, left-forearm club sensitivity ranged
1.026--1.696 m/rad and 83.970--480.758 px/rad; its body sensitivity was much
smaller. Right-forearm direct club sensitivity was zero in this authored tree,
while body sensitivity remained nonzero. Closure influence requires its own
signed-translation and rotation diagnostics. Zero landmark derivative does not
prove absence of a mechanically relevant joint effect.

The current definitions impose 32 finite authored ranges and leave 12 native
coordinates unbounded. Forearm limits are $\pm\pi/2$ radians. These are model
hypotheses, not measured subject ROM or a coupled clinical constraint system.
Review axes, sign, attachment placements and rotation sequence before mapping
native coordinates onto anatomical flexion, pronation or axial rotation.

The next controlled comparison keeps the original camera, generic body,
training/evaluation roles and budget fixed. It tests 31 versus 33 free
coordinates rather than simultaneously changing camera, morphology and shutter.
Range, ground, grip and phase checks remain separate from image residuals;
finite sampled passes do not certify complete continuous dynamics.
\chapterpage{Preserving Motion While Expanding Coordinates}
Each player's current variant final trajectory supplies both experimental
starts. The locked control restarts its original preserved spline. The expanded
arm adds only the formerly locked left/right forearms, constant at zero with
zero knot velocities. No authored range projection, clipping or resampling is
allowed in this preparation.

For a Hermite interval with normalized clock $u$, positions $q_0,q_1$ and
velocities $v_0,v_1$,
\[
q(u)=h_{00}(u)q_0+h_{10}(u)\Delta t\,v_0+h_{01}(u)q_1+h_{11}(u)\Delta t\,v_1.
\]
Copying old position/velocity coefficient columns preserves their polynomial
motion. Added columns $q_0=q_1=0$, $v_0=v_1=0$ have zero position, velocity and
acceleration. Decision-size changes may alter floating-point evaluation;
verify all 44 native-coordinate $q,v,a$ at every source time and interval
midpoint with absolute tolerance $10^{-12}$ per coordinate/unit. Here $v,a$
are derivatives with respect to container PTS seconds; preserving them does
not identify physical velocities or accelerations.

Use shared \code{expand_image_spline_coordinates}, a freshly evaluated strict
native initializer and public \code{build_native_fit_payload}. Register a
separate unoptimized seed with \code{coordinate_expansion_only}; it cannot
claim convergence or reuse fitted shaft metrics. Then submit both arms through
\code{NativeRefitSession}. No second scheduler or optimizer is introduced.

Both arms use the same V1 shaft objective, body observations, first-pose prior,
44 coordinate scales, knot clock/count, configuration and 30-evaluation/
300-second budget. The position prior already has 44 native rows per body-training time;
the two forearm rows become variable. The speed prior adds exactly two
free-coordinate rows per training time, with the same coefficient, duration
and scales and zero added initial cost. No unchanged total prior-mass claim
is made. V2 observations have already been inspected; later
adaptive trials cannot describe them as unseen validation. Compare raw body,
shaft and closure errors, rejection/convergence status and all source/interior
constraints independently before accepting any improvement.
\chapterpage{Three-Dimensional Model Proxy Rendering}
The saved plain MJCF currently contains no authored body visual solids.
Rendering therefore reuses the shared visual-skeleton provider's capsules,
ellipsoids and boxes, derived from exact saved geometry and visual hints/defaults.
Mass-derived radii are model assumptions, not measured player circumferences.
Cache shared local meshes once per export and apply the public native body FK.

Project through the saved camera, $x_c=R_c x_w+t_c$ and
$p=\pi(Kx_c)$. Clip triangles against $z_c=10^{-6}$ m before projection.
Use canonical projection rather than a second camera implementation.
At integer pixel centres with projected barycentric coordinates $\lambda_k$,
perspective-correct surface depth is
\[
z(p)=\left(\sum_{k=1}^{3}\lambda_k(p)/z_k\right)^{-1}.
\]
Retain the nearest model depth; deterministic geometry ordering resolves ties.
This establishes model-to-model occlusion only. The original scene's foreground,
clothing and hidden body surfaces are not segmented or calibrated.

Public \code{surface_layer} exposes sealed BGR pixels, Boolean coverage mask,
positive finite covered depths, infinite uncovered depths and exact string
geometry identities. Save Unicode IDs so the NPZ can be loaded with
\code{allow_pickle=False}. The inspection layer is the compositing layer,
not a separate approximate renderer. Verify silhouette, depth/ID invariants,
canonical layer equality and separately decoded saved-camera projection equations.
These checks establish implementation consistency, not measured depth.
\chapterpage{Transparency, Skeleton Retention and Export Verification}
For source pixels $S$, rendered surface $M$, coverage $m$ and opacity
$\alpha\in[0,1]$,
\[
C(p)=\begin{cases}\operatorname{round}((1-\alpha)S(p)+\alpha M(p)),&m(p)=1,\\S(p),&m(p)=0.\end{cases}
\]
Blend once with no black-background contribution. Validate typed finite opacity
and BGR source dimensions before FK. Surface-only opacity zero equals original
pixels and performs no FK; final video opacity zero also retains the existing
skeleton/observations/captions, so it is not a claim that the final annotated
frame is the raw photograph. Disabled shape exports preserve legacy pixel and
manifest behavior.

The pilot protocol inspects Tiger 0/150/209 and Hogan 200/375/550 at opacity
0, 0.35 and 1. The full canonical jobs select Tiger 0/105/209 and Hogan
0/375/749 stills and retain all 210/750 source frames. Keep those cohorts distinct.
Maximum measured opacity-0.35 pilot time times source count gates the unchanged
600-second worker budget; this is a heuristic, not a full-video timing certificate.

Full-export authorization binds the exact pilot producer, fit ID/raw hash,
selected indices, source count and opacity. Journal owned run IDs immediately;
verify package ZIP members/CRC/size/SHA, all 960 decoded frames, dimensions,
rational source identities and selected PNGs independently. Lossy MP4 pixels
need not equal original PNGs or the exact unencoded compositor output.
Shape manifests bind native definition/XML/visual-description hashes, opacity,
tessellation and occlusion limitations. Native/web controls share the typed
options, canonical queue/worker and guarded publication/download boundary.

\chapterpage{Camera, Morphology and Shutter Qualification Procedure}
Both current native definitions use generic stature 1.71 m, mass 78 kg,
arm-length factor 1.1 and trunk factor 1.15. They are not measurements of
Tiger Woods or Ben Hogan. Saved camera centres/FOV are conditional on this
model and assumed intrinsics; they do not establish historical camera height,
lens calibration or target-line angle. Single-view image agreement leaves
camera/scale/body-shape ambiguity.

Order experiments: authored geometry and joint constraints; controlled forearm
freedom; individually versioned body/camera hypotheses; then distortion/time
alternatives. Reuse shared anthropometric-candidate/segment-scaling and camera
initialization providers. Population proportions remain population priors.
Do not substitute a later listed body weight for capture-date mass.

Retain zero-readout as a baseline. A row-time hypothesis has
$t(y)=t_0+\tau\rho(y)$ and evaluates moving geometry and camera pose at that time.
Readout direction, sensor-row mapping, cropping/resizing, field conversion and
playback retiming may be unidentifiable. Exposure integration is separate from
row timing, and shaft flexure is separate from a rigid authored line. Neither
progressive encoded frames nor a curved shaft alone identifies original shutter.
The separate nine-page source audit documents all 960 accepted decoded frames
and the corrected Hogan-window screening history.

Declare parameter bounds, priors, uncertainty, gauge and stopping rules before
comparison. Preserve original footage and version every transformed derivative.
An attractive overlay, lower training error or a finite geometric pass cannot
by itself qualify historical anatomy, motion, timing, impact or torque profiles.
