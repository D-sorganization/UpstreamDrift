# Simscape Axis Conventions: Validation Against GolfSwing3D_Kinetic

Issue #9714. Generated by
`python3 -m src.motion_capture.reconstruct.model.simscape --datasets <Dataset Generator> --out simscape_axes.json`
on the 18 dataset-generator trials that ship with the MATLAB model
(`src/engines/Simscape_Multibody_Models/3D_Golf_Model/matlab/Scripts/Dataset Generator`,
620 logged frames). Machine-readable result: `simscape_axes.json` beside
this file. Encoded in `golfer.py` as `golfer-scapula/2.0`.

## Method

Each logged body carries a sensor rotation `R_body` (world from body) and a
global position. For a joint between bodies P and C with logged angles
`a_1..a_k`, the relative rotation must satisfy

    R_Pᵀ R_C = A · R_ax1(s_1 a_1) · ... · R_axk(s_k a_k) · B

with constant frames A and B and unknown axis labels and signs. The search
tries every axis order and sign pattern (and, for composite sets, every
column order), solves A and B by alternating Procrustes from 25 seeds, and
keeps the hypothesis with the smallest mean residual angle. The child origin
expressed in the parent frame gives the segment offset; its spread over
frames shows whether the child origin is rigid in the parent body.

## What the Logs Prove About the Chain

- **Trunk order.** The relative rotation between the two trunk sensors is
  the spine universal, so the `TorsoLogs` sensor is the lower trunk and the
  `SpineLogs` sensor the upper trunk. The torso revolute sits between the
  hip body and the lower trunk, about the trunk axis (it shares the hip's
  third primitive axis). The MATLAB guide's prose lists these two joints the
  other way round; the built model does not.
- **Hub.** The hub is a rigid point of the upper trunk at
  (0, -0.0508, -0.2438) m in the `SpineLogs` frame, 0.249 m from the spine
  universal, which itself is 0.061 m above the `TorsoLogs` origin; the
  scapula joints are centred there and both scapula origins coincide with
  it.
- **Strut and arm.** The glenohumeral centre is 0.254 m from the hub along
  the scapula frame's ∓z (left/right). The upper arm runs 0.3047 m along
  the shoulder frame's +x. The forearm sensor (`LFLogs`) sits 0.1778 m
  (7 in) beyond the elbow along its own +z, which is why its origin is not
  rigid in the upper-arm frame; the elbow lies exactly on that axis and the
  hand point 0.178 m further.
- **Wrists.** No wrist angles are logged; the wrist universal is named only.

## Replay Check

`test_logged_simscape_angles_replay_to_the_logged_sensor_frames` drives the
Python golfer with the logged angles. Over three trials every body frame
matches its sensor to ≤ 1.7e-3 rad (mean ≈ 1e-4 rad, orthonormalised
metric) and the spine, shoulder, elbow and hub positions match the logged
positions to ≤ 0.6 mm.

## World Assumption

The Simscape world is taken as z up, x toward the target, y to the golfer's
right; ours is x toward the target, y up, z to the golfer's right. Only the
root's hip angles and translation depend on this; the joint conventions
above are relative and do not.
